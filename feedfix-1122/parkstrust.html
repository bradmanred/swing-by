Play Areas

The Parks Trust cares for over 30 play areas across Milton Keynes' parks

Find parks with play areas

The Parks Trust care for over 40 parks in Milton Keynes so you’re never more than half a mile from a park. Many of these parks also have play areas, we look after many free to use play areas across the city.

Having these facilities right on your doorstep is hugely beneficial as play areas provide children with the opportunity to develop their physical strength, social interactions and build their confidence.
