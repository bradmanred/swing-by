Parks - Bentilee Park

Address
Ubberley Road,
Stoke-on-Trent, ST2 0HA
Children's play equipment
Yes
Facilities available

Our neighbourhood parks are generally around 20 minutes walk from residential areas. 

They have good play facilities for teenagers and children and include a multi-use games area. 

They do not have toilets or parking. 

Are dogs allowed?
Dogs not allowed in children's play area
