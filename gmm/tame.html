Playgrounds in Tameside

There are lots of playgrounds in Tameside, from larger playgrounds for kids of all ages in our bigger parks to local sites where there are a few items such as a slide and a springy for smaller children. On this page you will find out more about what play equipment is available at each site so you can decide if this playground is suitable for the children that you care for. Please keep checking back for updates as eventually we want to provide video guides to some of our play areas which demonstrate the experiences of children with different needs when visiting playgrounds so you can see how accessible they are. All disabilities are different and children have different needs but we hope this guide helps you make an informed choice.
