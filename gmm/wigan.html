Children's play areas

Keeping the kids entertained with sports and play helps them to make friends and keep themselves fit and healthy. 

There are over 100 parks in the borough with equipment they can use and areas to play in, as well as free events and activities that run throughout the year. 

  • Find out more about quality sports facilities
  • See our range of leisure activities for young people.

How do I report damaged or dangerous play equipment?

You can report damaged or dangerous play equipment as a Wigan Borough resident through MyAccount or as a non-resident through our online form.

  • Report damaged or dangerous play equipment as a Wigan Borough resident through MyAccount
  • Report damaged or dangerous play (unregistered user).
