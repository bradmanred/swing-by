Parks and gardens

With over 35 urban parks and formal gardens to visit, Wigan Borough has a lot to offer, from leisurely walks and children's events, to family fun days for your enjoyment.

There are a range of quality sports facilities and adventure playgrounds in each of the borough parks with free events and activities running throughout the year.

  • See Be Well for a variety of outdoor activities.

Local parks include the recently restored Mesnes Park which now boasts a beautifully refurbished café pavilion, brand new play area and restored bandstand.

We also have a number of Green Flag parks, which recognise and reward the best green spaces in the country, including Alexandra Park, Jubilee Park and Mesnes Park.
