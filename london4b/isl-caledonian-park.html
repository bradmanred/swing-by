Caledonian Park

Caledonian Park is a popular site full of history.

The centrepiece of the park is a 155ft (47.5m) high grade II* listed Clock Tower built in 1853.The Heritage Lottery Fund confirmed a grant award of £1,873,300 in December 2016 which has funded our vision to revitalise Caledonian Park and has secured the future of its clock tower.

Get involved

Have your say about a mural planned for the brick wall next to the PlayZone.

Park features

  • grade II listed Clock Tower  
  • grade II listed Metropolitan Cattle Market railings 
  • café
  • toilets
  • semi-mature woodland areas 
  • community orchard 
  • short and long grass meadows 
  • community gardens 
  • site of Importance for Nature Conservation, Borough Grade 1.
  • community amphitheatre
  • wildlife pond

The park has a Friends Group called Friends of Caledonian Park.

Sports facilities

  • children’s play area (age 3 – 12 years) 
  • large grass open space suitable for ball games and other sports activities
  • PlayZone to play football, basketball, netball and cricket. From June 2026 programmed activities are on offer
  • two table tennis tables
  • free outdoor gym facility

You cannot pre-book the sports facilities at this site as they are free-play only.
