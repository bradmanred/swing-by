Elthorne Park | Islington council

Elthorne Park’s sports and fitness facilities include seven-a-side football pitches, a basketball court and gym equipment.

Park features

  • Park keeper
  • Children's play areas with accessible toilet
  • Walled Phillip Noel Baker Peace Garden
  • Conservation area with an avenue of silver birch trees, native hedgerow and a perennial meadow

Sports facilities

  • Seven-a-side or two smaller floodlit Astroturf pitches with football goals
  • Floodlit basketball court with a set of hoops
  • Floodlit outdoor gym equipment

You cannot book the pitches as they are free play.

Address

Hazellville Road, N19

Size

2.45 hectares or 6.06 acres

Getting there

Buses: 91, 210 to Hornsey Rise

Tube: Archway Station

Train: Crouch End Station

Accessibility

Use Interactive Maps to identify nearest disabled parking bays and distance from public transport routes. A Disabled Access Guide is available at Disabled Go.
