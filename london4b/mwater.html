Paddling pools and splash pads

There are six paddling pools and two ‘splash pad’ water play areas in Merton's parks. All are free to use and are supervised and maintained throughout the opening period.

Dos and don’ts

We want you to be able to enjoy these facilities in a safe and responsible manner, and avoid closures for unplanned maintenance, so we kindly request the following:

  • Children are to be supervised at all times
  • Suitable swimming clothing is to be worn at all times for hygiene - Swimming nappies, swimming trunks or a swimming costume is required
  • No food or drink is to be brought into the facility
  • No sand is to be brought from the sandpit into the facility to keep the filters clear
  • Please be respectful to other users and staff on site
