Local parks and open spaces

Kensington Memorial Park

Kensington Memorial Park has gardens to visit or use a popular waterplay area. A kiosk is open in the park in the summer months.

The park is open from 7.30am until dusk. Closing times are displayed at all entrances. Some gates may close up to 20 minutes ahead of the advertised closing time.

  • St Mark’s Road, W10
  • What3words: crowned.hotel.tides (St. Mark’s Road entrance)

Park rules

Our park rules include:

  • barbecues are not permitted in the park
  • events and professional photography need permission
  • no cycling in the park
  • dogs are not permitted in the playground

Find further information about our Park rules.  

Facilities

  • Public toilets; including disabled toilet and Changing Places
  • Children’s playground
  • Sports facilities
  • Water play facility in the summer months
  • Outdoor gym facility
  • Kiosk is open during the summer months selling hot and cold drinks and snacks.

Waterplay area

The waterplay opens for two-hour sessions to allow for additional cleaning and system checks throughout the day.

Opening times

All week except Tuesdays:

  • session 1: 10.30am to 12.30pm (Sunday - this session is reserved for SEND children and their families)

  • session 2: 1pm to 3pm

  • session 3: 3.30pm to 5.30pm

Tuesdays:

  • session 1: 12pm to 1.30pm (This session is reserved for SEND children and their families)
  • session 2: 2pm to 3.30pm
  • session 3: 4pm to 5.30pm

Play sports

Book to play sports at Kensington Memorial Park. To book or for more information telephone 020 7602 2226 or email K&[email protected].

History

Kensington Memorial Park has been a public park since 1926 when it was opened to the public by Princess Louise, Duchess of Argyll.

The land was bought in 1923 with funds provided by the Kensington War Memorial Committee to create a suitable tribute to those who had given their lives in World War 1.
