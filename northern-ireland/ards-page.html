      Update re: Jonny the Jig Playpark, Holywood (16 November 2026)

      We know how much the Johnny the Jig Play Area means to local families and appreciate that the continued closure is disappointing.

      Following a recent meeting between Council, the South Eastern Health and Social Care Trust and the contractor, we can provide an update on the progress of the essential remedial works to the boundary wall.

      Unfortunately, some unforeseen issues have been identified during the works, which has resulted in delays to the original programme.

      The contractor has confirmed that the remaining works will be completed by the end of the month. 

      To ensure the safety of all park users, the decision has been taken to keep the play area closed until the works are fully completed and the area can be safely reopened.

      We appreciate this is not the update families were hoping for, but safety remains our priority. Thank you for your continued patience and understanding while these essential works are brought to completion. 

      Ards and North Down Borough Council has recently agreed a Play Strategy for the Borough. Outdoor Recreation NI (ORNI) was commissioned to develop a Play Strategy for the period 2021-2032.  A new Borough-wide Play Strategy sets out recommendations for the next 11 years and a detailed Action Plan outlining the priorities for the next 5 years which will be subject to a review at the end of this period. 

      The Play Strategy Document (PDF) can be downloaded here Play Strategy 2021-32 (PDF, 7 MB) (opens new window)
