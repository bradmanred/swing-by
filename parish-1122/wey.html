    Weymouth Play Areas

    We manage 18 play areas that provide a range of fun, innovative and exciting equipment for all age groups.

    Lodmoor Country Park and Louviers Road Play Area include green gym equipment suitable for adults. There is a free to use skate park at Louviers Road with a quarter pipe, middle ramp, grind box, grind rail and half pipe. There are roundabouts and a boat at Melcombe and Wyke which have been revamped. Where possible, we aim to install a range of inclusive equipment with a wide variety of play functions.

    £70,000 Play Area Investment

    We invested £70,000 in upgrading seven play parks across Weymouth, bringing new and exciting equipment for children to enjoy in these community spaces. We made enhancements to the following play areas at Pottery Lane, Melcombe Regis, Lodmoor Country Park, Telford Close, Miles Gardens, Westmacott Road and Honeysuckle Close. To read more click here.

    Report a Problem

    To report a serious defect that may cause injury, please contact our Greenspaces Team on parks@weymouthtowncouncil.gov.uk or 01305 239839.

    Health and Safety

    • All our equipment and safety surfacing products are purchased from the Association of Play Industries who are professional play equipment providers
    • All our play areas, wherever possible, meet BS-EN 1176, the standard for playground equipment and BS-EN 1177, the standard for impact absorbing playground surfacing
    • All our play areas are visually inspected weekly for any obvious damage
    • All our play areas are inspected monthly for mechanical defects and any necessary maintenance is carried out on this visit
    • Once a year our play areas are independently inspected by an outside agency

    Accessibility

    Please let us know if you have any suggestions on how we could improve any of our open spaces or play area facilities for you.
