Broughton & Milton Keynes Parish Council  

Broughton & Milton Keynes Parish Council proudly serves the historic Milton Keynes Village—the namesake of our vibrant city—alongside the neighbouring communities of Atterbury, Broughton, Broughton Gate, Brooklands, Middleton, and Oakgrove. Not forgetting Broughton Village, with its unique character and heritage, remains an integral part of our ever-evolving parish. These neighbourhoods reflect the rich diversity and ongoing growth of our thriving community. 
 
Our office is based at Broughton Pavilion, 139 Tanfield Lane, Broughton, MK10 9NJ 
 
Our Parish Council office opening times at Broughton Pavilion are: 
Monday - Closed 
Tuesday - 9am till 4pm 
Wednesday - 9am till 4pm 
Thursday - 9am till 4pm 
Friday - Closed 
 
The operational team's working hours are 8.30am till 4.30pm Monday to Friday - please call 01908 087499 to speak to a staff member during this time.

A parish council operates under rules set by the Government. The council is made up of 12 Councillors who meet regularly to discuss and agree on plans to better the Community. Also a team of council staff support the Councillors and Community by putting these plans into place and attending to many environment and operational tasks to make your Parish a place to be proud of. 
 
With our website we aim to provide information about the Parish, details on Council Meetings, Council Finances, Community Events and much more. 
 
If there is anything you think is missing from our website and would like adding then we want to hear from you, it is for the benefit of our residents so we encourage feedback.

Middleton Sports Pavilion and Playing Fields residents’ survey 
 
Broughton and Milton Keynes Parish Council is inviting residents to share their views on Middleton Sports Pavilion and Playing Fields as the current lease comes to an end in 2027. 
 
The survey asks the same three questions as the recent public meeting. We’d like to hear what you like about the current set-up, what you feel could be improved and what you’d like to see in the future. You’re welcome to take part whether or not you attended the meeting or currently use the facilities. 
 
The Parish Council’s Middleton Sports Pavilion Working Group will consider your feedback as part of its discussions about the future of the site. 
 
The online survey closes at 11.45pm on Saturday 12 December 2026. Paper copies are available from the Parish Council office at Broughton Pavilion.

FoodBank MK 

We now run a serving centre from Broughton Pavilion, twice a week on Tuesdays & Thursdays - 10am till 4pm.  
 
Please remember to bring your ID as we cannot issue Food Parcels without this. 
 
If you need help, please call the MK Food Bank helpline on 0300 303 4933 or text HELP to 07874 964505 to discuss your situation in confidence with experienced staff. 
 
Alternatively you can visit their website mkfoodbank.org.uk 
 
Donating Food Made Easy! 
 
If you're interested in giving back to the community, you can drop off a food donation at Broughton Pavilion. Simply come by on Tuesday through Thursday between 9am and 4pm, and leave your contribution in the green box at reception. 
 
Mobile Top Up Shop! - We are very pleased to announce we now have the Mobile Top Up Shop coming to the car park at Broughton Paviilon EVERY Wednesday. 
 
This service is available to anyone in financial hardship and struggling, you can get 10 items for £2 (individuals) or 20 items for £4 (families) - cash only please.
