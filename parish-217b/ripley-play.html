Play Areas 

Ripley Town Council own and maintain two play areas in the Ripley Township. 
Cedar Avenue Play Area 
Ripley 
Slide, round-about, see-saw, and 
seating. 
 
 
Porterhouse Road Play Area 
Ripley 
Play tower with slide, bridge, balancing posts, seating, football and basketball areas. 
 

We take pride in our play areas 

Both our play areas are inspected weekly for any health and safety issues and they are also litter picked. 
 
If you notice that there is a problem with the equipment or seating at either Cedar Avenue or Porterhouse Road play area, please let us know.
