Welcome to Yeovil Town Council

Yeovil Town Council is a local government body with the best interests of the town at heart.  

The Council is made up of 24 locally elected Councillors, some of whom also serve on the unitary authority, Somerset Council.  Regular meetings are held to make decisions and respond to local need.  Work is often carried out in collaboration with Somerset Council, particularly for larger projects and services.
