Stirling Council’s Open Data website provides public access to datasets, maps, applications, reports and other information published by the Council.

The site brings together information from across Council services, covering subjects such as population and local area profiles, planning and conservation, schools and education, finance and licensing, housing and assets, roads and transport, environmental health, climate and nature, tourism and culture, and burial records.

Information can be searched through the data catalogue or browsed by subject. Supporting maps, dashboards, documents and applications are also provided where appropriate, helping people to find, understand and reuse Council data.

New datasets are added as they become available, while existing information is reviewed and updated regularly. Users can also provide feedback or suggest datasets they would like to see published.

The website is maintained by Stirling Council’s Research, Data and Innovation Team as part of the Council’s commitment to openness and transparency.

UK Open Government License

It sets out best practice, standardises the licensing principles for government information, and recommends the Open Government Licence (OGL) as the default licence for public sector information. The OGL is an implied licence. By using information made available under the licence you indicate that you have accepted its terms and conditions. There is no need to register or formally apply for a licence.
