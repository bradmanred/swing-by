Electric vehicle charging points
 </h1>
 </div>
Electric vehicle charging points are now available in the town centres of Belper, Heanor, Ripley, and Alfreton.</p>
 How do I use a BP Pulse point as a pay as you go user </li>
 BP Pulse quickstart guide </li>
You can also find out more by viewing our frequently asked questions below, use the drop down menu to expand each question.</p>
Frequently asked questions</h2>
 Where are the charging points located?
 ˅ 
Field Lane, Belper – 8 charge points</p>
</li>
Shirley Road, Ripley – 8 charge points</p>
</li>
Rogers Lane East, Alfreton – 8 charge points</p>
</li>
Ilkeston Road, Heanor – 8 charge points</p>
</li>
 </div>
 What type of charge point are they?
 ˅ 
All locations are equipped with 7kw fast chargers.</p>
 </div>
 How much is it to charge?
 ˅ 
Charging is priced at 59p per kwh.</p>
 </div>
 Do I have to pay to park whilst charging?
 ˅ 
Yes. You must pay and display or hold a valid parking permit while your vehicle is charging.</p>
 </div>
 Who do I contact if I encounter any issues with the charge point?
 ˅ 
Shirley Road and Field Lane charge points are operated by BP Pulse. The Bp Pulse team is available 24/7 and happy to help. Call BP Pulse on 0330 016 5126 or email at heretohelp@bp.com .</li>
Rogers Lane East and Ilkeston Road charge points are operated by Evolt/Swarco.  For immediate help with EV charger support please call freephone helpline: 0808 281 3508 or email driversupport.uk@swarco.com </li>
 </p>
 </div>
 I am a first time user, is there a guide?
 ˅ 
For Evolt/Swarco, please visit https://evoltnetwork.co.uk/faqs/ </li>
For BP Pulse, please visit: https://www.bppulse.com/en-gb/help-and-support/public-ev-charging </li>
 </div>
 How do I pay to charge if I do not have an RFID card?
 ˅ 
 </p>
For BP Pulse download:  How do I use a BP Pulse point as a pay as you go user </li>
For Evolt/Swarco, please visit: https://evoltnetwork.co.uk/payment-methods/ </p>
</li>
 </div>
 How long can I charge for?
 ˅ 
A maximum stay of four hours applies between 8am and 6pm, with no return within three hours.</p>
From 6pm to 8am, the four-hour limit does not apply.</p>
 </div>
 Are the parking bays dedicated to electric vehicles only?
 ˅ 
 At Shirley Road and Field Lane 50% of bays are mixed use (available to all vehicles with a valid ticket or permit).</p>
50% are dedicated to EVs only and may only be used while charging with a valid pay & display ticket or permit.</p>
 At Rogers Lane East, Alfreton and Ilkeston Road, Heanor:</p>
All eight bays are dedicated exclusively to electric vehicles while charging.</p>
 </div>
 Availability during the Charter Fair
 ˅ 
 Please note: the EV charge points on Shirley Road, Ripley will be unavailable during the Ripley Fair:  fairs page .</p>
 </div>
 </div>
Need further help or information?</div>
Contact the team directly on 01773 841437 or email parking@ambervalley.gov.uk </p>
