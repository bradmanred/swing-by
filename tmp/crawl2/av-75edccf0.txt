 Gold Cards </h2>
 Derbyshire County Council offer a Gold Card, which is their travel, discount and library card for older people and people with certain disabilities. </p>
 Apply or renew your Gold Card via the following methods: </p>
Apply online at  www.derbyshire.gov.uk/transport-roads/public-transport/fares-tickets-passes/concessionary-fares/goldcard/gold-card </li>
In person - visit our office at Town Hall, Market Place, Ripley, Derbyshire, DE5 3BT</li>
By phone - 01773 570222 (renewals only)</li>
By email - send an email with your name, address, and request to renew your card to goldcard@ambervalley.gov.uk </li>
Pick up an application form at your local library</li>
You can apply or renew your Gold Card up to 30 days in advance.</p>
 </div>
Need further help or information?</div>
Contact the team directly on 01773 570222 or email goldcard@ambervalley.gov.uk </p>
