Dead animals
 </h1>
 </div>
You can report any dead animals you find on the road using the form below. </p>
Landscape services will dispose of dead animals on the highway, within parks/open spaces and on any other council owned land. Please report any sightings of dead animals using the contact details or online form below.</p>
It is the responsibility of Derbyshire County Council to remove dead animals from the following roads:</p>
A38 (the area of the A38 that runs through Amber Valley is maintained by consultants on behalf of   Highways England ).</li>
A52</li>
A61</li>
A610</li>
