Roads and parking
 </h1>
 </div>
In this section you'll find locations of our car parks, how to apply for parking permits or bus passes, and locations of grit bins to help during the bad weather. You can also report abandoned vehicles or look up notices of temporary road closures. </p>
Your first point of contact about street lighting, pot holes and roads will be Derbyshire County Council . They are responsible for the highways in Amber Valley.</p>
