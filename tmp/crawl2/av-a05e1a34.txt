Temporary road closures
 </h1>
 </div>
Residents who wish to apply for a temporary road closure will need to complete  our application form  our application form and return it to the below address.   </p>
 Notes to applicant </p>
At least 6 weeks notice must be given (failure to do so may result in the council refusing the application).</li>
Permission from the council is subject to final approval by Derbyshire County Council.</li>
Enclose a plan of the proposed closure clearly marked.</li>
Please note that it is the responsibility of the organiser to close the road and provide road closure signs.  Amber Valley Borough council does not provide this facility.</li>
Please ensure that all neighbours who may be affected by a closure agree to the proposal or that arrangements will be made to accommodate their access requirements. </li>
Please return the form and plan to Amber Valley Borough Council, law and assets, town hall, Market Place, Ripley, DE5 3BT or email them to   lawandassets@ambervalley.gov.uk </p>
 </div>
Need further help or information?</div>
Contact the team directly on 01773 570222 or email enquiry@ambervalley.gov.uk </p>
