Park Features:
Leverhulme Park is the largest area of public parkland in the Bolton area with football
fields, 5-a-side football pitches, and a running and athletics ground as well as nature
walks and picnic areas. It is bounded by Darcy Lever, Haulgh and Breightmet.
The Senior Play is located beyond the car parking and the Junior and Toddler Play is
located next to the community centre.
The width of the pedestrian access gate is suitable for wheelchairs at 1m.
