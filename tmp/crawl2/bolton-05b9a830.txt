 Car parks – Bolton Council 
Most of the car parks in Bolton town centre are managed by NCP Ltd.</p>
Car park location maps, details and pricing information are available online</p>
 Visit the NCP website </p>
 </p>
There are also free off-street car parks  throughout the Bolton area.</p>
 </h2>
Coach parking</h2>
Free town centre coach parking is available close to Bolton Market and a variety of shops and attractions. Parking is available on a first come – first served basis and must be booked in advance. For more details and to reserve your space, please call Visit Bolton on 01204 332853 or alternatively e-mail Tourist.Information@bolton.gov.uk  or  tourism@bolton.gov.uk .</p>
