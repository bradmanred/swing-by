 Parking permits – Bolton Council 
 Parking dispensation
</h2>
A parking dispensation can be issued when:</p>
loading and unloading is to take place for longer than is normally permitted</li>
work needs to be carried out on the highway or adjacent properties</li>
parking is needed during special events or under unusual circumstances</li>
How to apply</h4>
 Apply for a parking dispensation </p>
The dispensation must be obtained before parking and an administrative charge of £7.90 per vehicle, per day must be paid when collecting the dispensation.</p>
You must apply for the dispensation at least two working days before the date you require it to start.</p>
