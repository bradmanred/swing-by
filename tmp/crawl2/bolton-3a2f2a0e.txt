 Disabled parking bays – Bolton Council 
There are two types of Disabled Parking Bay on the streets of Bolton.</p>
 Enforceable Bays </p>
 </p>
Usually found in town centres and shopping areas. These bays are marked with the text DISABLED and an accompanying sign stating 'Disabled Badge Holders Only'.
See below for more information.</p>
 </p>
 Advisory Bays </p>
 </p>
Installed in residential areas to help disabled residents park near their home. These bays are marked with the disabled persons symbol.
See Disabled parking bays outside your home for more information.</p>
 </p>
 </p>
Enforceable disabled parking bays</h2>
These disabled parking bays are provided in the town centre, district centres and shopping areas for holders of a disabled parking badge provided via the Blue Badge Scheme . These are legal bays enforced by parking wardens. Parking without a badge or not observing the rules can result in a Penalty Charge Notice. </p>
A full list of these disabled bays are outlined in our On Street Parking Guide.   A map of enforceable disabled bays is also accessible in our downloads section.</p>
