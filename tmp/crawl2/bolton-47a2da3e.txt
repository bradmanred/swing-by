                      THE BOROUGH COUNCIL OF BOLTON
                       COMMONS ACT 2006 – SECTION 15
                      NOTICE OF PUBLIC LOCAL INQUIRY
APPLICATION TO REGISTER LAND KNOWN AS HORROCKS FIELDS, BOLTON
AS A NEW TOWN OR VILLAGE GREEN (APPLICATION REF. NO. TVGA 86526)
Notice is hereby given that an Independent Inspector has been appointed by the
Borough Council of Bolton as Commons Registration Authority (“the Registration
Authority”) to prepare a report and recommendation to be considered by the
Registration Authority and will attend at:
        PADERBORN ROOM, BOLTON TOWN HALL, BOLTON, BL1 1RU
                            On 2nd July 2024 at 10.00am
             (and continuing if necessary on the subsequent three days)
to hold a non-statutory public inquiry into the application. The application seeks the
inclusion in the Register of Town and Village Greens of the land described in the
application as Horrocks Fields, Bolton.
Copies of the application documents, representations and inquiry evidence can be
inspected by appointment at Legal Services, 1st Floor, Town Hall, Bolton, BL1 1RU.
Please contact Legal Services on 01204 331325 to make an appointment prior to
visiting to ensure that a member of staff is available to assist you.
