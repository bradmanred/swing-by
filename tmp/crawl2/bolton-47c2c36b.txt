 Pay for parking by phone or mobile app – Bolton Council 
Bolton Council is Going Live on the National Parking Platform</h2>
From 25 February 2026 drivers parking in Bolton Council will be able to benefit from an innovative new parking payment approach being piloted in council car parks.</p>
The National Parking Platform (NPP) is a national initiative supported by the Department for Transport, designed to make parking simpler, more flexible and more customer focused.</p>
What is the National Parking Platform?</h2>
The National Parking Platform allows drivers to choose how they pay for parking, rather than being restricted to a single provider or app.</p>
At participating councils on street parking bays, drivers can select their preferred parking payment provider, giving greater choice and improving the overall parking experience.</p>
Why Bolton Council Has Joined the NPP</h2>
By joining the National Parking Platform, we are able to offer: </p>
Greater customer choice</li>
Multiple parking payment providers</li>
Competitive pricing</li>
A more flexible and modern parking experience</li>
This approach helps future-proof our parking services while putting drivers first.</p>
How to Pay</h2>
Payment can be made by: </p>
Downloading your preferred payment provider’s app on a smartphone</li>
Calling the provider’s automated payment line from any phone</li>
Where payment machines are available, these can still be used .</p>
Available Payment Providers</h2>
Available providers may include: </p>
 APCOA Connect </li>
 AppyParking </li>
 Caura </li>
 JustPark </li>
 PayByPhone </li>
 RingGo </li>
Each provider has its own app, contact number and fees.</p>
Fees</h2>
Fees are set by each payment provider and may vary. Any additional SMS charges relate to optional text confirmations and parking expiry reminders.</p>
Find Out More</h2>
The National Parking Platform is supported by the Department for Transport.
Find out more at  The National Parking Platform (NPP) website </p>
