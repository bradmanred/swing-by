 Parking motorcycles – Bolton Council 
Within the Town Centre there are dedicated parking bays for motorcycles located on Victoria Square. </p>
Motorcycles may be parked in the signed area without time restriction or payment.</p>
Parking motorcycles in vehicle bays</h2>
If you park in a pay and display bay you will need to pay.  It can be difficult to display tickets on motorcycles and they can be easily removed, so please either:</p>
pay via an app on your mobile phone and no ticket is needed</li>
buy a pay and display ticket and then write your registration number on it so that no one else can use it</li>
If you are unable to display the ticket on your motorcycle and you receive a Penalty Charge Notice you can it with a legible photocopy of your pay and display ticket.</p>
Parking motorcycles in residents parking zones</h2>
You must display a valid parking permit if parking in residents parking zones.</p>
You can apply for a permit if you are resident or use a visitors permit or scratchcard if parking when visiting the zone.</p>
