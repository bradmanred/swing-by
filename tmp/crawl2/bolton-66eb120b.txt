Park Features:
Westhoughton Central Park is a local park. There is a medium sized field to play on
and a bowling green.
The Senior Play is located beyond the car parking and near the Kings Street
entrance and the Junior and Toddler Play is located near the Bolton Road entrance.
The width of the pedestrian access gate is suitable for wheelchairs at 1m.
