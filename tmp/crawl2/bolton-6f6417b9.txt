 Parking permits – Bolton Council 
 Parking bay suspension
</h2>
What is it?</h4>
A pay and display bay may be suspended in certain circumstances, eg contractors loading and unloading throughout the day to adjacent premises.</p>
How to apply</h4>
 Apply for a parking bay suspension </p>
You must apply for the suspension at least two working days before the date you require it to start.</p>
If you apply for a suspension and then decide to cancel, you must do this at least two working days before the suspension is booked to start, otherwise you will still be charged.</p>
How much does it cost?</h4>
There is a standard charge of  £26.50  plus an additional  £7.90  per bay, per day.</p>
