Section 1: Introduction
The following report details the operations, enforcement, and financial activities of
Bolton Council’s Parking Services for the period 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025. This
report is intended to ensure transparency and accountability in all parking related
activities.
Parking enforcement across Bolton is conducted by Civil Enforcement Officers
(CEO’s) who issue Penalty Charge Notices (PCN’s) on regulated highway and car
parks. Enforcement operates year-round, ensuring compliance with parking
restrictions and other traffic regulations.
Bolton Council exercises powers granted under Civil Parking Enforcement (CPE)
enabling enforcement not only of parking contraventions but also certain moving traffic
offences, including bus lanes.
Section 2: Penalty Charge Levels
Penalty charges are set nationally and vary by type of contravention. Bolton Council
enforces both parking and bus lane contraventions, with varying stator charge levels.

Section 3. Enforcement
Parking Restriction Enforcement
Civil Enforcement Officers (CEOs) proactively enforce parking regulations across
Bolton, addressing contraventions relating to waiting, loading, and parking restrictions.
Enforcement covers all regulated highways, Controlled Parking Zones (CPZs),
resident permit schemes, and council-operated car parks through routine patrols and
targeted operations.
To support operational effectiveness, CEOs have limited dispensations allowing them
to park temporarily in restricted areas while performing essential enforcement duties,
but only where no lawful alternative parking is reasonably available. This approach
ensures enforcement activities are carried out efficiently without negatively impacting
traffic flow, pedestrian safety, or residential accessibility.
Bus Lane Enforcement (CCTV)
Bus lane contraventions are actively monitored and enforced using CCTV systems at
strategic locations.

School Enforcement
School Keep Clear School safety zones marked "School Keep Clear" are proactively
monitored by rotating CEO patrols. This targeted approach effectively promotes
compliance and enhances pupil safety during peak school hours.
Future Enforcement Developments
In the coming year, it is anticipated Bolton Council will expand its enforcement
capabilities by introducing Moving Traffic Enforcement (MTE) powers, as provided
under Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004. This expansion will address
additional contraventions, including box junction misuse, prohibited turns, and other
moving traffic violations. These strategic enhancements aim to further improve traffic
management, road safety, and overall compliance within Bolton.
Section 4: Enforcement Arrangements
On-street parking enforcement is contracted to NSL Ltd, whose Civil Enforcement
Officers (CEOs) issue PCN’s directly for observed contraventions.
Contraventions captured via CCTV enforcement, including bus lanes, are also
reviewed by NSL ltd. Confirmed contraventions are subsequently passed through to
the Parking Services notice-processing system, where postal regulation 10 PCN’s are
issued by council officers.
All PCN processing, including the management of appeals and representations, is
handled internally by the Council’s Parking Services team.

All representations and appeals are considered in-house by specially trained council
appointed Parking Services team members, who assess each case on its individual
merits and in accordance with statutory guidance and local enforcement policy.
If a motorist remains dissatisfied with the outcome of their representation, they may
appeal to the independent Traffic Penalty Tribunal (TPT). TPT adjudicators provide a
final and binding decision on the validity of the PCN. TPT adjudicators provide a final

Section 6: Car Parks
NCP operate the councils pay and display car parks in the Bolton Town centre as part

Bolton council also operates free car parks across the borough, further details on the
free car parks in operation can be found at: Free Car Parks. It is important that drivers
check any signage when parking on a car park to ensure they are parking in line with
the restrictions specified.

Section 8: Suspensions and Dispensations
Suspensions
Bolton Council allows parking restriction suspensions in specific areas to support
essential activities such as building works, utility repairs, and special events. These
temporary suspensions permit vehicles to park where restrictions would otherwise
apply, helping facilitate short-term operational needs.
Application Process:
    •   Applications must be submitted in advance using the official Council request
        form.
    •   Each request is assessed on a case-by-case basis and is subject to availability.
    •   A charge is applied to facilitate the suspension, the fee is outlined on the
        councils website at Streets and parking: Request a parking bay suspension
Conditions and Eligibility:
Suitable for activities where vehicle access is necessary and no lawful alternative is
available. Filming requests.
Suspended areas must be clearly indicated and strictly adhered to.
The Council reserves the right to withdraw the suspension if misused.
Dispensations
Bolton Council provides the option to request temporary parking dispensations for
vehicles that need to be parked in contravention of normal restrictions to facilitate
essential activities. These may include building works, utility maintenance, or specialist
deliveries where access is critical, and no lawful parking alternative exists.
Key Points:
   •    Dispensations are considered on a case-by-case basis.
   •    Applications must be submitted in advance using the Council’s official request
        form.
   •    Charges apply for approved dispensations
   •    Further Information and Application Form: Streets and parking: Request a
        parking dispensation permit
