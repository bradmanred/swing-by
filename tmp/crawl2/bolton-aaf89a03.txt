Park Features:
Astley Park is a small local park. There is a medium sized field to play on and a
bowling green.
The Senior Play is located next to the main entrance and the Toddler and Junior Play
is just next to it.
The width of the pedestrian access gate on the Play Area is suitable for wheelchairs
at 1m.
