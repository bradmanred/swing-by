 Common land, town and village greens – Bolton Council 
As a Commons Registration Authority, we have a responsibility under the Commons Act 2006 to maintain the register of common land and of town and village greens in Bolton.</p>
The register is a statutory register open for inspection by any member of the public in the One Stop Shop in Bolton Town Hall.  There is no charge to inspect the register.</p>
Alternatively, you can apply for an official search of the register by completing this form  and selecting the CON29O search option.  This search relates to Common Land and Village Greens.  Typically, a solicitor will complete this form on your behalf, but you can also do it yourself if you prefer.</p>
