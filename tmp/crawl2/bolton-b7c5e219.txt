Parking Services
Our core data protection obligations and commitments are set out in the council’s
primary privacy notice at www.bolton.gov.uk
This notice provides additional privacy information for:
   • Members of the public
It describes how we collect, use and share personal information about you
    • In relation to parking services
    • the types of personal information we need to process, including information the
       law describes as ‘special’ because of its sensitivity
It is important that you read this notice, together with any other privacy information we
may provide on specific occasions when we are collecting or processing personal
information about you, so that you are aware of how and why we are using such
information.
The information collected and held will vary dependent on the service used and will
include parking enforcement, parking appeals, correspondence, parking suspension
and dispensations, parking permits, resident permits and card payments for parking.
Please ensure that you include the correct Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) reference
within any correspondence with the council or when making payment. If the
reference is inputted incorrectly, this could result in your PCN continuing through the
process and your appeal or payment not being actioned.
Please note that your PCN starts with the prefix BO and is followed by 8 digits.
Purposes
We collect your personal information for the following purposes:
• Carry out parking enforcement under Civil Parking Enforcement
• Deal with your challenges, queries and formal appeals in respect of Penalty
  Charge Notices
• Process applications and issue parking suspensions and dispensations
• Process applications and issue resident permits
• Process applications and issue parking permits
• Take payments for parking permits, resident permits, Penalty Charge Notices and
  parking suspensions and dispensations
• Prevent or detect crime and disorder
• Investigate fraud or misuse of public funds
• In connection with legal proceedings

Categories of personal data
In order to carry out these purposes we collect and obtain:
   •    Basic details of your vehicle such as vehicle registration number, vehicle
        ownership checks (DVLA), vehicle make and model, location of vehicle, civil
        parking contravention details.
   •    Basic details about you such as name, address, telephone number, email
        address.
   •    Contact we have had with you, such as any correspondence and formal
        parking enforcement notices.
   •    Photographs and CCTV footage if the civil enforcement officer has
        activated their body worn video camera or hand held camera.
   •    Video footage of vehicles contravening bus lane and moving traffic restrictions.
   •    Your credit/debit card details if you have paid for a service by card.
Legal basis for processing
The legal basis for processing your personal information is:
   • you have entered into a contract with us (e.g. parking permits, resident
     permits, parking suspensions and dispensations)
   • performing or exercising obligations under law
   • in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority
   • fraud prevention
   • where it is needed to protect your interests (or someone else's interests) and
     you are not capable of giving your consent
If you fail to provide certain information when requested, we may not be able to
perform the contract we have entered into with you or we may be prevented from
complying with our legal obligations.
Information sharing/recipients
We may share personal information about you with the following types of
organisations:
    •   DVLA (Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency
        In order to identify the registered keeper of a vehicle at the date of the
        contravention, for the purpose of the enforcement of a Penalty Charge Notice
        (in accordance with The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions
        (England) General Regulations 2007) or for the consideration of prosecution
        in any other related matter, we may contact the DVLA and provide them with
        the details known to us.

       Provide an independent service for considering formal appeals against
       penalties issued for parking, bus land and moving traffic contraventions in
       England (outside London) and Wales.
   •   TEC (Traffic Enforcement Centre at Northampton County Court)

       Newlyn recover outstanding debt owed to the council for penalty charge

   •   CDER Group

       notices issued in respect of Civil Parking Enforcement.
   •   Marstons
       Marstons recover outstanding debt owed to the council for penalty charge
       notices issued in respect of Civil Parking Enforcement.
   •   NSL Ltd
       The council’s Enforcement Contractor will access information for the purpose
       of monitoring, training and complaint handling of Civil Enforcement Officers
       and for investigating PCN appeals.
   •   National Fraud Initiative
       This authority is under a duty to protect the public funds it administers, and to
       this end may use the information you have provided for Residents and
       Visitors permits for the prevention and detection of fraud. It may also share
       this information with other bodies responsible for auditing or administering
       public funds for these purposes.
   •   Unity 5
       Unity 5 hosts the IT system (ZatPark) and provides support and maintenance
       for the IT system used to issue and process penalty charge notices.
   •   Videalert provides cameras and the support and maintenance for the IT
       system used to issue Regulation 10 and bus lane penalty charge notices.
   •   Greater Manchester Police – or any Police Force as required
       Police Officers may be provided with information for the detection and/or
       prevention of crime.
   •   Motoring Organisations such as the RAC and the AA or independent
       breakdown and recovery agents for the purposes of verifying
       evidence.

 We will not normally share your information with organisations other than our partner
 organisations without your consent; however, there may be certain circumstances
 where we would share without consent such as where we are required to do so by
 law, to safeguard public safety, and in risk of harm or emergency situations. Only the
 minimum information for the purpose will be shared.
As well as information collected directly from you, we also obtain or receive
information from:
    •   NPP (National Parking Platform) - This provides users of the on street pay and
        display parking bays the option of paying to park by phone and app.
    •   NCP Ltd - NCP manage and maintain some of the Council car parks (Primarily
        in Bolton Town Centre) and Bolton Council may request customer details for
        the purpose of monitoring, complaint handling and administration of the
        Council’s salary sacrifice permit parking scheme.
Automated Decisions
For this service:
   • All the decisions we make about you involve human intervention.
Data retention/criteria
We will only keep your personal information for as long as the law specifies or where
the law does not specify this, for the length of time determined by our business
requirements.
Rights of individuals
You have certain rights in relation to the council’s use of your personal information.
To find out more about how these rights apply in particular circumstances, please
refer to our Guide to exercising your rights or alternatively visit the Information
Commissioner’s web site at www.ico.org.uk.
If you wish to exercise your rights or to raise a concern about the handling of your
personal information by the council, please contact our Information Governance
Team at information.security@bolton.gov.uk.
If you are still unsatisfied you should contact The Information Commissioner’s
Office by post at the ICO, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9
5AF or by telephone: 0303 123 1113
