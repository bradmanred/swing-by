 Parking permits – Bolton Council 
 Resident parking permits
</h2>
Existing areas</h3>
We have three residents parking scheme areas. These allow residents and their visitors to park their vehicles, displaying valid parking permits, close to their homes.</p>
Zone A - Dorset Street area, Bolton</h4>
Dorset Street - odd numbers 1-59 and even numbers 56-126 </li>
Castle Street - even numbers 2-76</li>
Dowson Street - 1, 3, 4 & 5</li>
Court Street - even numbers 2-6</li>
May Street - even numbers 4-16</li>
Halstead Street - even numbers 2-52</li>
Bolton Spiritualist (SNU) Church - 21 Bradford Street</li>
Grace Christian Centre - 126 Dorset Street</li>
Zone C - Marsden Street area, Westhoughton</h4>
Marsden Street - even numbers - 6-16</li>
Edward Street - odd numbers 13-17</li>
Wood Street - even numbers 2-16 and odd numbers 1-9</li>
Zone D - Mill Street, Westhoughton</h4>
Mill Street - even numbers 4-14</li>
If you are a resident in one of the above zones and need to purchase or renew a permit, please contact us  for further details.
