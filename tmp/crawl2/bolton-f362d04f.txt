Park Features:
This park is built on what used to be Horwich Station. The trains have long gone, but
they’ve been replaced by a very good little park. There’s plenty of space for the kids
to let off steam, paths to explore, a small stream that runs through the park and
sports facilities.
The Senior Play is located next to the Chorley New Road entrance and the Toddler
and Junior Play is located next to St John Street entrance.
The width of the pedestrian access gate is suitable for wheelchairs at 1m.
