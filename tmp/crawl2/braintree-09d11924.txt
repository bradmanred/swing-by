 Report an abandoned vehicle
</h1>
You should report vehicles that are causing an obstruction or parked in a dangerous position to the Police.  </p>
You can use our online form to report a vehicle that you think has been abandoned.</p>
 Report an abandoned vehicle </p>
A vehicle may be considered abandoned if two or more of the following signs are present:</p>
Flat tyres</li>
Broken or missing windows/windscreen</li>
Mould, rust, or signs of long-term neglect</li>
Missing number plates</li>
Vehicle is not roadworthy or in a poor state of repair</li>
Burned out or severely fire-damaged</li>
Parts missing (e.g. wheels, engine components)</li>
Accumulation of debris around the vehicle</li>
Rubbish or waste inside the vehicle</li>
Vehicle is unlocked or unsecured</li>
Unpaid tickets or notices present</li>
To report an abandoned vehicle, you will need to provide the following information:</p>
Your name, address, and contact details</li>
The vehicle registration number</li>
The make, model, and colour of the vehicle</li>
The location of the vehicle</li>
Its current condition</li>
The time and date you noticed it</li>
A photo of the vehicle</li>
To check that a vehicle has been taxed and has an MOT, visit the gov.uk website .</p>
Once we receive your report within 72 hours we will investigate:</p>
If the vehicle is taxed</li>
Has a current MOT</li>
Determine if the vehicle meets the Abandoned Vehicle criteria</li>
Complete a site visit</li>
After this, we will:</p>
put a sticker on the vehicle to show it has been reported as abandoned</li>
The council does not have the legal authority to investigate the following:</p>
Untaxed vehicles must be reported to the  DVLA </li>
Vehicles without an MOT certificate must be reported to  Essex Police </li>
Illegally or inconsiderately parked vehicles should be reported to  North Essex Parking Partnership  (NEPP).</li>
Vehicles causing obstruction or danger should be reported to  Essex Police .</li>
