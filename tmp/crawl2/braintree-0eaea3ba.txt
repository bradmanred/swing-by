 Transport East consultation
</h1>
A public consultation has been launched by Transport East on a new regional transport strategy to support growth and improve people’s quality of life across the East.</p>
 Webcast Available Here : https://braintree.public-i.tv/core/portal/webcast_interactive/628331 <!-- rel:snippet --> </p>
They have developed its draft strategy to guide transport investment and set priorities for better transport over the next 30 years covering Essex, Norfolk, Suffolk, Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock.</p>
A virtual exhibition containing more information about the proposed strategy was available during the consultation.</p>
The survey and consultation closed on Sunday 30 January 2022.</p>
