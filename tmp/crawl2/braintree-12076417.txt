 Off street parking places order
</h1>
An off-street parking order is the legal basis for providing parking spaces, setting appropriate charges, and enforcing restrictions.</p>
Braintree District Council’s current Off Street Parking Places Order came into force on 4 September 2023. The Order has subsequently been varied.</p>
