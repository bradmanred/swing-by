 Residents parking
</h1>
We use controlled zones in parts of our district to make sure residents can park conveniently for where they live.</p>
We work with the North Essex Parking partnership to manage our residents parking.</p>
You can find more information on residents parking on the North Essex Parking Partnership website.</p>
Residents permit</h2>
You can apply for a residents parking permit online</p>
 Apply for a parking permit </p>
To apply for a parking permit you will need:</p>
your name, address and contact details</li>
your phone number</li>
your email address</li>
your Council Tax number</li>
your vehicle registration number</li>
Residents permit prices</h3>
You pay for your resident permit annually:</p>
the first permit costs £75.00 per annum</li>
the second permit costs £125.00 per annum</li>
You can apply for a Concessionary permit if you receive the higher mobility component of Disability Living Allowance. You can contact the North Essex Parking Partnership on 01206 282316 for further information.</p>
Eligibility</h3>
Any resident who lives at an address in a controlled zone can apply for a residents parking permit.</p>
You cannot apply for a residents parking permit if you home is:</p>
a holiday home</li>
a second home</li>
not occupied</li>
Using your permit</h3>
Your permit allows you to park in any available space in the controlled zone that your permit covers.</p>
A permit does not allow you to:</p>
park where there are waiting restrictions  such as single or double yellow line</li>
to exceed time limits for parking</li>
You can find full details on how to apply for and use your permit in the guidelines.</p>
Renewing your permit</h3>
The North Essex Partnership will send you a renewal notice around three weeks before your permit runs out. You should renew your permit as soon as possible so that you can get your new one in time.</p>
Visitor permits</h2>
You can buy a visitors permit to allow your guests to park in zone controlled areas. You can apply for visitors permits online.</p>
 Apply for visitors permit </p>
To apply for visitors  permits you will need:</p>
your name, address and contact details</li>
your phone number</li>
your email address</li>
your Council Tax number</li>
Visitors permit prices</h3>
You can buy visitors permits for different lengths of stay:</p>
£18.00 for 10 stays of 24 hours</li>
£10.00 for 10 stays of 6 hours</li>
£38.00 for a carers permit</li>
£250.00 for an annual Traders Permit</li>
You can contact the North Essex Parking Partnership on 01206 282316 for further information.</p>
