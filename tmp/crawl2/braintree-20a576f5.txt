 Street cleaning standards
</h1>
 Our Customer Charter
</h2>
Keeping our district clean and tidy is a major task which our team of street cleaning operatives carry out. Their work includes:</p>
litter picking</li>
mechanical street sweeping</li>
emptying of dog poo and litter bins</li>
removal of graffiti, fly-tips and fly-posting</li>
We have committed to providing the best possible service to all our customers. Our Customer Charter</p>
sets out the standards of service you can expect from us in relation to street cleansing</li>
will help you tell us if we are performing well or if we need to improve</li>
What we do</h2>
Under our charter we will:</p>
clear litter hotspots within one working day of getting a report</li>
remove accessible and non-hazardous fly-tips within one working day of getting a report</li>
respond to reports of fly-posting within five working days of getting a report</li>
remove general graffiti within five working days of getting a report</li>
remove offensive graffiti within one working day of getting a report</li>
clean the town centres daily, including Saturdays and Sundays</li>
sweep the channels (kerbs) in all parishes four times a year</li>
empty litter and dog bins at least once a week</li>
respond to dog fouling complaints within three working days of getting a report</li>
investigate reports of abandoned vehicles within one working day</li>
remove any genuinely abandoned vehicles within 15 working days</li>
You can contact our Street Scene team if you have any questions about our Customer Charter.</p>
