Our toilets are equipped with disabled facilities and are generally open from 8am to 5pm.</p>
We clean our toilets are twice a day, but you can contact our Customer Service team if you find any problems.</p>
You can contact our Customer Services team if you need a Radar key, which gives you access to disabled toilets nationwide.</p>
