 Joint parking initiative
</h1>
The North Essex Parking Partnership is a partnership between Essex County Council and six district and borough councils in North Essex.</p>
Colchester Borough Council leads the partnership and bring together all street-based parking services across North Essex on behalf of Essex County Council. This includes lines, signs, parking restrictions, on-street parking enforcement and the management of residential permit schemes.</p>
Find out about the partnership on the  North Essex Parking Partnership website .</p>
Find out about the North Essex Parking Partnership Joint Committee for On Street and Off Street Parking .</p>
We have a separate arrangement with the North Essex Parking Partnership for them to carry out:</p>
ticket machine maintenance</li>
parking permit/season ticket administration</li>
parking enforcement in its Pay and Display car parks</li>
The benefits of this include:</p>
3PR parking scheme to make parking around schools safer</li>
MiPermit: 24/7 cashless parking</li>
Safer roads with less congestion</li>
fair, consistent and transparent parking enforcement process</li>
provision and administration of on-street permit parking schemes</li>
educational programmes that promote safe, considerate and legal parking</li>
Parksafe camera cars, which focus on dangerous, careless or inconsiderate parking</li>
removal of vehicles in certain circumstances</li>
