 Illegally parked vehicles
</h1>
Illegally parked vehicles cause congestion hinder:</p>
other road users</li>
legitimate parkers</li>
delivery vehicles</li>
buses</li>
emergency vehicles</li>
Dangerous parking affects the safety of all road users.</p>
Parking restrictions</h2>
Controlled parking zones restrict where and when you can park and are shown by</p>
yellow lines</li>
loading bays</li>
bus stops</li>
taxi ranks</li>
Double yellow lines mean no parking at any time.</p>
Single yellow lines will have a sign nearby explaining the restrictions. You should always check the signs.</p>
As a driver you are responsible for making sure that you park your vehicle properly and do not cause an obstruction. If you have followed the regulations you will not receive with a Penalty Charge Notice.</p>
