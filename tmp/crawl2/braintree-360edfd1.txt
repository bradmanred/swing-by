 Taxis and minicabs
</h1>
 About Hackney carriages and private hire vehicles
</h2>
We have a responsibility to provide information on your rights to as a passenger in a Hackney carriage (taxi) or private hire vehicle (minicab) in our district.</p>
Any vehicle which is used for the purpose of conveying passengers for hire or reward must be properly licensed to do so. This means that we</p>
licence the drivers</li>
make sure the vehicles are safe</li>
they driver has the correct insurance</li>
Taxis and minicabs offer similar services, but operate in a different way.</p>
 Hackney carriage </h2>
Hackney carriage is the official term used for a taxi.</p>
Hackney carriages can:</p>
carry passengers for hire or reward</li>
be hailed by prospective passengers in the street</li>
park on a rank to await the approach of passengers</li>
 Private hire vehicle </h2>
A private hire vehicle is:</p>
a motor vehicle built or adapted to seat fewer than nine passengers, which is not a hackney carriage or public service vehicle</li>
carries passengers for hire or reward by prior booking</li>
Private hire vehicles can only take bookings through a licenced private hire operator.</p>
