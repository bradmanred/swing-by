 Silver End Memorial Gardens
</h1>
Silver End Memorial Gardens are a great place to enjoy fresh air, green space, and time with your family.</p>
Visitor information</h2>
The gardens have a range of facilities including:</p>
free entry</li>
three children's play areas</li>
lots of seating surrounding the pond</li>
an extensive grassed area</li>
a wide selection of trees such as lime, chestnut and cherry trees</li>
a railing protecting key areas</li>
three commemorative plaques</li>
an accessible toilet</li>
refreshments which are available to buy within an easy walk from the garden</li>
Cycling is not allowed in the park.</p>
You must keep your dogs on a lead at all times in the gardens.</p>
The garden features are set amongst extensive grassed areas surrounded by lime and chestnut trees. There is a central double avenue of poplar and cherry trees. You will also find glorious specimen trees, mixed borders, rose and heather gardens, a pergola and a Japanese Garden. Railings with three sets of historic gates embellished with commemorative plaques protect the gardens.</p>
