 Witham Weekly Market
</h1>
About the market</h2>
We’ve revamped the Witham Market to bring you more traders every week and more stalls every week.</p>
You’ll be able to discover a new cosmopolitan mix of stalls including:</p>
street food</li>
jewellery</li>
traditional sweet treats</li>
bespoke gifts</li>
a drinks bar</li>
and much more</li>
All the usual traders will be there too.</p>
The market runs all year round, every Saturday.</p>
Where we are</h2>
The weekly market runs in Lockram Lane car park.</p>
Parking</h2>
You can look at car parks in Witham to find parking in Witham town centre.</p>
Opening times</h2>
The weekly market runs from 7:30am to 3:30pm.</p>
Apply to trade</h2>
If you are looking to trade at a market in our district, visit our trade at a market page .</p>
Keep up to date with our social media channels</h2>
You can find news and information on Witham weekly market on our social media channels:</p>
