 Witham Street Market
</h1>
Whether you're looking for a bite to eat or to do some shopping, Witham Street Markets are a great social experience.</p>
Where we are</h2>
You can find Witham Street Market in Newlands Shopping Centre.</p>
The market also runs into Lockram Lane Car Park, where you will find the Witham Traditional Market.</p>
Parking</h2>
You can look at car parks in Witham  to find parking in Witham town centre.</p>
Opening times</h2>
Traders for the street market open their stalls at 8:30am.</p>
They begin to close their stalls at 3pm, with a final closing time of 6:30pm. </p>
Future dates</h2>
We do not have dates planned at the moment. We are keeping this under review and will let shoppers and traders know when we are able to re-introduce them.</p>
Apply to trade</h2>
If you are looking to trade at a market in our district, visit our trade at a market page .</p>
