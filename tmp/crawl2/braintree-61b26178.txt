 Grass cutting and horticulture
</h1>
We are responsible for maintaining public parks and open spaces in the district including our sports pitches and cemeteries. Our maintenance includes:</p>
cutting grassed areas</li>
planting and tending flower and shrub beds and hanging baskets</li>
trimming hedges and bushes</li>
emptying litter bins and clearing litter</li>
marking out sports pitches</li>
ensuring that play equipment is safe</li>
keeping footpaths and fencing for which we are responsible in good repair</li>
We also carry out some highway verge cutting for Essex County Council.</p>
All of our parks have a dedicated team of staff who pride themselves on delivering a high standard of grounds maintenance. Mobile gardening teams regularly visit sites to make sure we maintain these standards.</p>
If you notice a problem in one of our parks or open spaces, or think we have missed something during our routine maintenance, you can report it online.</p>
 Report a problem </p>
To complete this form you will need to tell us:</p>
your name, address and contact details</li>
information about the problem and where you saw it</li>
the date and time you saw the problem</li>
Grass cutting</h2>
We maintain roadside verges and open grassy areas, such as parks and open spaces. However, some areas may be cut by the local town or parish council to a different standard.</p>
We aim to cut the grass every 20 working days.</p>
We are unable to cut the grass:</p>
when there is excessive rainfall</li>
during the height of summer, i.e middle of July to middle of August</li>
Areas that have been rewilded or left to naturalise receive a single cut in the autumn each year.</p>
We do not cut grassed areas on privately owned land including that owned by social landlords.  </p>
Rural Highway Verges</h2>
Essex County Council (ECC) are responsible for rural highway verge cutting in Essex.</p>
In the Braintree district, we carry out this service on behalf of Essex County Council. Rural roadside verges are typically cut twice a year, with one cut in the spring and a second cut in the autumn.</p>
Where a need is justified, you can request a safety cut to verges to maintain clear sight at junctions, road splays and roundabouts etc.</p>
Some parish councils have their own arrangements with the Highways Authority to cut the highway verges in their parish.</p>
Grave maintenance service</h2>
Our staff maintain the grounds in our four Cemeteries and can provide a chargeable grave maintenance service on request. You can find more information on our cemeteries page .</p>
Commercial grounds maintenance service</h2>
If you are looking for a professional and reliable grounds maintenance service, look no further. We have experienced staff who can deliver a wide range of services to help ensure that your public facing areas always project the right image.</p>
You can contact our Customer Services Team for a free, no obligation quote .</p>
Verge cutting schedule</h2>
You can find out when cutting begins in the district on our  verge cutting schedule webpage .</p>
Reports</h2>
We took part in a trial with Essex County Council to leave sections of rural road verges in central Braintree uncut until the autumn, in a new environmentally friendly trial.</p>
To find out more information, you can read our verge cutting reports for the previous two years:</p>
