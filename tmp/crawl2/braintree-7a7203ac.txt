 Braintree Street Market
</h1>
Whether you're looking for a bite to eat or to do some shopping, Braintree Street Markets are a great social experience. </p>

Where we are</h2>
The Braintree Street market, runs through the town centre in various areas.</p>
Street Market traders are located within Great Square, sweeping along the High Street and New Street and up into Bank Street.</p>
We also have occasional stalls located with George Yard Shopping Centre, and Sandpit Lane.</p>
Parking</h2>
You can look at car parks in Braintree to find parking in Braintree town centre.</p>
Opening times</h2>
9am - 5.30pm</p>
Traders must be on site before 8am to set up, and all vehicles of site and parked at the designated parking for traders by 9am. </p>
Apply to trade</h2>
If you are looking to trade at a market in our district, take a look at our trade at a market page.</p>
