 Taxis and minicabs
</h1>
 Complain about a taxi or minicab
</h2>
You can contact us if you are not happy with the service you have received from a taxi or minicab.</p>
To make a complaint you need to tell us:</p>
your name, address and contact details</li>
the fare shown on meter, if there is one in the vehicle</li>
the driver's ID Card name or number</li>
the vehicle licence plate number and colour</li>
the time, date and place of your journey</li>
You should send the details of your complaint our Licensing Services Manager.</p>
Once we receive your complaint, we will investigate the incident within seven working days.</p>
We also welcome comments or letters of satisfaction. When we get these, we pass them on to the company and driver concerned.</p>
