 Cashless parking
</h1>
As from 4 September 2023 our car park ticket machines will no longer accept cash payments. Customers can pay by debit/credit card at the ticket machines, or pay by MiPermit by registering online or downloading the phone App.</p>
MiPermit offers great flexibility including:</p>
cashless payments</li>
ticketless parking.</li>
extending your stay without having to return to the car park</li>
booking up to seven days in advance</li>
 Watch a video on how to use MiPermit </p>
Locations supported by MiPermit can offer customers the ability to park and pay with ease.</p>
 View car parks in Braintree </li>
 View car parks in Witham </li>
You can register with MiPermit either online, mobile app or by SMS</p>
 Register for MiPermit online </p>
To register online you will need your:</p>
vehicle registration</li>
payment card details</li>
mobile number</li>
email address</li>
 Register for MiPermit by SMS </p>
To register by SMS you will need your:</p>
vehicle registration</li>
payment card details</li>
Create or pay for a stay</h2>
If you are already a member and would like to create or pay for a stay, you can do so by:</p>
 phone </li>
 SMS </li>
 online </li>
 iPhone app </li>
 Android app </li>
If you have any questions or would like to know more, you can visit the MiPermit website .</p>
