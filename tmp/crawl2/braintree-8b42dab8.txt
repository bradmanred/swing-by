 Pedestrianisation of Braintree town centre
</h1>
 About the scheme
</h2>
We have transformed Braintree town centre to create a safer, flexible, traffic-free and more attractive town centre space to encourage people to visit and use.</p>
 </p>
The pedestrian area restricts access for most of Braintree town centre from:</p>
The top of the High Street (near entrance to Sandpit Lane)</li>
Great Square</li>
Market Place</li>
Access is maintained at all times for emergency services, emergency utility works and refuse collection vehicles only.</p>
There is also a restricted parking zone at Manor Street, Victoria Street and Fairfield Road. Visit our parking web page for details of the arrangements.</p>
 </p>
