 Book a sports pitch at Great Notley Country Park
</h1>
 Book a sports pitch
</h2>
 The Artificial Grass Pitch at Great Notley Country Park, has been temporarily closed, with immediate effect. </p>
The pitch remains closed at this time, and we are therefore unable to accept bookings. We are currently working with the contractor to finalise the programme of works and confirm the timescales for completion before the facility can reopen.</p>
We would like to thank you for your patience and understanding and we will provide a further update as soon as we are able to confirm the reopening arrangements. Please accept our apologies for the ongoing delays</p>
</div>
Please note that bookings can’t be made within 24 hours and weekend bookings need to be arranged before 2pm on Fridays.</p>
We have two different styles of pitch that you can book at Great Notley Country Park.</p>
We have an Artificial Grass Pitch (AGP) and a Multi Use Games Area (MUGA) available for hire for football, basketball, netball or tennis. </p>
Both pitches are open from 9am to 10pm Monday to Friday and 9am to 4pm Saturday and Sunday. </p>
Parking is available onsite for both types of pitch. </p>
Pitch dimensions </h2>
As per the FA guidance for pitch sizes, the AGP measures at 60 yards long and 40 yards wide with a 16ft wide goal at each end without runoff. Which is the standard size for a 7-a-side match. </p>
Make a casual booking</h2>
To make a casual booking please use our online booking forms.</p>
Artificial Grass Pitch</h4>
Book a sports pitch online (AGP) - Pitch closed until further notice</p>
 </p>
Multi Use Games Area</h4>
 Book a sports pitch online (MUGA) </p>
You must make same day bookings at least two hours in advance.</p>
You must pay for your booking when you make it.  You must pay using a debit or credit card.</p>
Please read the terms and conditions before making a booking. If you have any queries before you book please call 01376 552525.</p>
Cancel a booking</h2>
To cancel a booking, you need to call us on 01376 552525 and ask for the Pitch Booking team or email bussupport@braintree.gov.uk .</p>
You must cancel your booking at least an hour before your start time.</p>
Single bookings</h3>
If you cancel or postpone your booking at least 14 days before your hire date, we will refund half of the hire fee</p>
If you cancel or postpone your booking less than 14 days before your hire date, you will have to pay the full hire fee.</p>
If you cancel a booking, but rebook it within four weeks of the original hire date, we will transfer your payment to your new booking.</p>
Block bookings</h2>
If you are interested in making a block booking, please email your desired days and times to our Pitch Booking team at  bussupport@braintree.gov.uk  who will be able to look into your request and respond within 2 working days.</p>
If you are already one of our block bookers and would like to cancel a booking, you should email bussupport@braintree.gov.uk . Please refer to the terms and conditions on your booking sheet in regards to cancellations and refunds. </p>
