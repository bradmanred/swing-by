 Taxis and minicabs
</h1>
 Your rights as a passenger
</h2>
When you hire a taxi or minicab you have certain rights. These are:</p>
the driver must charge no more than the fare that is shown on the meter unless you agree a before the journey starts</li>
the driver must take you to your destination by the shortest possible route unless you ask for a different route</li>
the driver must not allow other passengers to share the vehicle without your agreement</li>
if you ask the driver. They should help in loading and unloading luggage.</li>
the driver should also carry it to or from a house, station or place where you are picked up or set down</li>
the driver should not smoke in his vehicle</li>
Insurance</h3>
The driver must make sure that he has adequate insurance to cover you for the duration of your journey.</p>
You may not be covered if the number of passengers carried exceeds what is permitted by the licence:</p>
each person is a passenger regardless of age</li>
the number of passengers the vehicle can carry is on the plate on the back of the vehicle</li>
If you use a minicab, you may not be covered if you didn't pre book journey with the company's office beforehand and there is an accident which is the driver's fault.
