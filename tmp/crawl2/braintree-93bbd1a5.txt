 Allotments
</h1>
Our allotment sites</h2>
We own and run 10 allotment sites across our district.</p>
The average size for of our allotment plots is 126 square metres, however we do offer smaller "half plots" or larger plots which you can request upon making your application. </p>
Each allotment site has at least two taps, which are free to use by all tenants. </p>
You can find out where our allotments are by looking at our  list of sites .</p>
Apply for an allotment</h2>
We do not currently have any allotment plots available, however we are accepting applications for our waiting lists. Due to high demand, we cannot guarantee a waiting time on any of our lists. </p>
You can join the waiting list by filling in an  allotment application form .</p>
Once a plot becomes available, we will email you to let you know and arrange for you to attend a site visit before accepting the plot.</p>
If you wish to be taken off of a waiting list, please email  bussupport@braintree.gov.uk .</p>
Allotment pricing</h2>
We charge £0.60p per square metre for an allotment.</p>
An average-sized plot is 126m, so this means an allotment costs around £75.60 per year.</p>
We review our prices each year on 1 April.</p>
We send invoices on a yearly basis every August. These include any price increases for the year. </p>
You can pay invoices using our  online payments system .</p>
Allotment rules</h2>
Our full list of Terms and Conditions is available to you upon receiving your agreement, or if you require a copy of these please send a request to bussupport@braintree.gov.uk . </p>
Structures and sheds</h3>
You need to email us if you want to build a structure or shed on your plot.</p>
Your email needs to include details about your plot and the size and purpose of your structure.</p>
We will send you an email telling you if we have approved your request.</p>
Bonfires</h3>
You can have bonfires on your plot but you must follow these rules:</p>
the bonfire must not encroach onto other plots</li>
you must be respectful to neighbouring properties and gardens</li>
you must not leave the bonfire unattended</li>
you must extinguish the bonfire if asked to do so</li>
Inspections</h3>
We inspect the allotments twice a year to check that you are not breaching any of the terms of your allotment agreement. </p>
If we find that your plot is untidy or not to our expectations you will be issued with a written warning. </p>
If you do not improve your plot, we will give you a 30-day notice to remove your items before we offer the plot to someone else.</p>
