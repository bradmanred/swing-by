 Outdoor gyms
</h1>
We have three outdoor gyms across our district.</p>
Our outdoor gyms are:</p>
open all year round, subject to the weather</li>
free to use</li>
for anyone aged eight and over</li>
Locations</h2>
You can find our outdoor gyms at:</p>
Meadowside playing field, Coldnailhurst Avenue, Braintree</li>
John Ray Park, Braintree</li>
The playing field, Spa Road, Witham</li>
See Outdoor Gyms - Active Essex for more locations.</p>
Available equipment</h2>
Our outdoor gyms have a variety of equipment for you to improve your fitness.</p>
For resistance training</h3>
pull down challenger</li>
waist twist</li>
chest press</li>
leg press</li>
dipping bars</li>
sit-up benches</li>
For cardio training</h3>
cardio rower</li>
cross trainer skier</li>
health walker</li>
horse rider</li>
ski machine</li>
push hands yoga</li>
 </p>
