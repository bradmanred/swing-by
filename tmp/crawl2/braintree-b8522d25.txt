 Braintree Weekly Market
</h1>
We have worked with traders to put measures in place to keep customers safe. This includes making changes to the layout of the stalls, hygiene facilities and signs reminding shoppers how to stay safe when they are waiting, including:</p>
keeping 2 metres apart when queuing and shopping</li>
washing hands regularly and thoroughly once home</li>
browsing but not touching items on the stalls until you are buying</li>
We are doing what we can to make the town centre safe for people to visit. However, it is still people’s responsibility to be sensible and safe and keep following government guidance.</p>
About the market</h2>
Braintree is a lovely historic, market town that has hosted a charter market since the 11th century. The traditional style market runs in the heart of the town centre every Wednesday and Saturday, all year round. </p>
Come along to support your local market and traders, selling local food, drink, gifts and much more.</p>
The market runs all year round, weekly every Wednesday and Saturday.</p>
Where we are</h2>
The market is located within Braintree Town Centre.</p>
Our Traditional Traders are located within Market Place, Great Sqaure along the High Street and up into Bank Street. </p>
Parking</h2>
You can look at car parks in Braintree  to find parking in Braintree town centre.</p>
Opening times</h2>
The weekly market runs from 7:30am to 3:30pm</p>
Apply to trade</h2>
If you are looking to trade at a market in our district, take a look at our trade at a market page.</p>
