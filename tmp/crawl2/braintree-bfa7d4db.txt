 Parking fines
</h1>
 Pay a parking fine
</h2>
 If you pay your parking fine (PCN) within 14 days you will only have to pay the discounted amount shown on the notice. </p>
</div>
 Pay a parking fine online </h2>
</div>
 Pay a parking fine online </p>
 To pay your parking fine online using a debit or credit card, you will need: </p>
</div>
 your name , address and contact details </li>
 the 10 digit PCN number (you can find this on the front of your PCN) </li>
 your vehicle registration number </li>
 your debit or credit card details </li>
</div>
 Pay a parking fine by phone </h2>
 You can pay your parking fine by phone using the Parking Partnerships' automated payments line on 01206 507275. </p>
</div>
 To pay by phone, you will need: </p>
</div>
 your name , address and contact details </li>
 the 10 digit PCN number ( you can find this on the front of your PCN) </li>
 your vehicle registration number </li>
 your debit or credit card details, including the card number, CSV number, and start and expiry date </li>
</div>
