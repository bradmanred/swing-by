 Blue Badges
</h1>
We do not issue Blue Badges.</p>
Essex County Council are responsible for Blue Badges in our district.</p>
You can find out how to apply, renew and pay for your Blue Badge on their website.</p>
Parking using a Blue Badge</h2>
If you have a Blue Badge, you can park in any of our pay and display car parks.</p>
There are especially wide designated bays in some car parks although Blue Badge holders can use any normal parking bays if they want to.</p>
You must clearly display your Blue Badge in the windscreen of your vehicle while you are parked.</p>
On street parking using a Blue Badge</h2>
If you park on a yellow line in the street, you can usually stay for up to three 3 hours, providing the vehicle is not causing an obstruction.</p>
You should not park in a controlled parking zone unless you are parked in a disabled bay.</p>
You must clearly display your Blue Badge in the windscreen of your vehicle while you are parked.</p>
