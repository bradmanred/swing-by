 Taxis and minicabs
</h1>
 Regulation of drivers and vehicles
</h2>
We licence anyone who drives a taxi or minicab in our district and make sure the vehicles are safe.</p>
Drivers</h2>
We only issue licences to drivers after we have checked:</p>
they have a valid driving licence</li>
they are a fit and proper person</li>
they understand the relevant law </li>
We also carry out medical and criminal checks:</p>
when we first license a driver</li>
at regular intervals</li>
Licensed drivers must wear the ID card which includes their photograph.</p>
Vehicles</h2>
When we license a vehicle fo the first time, it must be under five years old.</p>
We make sure that vehicles are:</p>
pass an inspection every six months to higher standards than the normal MOT test</li>
are in a clean and tidy condition</li>
display their license plate on the rear of the vehicle</li>
We also carry out additional unscheduled inspections.</p>
