 About Braintree District
</h1>
Braintree District is located in the heart of Essex, between Colchester, Chelmsford and Uttlesford.</p>
Our district is broken down into 3 main towns and 26 wards .</p>
Our district consists of a population of 151,561 (according to data from ONS in 2019), which is broken down into:</p>
Population breakdown in the Braintree District</h3>

This makes our district the 4th most populated in Essex.</p>
Covering 612 square kilometres, Braintree District is the second largest district in Essex. We sit just behind Uttlesford at 641 square kilometres.</p>
90% of Braintree District is green space and contains 31 different parks and open spaces.</p>
Our district has over 9800 businesses in it and our residents have an average income that is 8.7% higher than the England national average.</p>
Residents in our district also say that safety is their number one priority. This is reflected in our crime rate, which is 24% lower than the England national average.</p>
 </p>
To find out more about our district and what we have to offer, you can look at our tourism site .</p>
