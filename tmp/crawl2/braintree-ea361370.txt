 Report graffiti and fly-posting
</h1>
 Report graffiti
</h2>
We have a duty to remove graffiti from:</p>
public buildings</li>
street furniture such as benches</li>
public monuments</li>
Responsibility for removing graffiti from other structures such as commercial buildings, telephone boxes, bus shelters and electricity boxes, rests with the owners or company that installed them.</p>
You can report instances of graffiti to us using our online form.</p>
 Report graffiti </p>
To complete the form you will need to tell us:</p>
your name, address and contact details</li>
where the graffiti is</li>
the date and time you saw the graffiti</li>
Response times</h2>
Once we receive your report we will:</p>
remove offensive graffiti within one working day</li>
remove non-offensive graffiti within five working days</li>
We aim to deliver a high quality service, which is always improving  meets the needs of the local community.</p>
Keeping our streets looking attractive and free of graffiti benefits all our residents and visitors to our district.</p>
What we will not do</h2>
We will not remove graffiti from private properties.</p>
