About Bus Service Operators Grants</h2>
We receive an annual Bus Service Operators Grant (BSOG) from the Department for Transport (DfT). This helps us to pay for community transport for older and vulnerable people.</p>
As part of the grant conditions, we report to the DfT how we use these funds.</p>
You can read our survey response below which explains how we used this funding.</p>
For more information about these grants see: Gov.uk - Bus Service Operators Grants </li>
