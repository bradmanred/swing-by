Bury Activation Scheme</h3>
Activation is working with those who live, work, study or visit an area to help identify, and break down barriers they face towards choosing to travel by active modes. The aim is to make walking, cycling, or wheeling a viable choice for people, and to empower people so that they feel able to change their behaviours.</p>
