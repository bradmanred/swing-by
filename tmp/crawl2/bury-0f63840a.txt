Report an obstruction on the highway</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We are responsible for ensuring that all adopted highways are not restricted, and that any obstructions are removed.</p>
Use this form to report any objects or structures that are obstructing the highway such as walls, fences, locked gates, building materials, vehicles or overhanging vegetation. </p>
Please answer all the questions in our online form as clearly and accurately as you can to help us deal with the issue you are reporting. </p></div></div>

How we respond to reports</h2>
On adopted highways, we aim to inspect the issue within five working days. How quickly we resolve the issue depends on how urgent it is.</p></div></div></div></div>
