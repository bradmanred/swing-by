Park volunteers</h2>
Volunteers play an extremely important role in maintaining the standards of our parks and countryside sites. It is a great way to get active and learn new skills, as well as helping with tasks like:</p>
Hands on maintenance</li>
Improvement work</li>
Gardening and gardening clubs</li>
 Litter picking </li>
Leading walks</li>
Help organising and running events</li>
The role of volunteer is open to a wide range of ages. Experience isn't necessary as volunteers will have an induction and receive training.</p></div></div>
