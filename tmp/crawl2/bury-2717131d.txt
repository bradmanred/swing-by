Report a street furniture issue</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We are responsible for maintaining street furniture in public areas within the borough of Bury. Examples of street furniture include:</p>
benches</li>
crash barriers</li>
fences and gates</li>
litter bins</li>
pedestrian guardrails</li>
cycle stands</li>
road signs</li>
street lights</li>
traffic bollards</li>
 Please note: </p>
Bury Council is not responsible for maintaining street furniture on private land.</li>
Bus shelters and bus stops are maintained by Transport for Greater Manchester </li>
Telephone boxes, poles and cabinets are maintained by Openreach .</li>
Report it</h2></div></div>
 Report faulty or missing street furniture </div></div>
 Report a sign issue </div></div>
 Report a faulty street light </div></div>
 Report a bollard issue </div></div>
How we respond to reports</h2>
In areas we maintain (for example, roads, pavements, public open spaces, parks, cemeteries and car parks) we aim to inspect the issue within 10 working days.</p></div></div></div></div>
