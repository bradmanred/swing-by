About reporting an abandoned vehicle </h2>
Vehicles may be abandoned if they have not been moved for a significant length of time. Other signs of an abandoned vehicle include flat tyres, missing wheels, broken or open windows and the vehicle is unlocked. Motorists no longer have to display a tax disc on their vehicle so this is not a sign that a vehicle has been abandoned. </p>
Please answer all the questions in our online form as clearly and accurately as you can to help us deal with the issue you are reporting. </p></div></div></div></div>
