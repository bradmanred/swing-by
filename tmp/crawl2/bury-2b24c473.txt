Main content area</h2>
Trades people or site workers may apply for permission to be exempt from parking restrictions on a temporary basis, if it is essential to their work.</p>
Whilst we will try to accommodate your request to support your business, our first concern is that it does not compromise road safety.</p>
Please complete one of the forms below:</p></div></div>
 Suspension Form - to park in an on street pay and display bay or council car park </div></div>
 Waiver Form - to park on yellow lines or any non pay and display bay (eg loading bay, residents parking zone etc ) </div></div>
We will process your application within 2 working days. If you need a permit sooner, for example for emergency remedial work, contact us on 0161 253 5353. Extra charges may apply.</p></div></div></div></div>
