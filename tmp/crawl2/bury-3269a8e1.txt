Reporting a litter issue</h2>
We are responsible for cleaning up litter on streets and public open spaces and we look into all the issues reported to us.</p>
Use this form to report:</p>
A fuel spillage from a car (small amount)</li>
Leaves gathering in an area</li>
Litter gathering in an area</li>
An overflowing litter bin</li>
A dog bin that needs emptying or is damaged</li>
Nitrous oxide canisters</li>
Please answer all the questions in our online form as clearly and accurately as you can to help us to deal with the issue you are reporting.</p></div></div>

About litter issues</h2>
Our Street Cleansing Service provides a range of services and works hard to keep Bury clean and tidy. Tasks include:</p>
Litter picking</li>
Emptying litter bins and dog waste bins</li>
Road and footway sweeping</li>
Pressure washing</li>
Removal of fly tipping</li>
Area deep cleaning to remove litter, leaves, detritus and weeds</li>
The Street Cleansing Service is complemented by volunteers and community groups that organise community clean-ups and carry out litter picking in their local areas.</p>
Other organisations, including schools, colleges and the Highways Agency also have a duty to keep their land clear of litter.</p>
Cleaning up litter costs us, schools, colleges and the Highways Agency a lot of money that could be used to support other public services if more people disposed of litter more responsibly.</p>

Littering: Fixed penalty notice</h2>
Dropping litter (including food waste, cigarette butts or chewing gum) is an environmental offence. This offence can lead to a fine.</p>
A fixed penalty notice will be issued if one of our officers has witnessed that someone has committed an environmental crime.</p>
You might be prosecuted and have to pay more if you do not pay your penalty on time.</p></div></div>
