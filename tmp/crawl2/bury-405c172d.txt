A welcome from the chair</h2>
We are working with Transport for Greater Manchester to deliver the Bee Network – Greater Manchester’s integrated, low-cost, high-frequency public transport network, bringing together local trams, buses and hire bikes – and ultimately local train services – with the largest walking, wheeling and cycling network in the country.</p>
We want the Bee Network to meet the needs of Bury’s residents, businesses and visitors when travelling within and beyond the borough – that means delivering on providing cleaner, cheaper and more reliable public transport.</p>
By bringing transport back under public control we can ensure that residents have a real voice over their buses, trams, trains and active travel, unlocking more opportunities for our residents and businesses.</p>
As well as chairing Bury Local Bee Network Forum, I am also one of Bury’s representatives on the Greater Manchester Bee Network Committee .</p></div></div>

 About the Bee Network<!-- --> 
</path> </h2>
The Bee Network is a multi-million pound investment programme to transform the way we travel across Greater Manchester, with joined up transport services, affordable fares, and cleaner, greener transport.</p>
A key goal of the Bee Network is to ensure that by 2040, 50% of all trips – equivalent to one million daily journeys – are made by public transport, walking, wheeling or cycling. Key benefits of more people regularly opting for sustainable travel will mean:</p>
Less road congestion.</li>
Improved air quality.</li>
Better health and wellbeing.</li>
A well-supported local economy.</li>

 About Bury Local Bee Network Forum<!-- --> 
</path> </h2>
The Bury Local Bee Network Forum has been created to help ensure the network within the Bury borough develops to meet the transport needs of our residents, businesses and visitors. The forum aims to:</p>
provide opportunities for locally elected councillors, residents and partner organisations to influence local transport policy and services</li>
support our ambitious regeneration plans, Let's Do It strategy and local priorities</li>
ensure Transport for Greater Manchester and other agencies are held responsible for the operational performance of our transport network.</li>
 </div></div></div></div></div></div>
The Bury Local Bee Network Forum is your opportunity to find out more and influence what's happening with local transport here in the Bury borough and across Greater Manchester. </p></div></div></div>
