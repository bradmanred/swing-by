Report a grass cutting issue</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
During the growing season we cut grass on highway verges and in public gardens and parks. We have a limited budget for grass cutting so some public grassed areas are left to grow naturally.</p>
We don't look after grass on private land. This is the responsibility of the owner.</p>
Please answer all the questions in our online form as clearly and accurately as you can to help us deal with the issue you are reporting.</p></div></div>
