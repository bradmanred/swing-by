Highway Safety Inspection Policy</h2>
The primary aim of this policy is to provide direction to the Highways Team, specifically those involved in undertaking highway safety inspections, so that they can carry out their duties consistently and to clear, recognised and understood criteria.</p>
The policy sets out the practices in terms of network hierarchy, investigatory levels, frequency of inspection and response times for repairs.</p>

Bury Local Transport Strategy</h2>
Bury Local Transport Plan sets out our vision for the borough’s transport for the next 20 years and is our 20-year plan for transport investment.</p>
Our Transport Strategy Vision</h3>
By 2040, the borough’s towns will be connected to each other, to Greater Manchester and beyond by an affordable, safe, reliable and well-maintained low carbon transport system. It will be easy to get around by public transport, on foot and by bike. Walking and cycling will be the first choice for short journeys for those who are able to walk and cycle. Investment in transport will help to grow the economy, reduce deprivation and improve the health and wellbeing of residents.</p>
