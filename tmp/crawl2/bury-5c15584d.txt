Road markings</h2>
Road marking work is generally carried out during spring, summer and autumn. Road markings contribute to a safe driving environment by conveying information to road users which might not be possible using upright signs.</p>
Road markings can be advisory, such as keep clear markings. They can also be enforceable, eg yellow lines, box junctions, or stop lines.</p>
We take a risk-based approach to the maintenance of road markings, prioritising those markings that contribute the most to the safety of road users.</p></div></div>
