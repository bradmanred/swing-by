Streets for All - Bury town centre links</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We are developing proposals to improve connectivity to Bury town centre and provide safe and convenient travel options for everyone.</p>
Improvements are proposed to make walking, wheeling and cycling safer and easier in the following three areas around Bury town centre:</p>
 Southern Gateway - to the south of Bury town centre, between Bury Grammar School / Fishpool and the town centre ring road</li>
 Western Gateway - to the west of Bury town centre, between Burrs Country Park and Woodhill Fold and the Woodfields area</li>
 Freetown - to the northeast of Bury town centre, between the town centre and Hoyles Park</li>

 About Streets for All schemes<!-- --> 
</path> </h3>
Streets for All schemes are part of the multi-million-pound investment in the Bee Network that aims to transform the way we travel across Greater Manchester, with joined up services, affordable fares, and cleaner, greener transport.</p>
A key goal of the Bee Network is to ensure that by 2040, 50% of all trips – equivalent to one million daily journeys – are made by public transport, walking or cycling. Key benefits of more people regularly opting for sustainable travel will mean:</p>
Less road congestion.</li>
Improved air quality.</li>
Better health and wellbeing.</li>
A well-supported local economy.</li>

 Funding for Streets for All schemes<!-- --> 
</path> </h3>
This scheme is being funded by UK Government via the Streets for All element of Greater Manchester’s City Region Sustainable Transport Settlement, which is part of the investment in the Bee Network to transform the way we travel across Greater Manchester.</p>
This particular element of funding may only be spent on capital investment for walking, cycling, and sustainable transport improvements and cannot be used to fund other day-to-day revenue spend such as maintaining the roads.</p></div></div></div></div></div></div>
Overview</h2>
We have been awarded funding from UK Government via the Streets for All element of Greater Manchester’s City Region Sustainable Transport Settlement (CRSTS) to make it safer, easier and more enjoyable to walk, wheel and cycle around Bury Town Centre.</p>
We want to improve connections to workplaces, schools and the town centre, as well as enhance the look and feel of local streets and neighbourhoods.</p>
By making walking, wheeling and cycling more attractive, especially for shorter journeys between residential areas and key destinations, we can help reduce congestion, lower emissions, and improve the health and wellbeing of our communities. </p>
 </p>
Community involvement</h2>
A consultation on these proposals was open from Monday 29 June to Monday 10 August 2026.</p>
Responses were provided by attendance at public engagement sessions and by completing an online survey.</p>
Two public engagement sessions were held at Castle Leisure Centre (30 June 2026) and Bury Market (8 July 2026). At these sessions there was an opportunity to view the Streets for All scheme proposals, meet the project team (to ask them any questions) and complete the survey.</p>
