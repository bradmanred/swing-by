Main content area</h2>
We inspect all road issues reported to us. How quickly we repair road faults depends on how urgent the problem is. We give the highest priority to those faults that may cause a threat to public safety.</p>
Pot holes should be reported online so that: you can check if the pot hole has already been reported to us or not and so that you can check yourself where we are up to with its repair. You can do this via the link we send you in your confirmation email.</p>
Please note, before you submit the form:</p>
Check if the pot hole has already been reported - there will be a pin in the map at the location of the pot hole if it has.</li>
If the pot hole has not already been reported, drop a red pin at the location of the pot hole before you continue with the form.</li>
