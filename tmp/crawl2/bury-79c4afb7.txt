Main content area</h2>
Fly posting is the display of adverts on land or premises without the consent of the owner or occupier. Fly posting is unsightly and illegal and anyone that fly posts can be fined. </p>
To report fly posting to us please let us know:</p> 
 the type of property or premises that the fly posting is on - eg residential or business;</li>
 where the fly posting is - eg the address if you know it, or details of the area and nearest land mark;</li>
 what the fly posting is advertising - eg details of the event it is advertising and where it is being held.</li> </div></div>
