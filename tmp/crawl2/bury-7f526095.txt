MOT testing</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We have an independent MOT testing station at Bradley Fold Depot, Bradley Fold Trading Estate, Bradley Fold Road, Bolton, BL2 6RS.</p>
There is a waiting area on site or alternatively you can go off site. All we ask is you are back to make payment and collect the vehicle by 3pm.</p>
We carry out MOT tests on vehicles for the general public and businesses. You can check your MOT status here: Check the MOT status of a vehicle - GOV.UK </p>
The facility is a non-repair testing station.</p>
Prices</h2>
MOT Class 4 (Most cars, vans, and passenger vehicles) - £49.50</li>
MOT Class 5 (Minibuses with more than 8 passenger seats) - £61.30</li>
MOT Class 7 (Commercial vehicles weighing between 3,000 kg and 3,500 kg) - £58.60</li>
Payments</h2>
You can pay by either Cash or Card on site. This can either before or after the test.</p>
How to book</h2>
 Phone:  0161 253 6623</p>
Opening hours</h2>
Monday to Fridays: 8am to 2.30pm </li>
Saturdays and Sundays: Closed </li>
