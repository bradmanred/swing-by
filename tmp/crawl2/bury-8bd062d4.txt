Pay a bus lane fine (Penalty Charge Notice)</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
Bus lane fines issued by the council are called Penalty Charge Notices. The bus lanes are monitored by CCTV cameras.</p>
The current bus lane Penalty Charge Notice fee is £70.00. If you pay within 14 days this is reduced to £35.00.</p>
You can pay now using a debit or credit card.</p></div></div>
 Pay a bus lane fine </div></div>
You will need the Penalty Charge Notice number (the 10 digit number that starts with BC) and the registration number of your vehicle.</p>
Payments and appeals</h2>
You can appeal against a bus lane Penalty Charge Notice. </p>
If you appeal within 14 days of the issue of the notice, and your appeal is unsuccessful, you will be given a further 14 days to pay at the discounted rate.</p>
If you pay your bus lane notice whilst your appeal is being considered, your case will be closed and this will bring the appeals process to an end.</p></div></div></div></div>
