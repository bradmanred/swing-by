Dog fouling: Fixed penalty notice</h2>
Not cleaning up after your dog is an environmental offence. This offence can lead to a fine.</p>
A fixed penalty notice will be issued if one of our officers has witnessed, or has proof, that someone has committed an environmental crime.</p></div></div>
