Stray dogs</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
A stray dog is a dog that is in a public place without its owner.</p>
If you find a stray dog and it has a collar with a tag that identifies it, please contact the owner as soon as you can.</p>
How to report a stray dog</h2>
You can report a stray or lost dog to Acorn Kennels on weekdays (Monday to Friday), between 8am and 3.45pm.</p>
 Online: Acorn Kennels - report a lost dog </li>
 Phone: 01948 662931</li>
Where you can take a stray dog</h2>
You can take a stray or lost dog to the following collection centre on any day of the week, between 9am and 4pm.</p>
Manchester Dogs Home, Crofters House, Moss Brook Road, Harpurhey, Manchester, M9 5PG</li>
If you cannot keep the dog secure you should contact an animal rescue centre who may be able to assist you.</p>
Dangerous dogs</h2>
If a dog is out of control or otherwise dangerous, please contact Greater Manchester Police on 101 as this is a Police matter.</p>
Micro chipping</h2>
Micro chipping is recognised as the most effective and secure way of permanently identifying a pet. Once microchipped, the unique identification number is registered to the animal and the owner's details are placed on a national database. It is the law for all dogs to be microchipped.</p></div></div></div></div>
