Parking fine - make a payment</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
Parking fines issued by the council are called Penalty Charge Notices. NSL Services Ltd provide parking enforcement for council car parks and on street parking on behalf of Bury Council.</p>
The current parking Penalty Charge Notice fee is either £50.00 or £70.00. If you pay within 14 days this is reduced by half to £25.00 or £35.00.</p>
You can pay now using a debit or credit card. You will need the Penalty Charge Notice number (the 10 digit number that starts with BC) and the registration number of your vehicle.</p></div></div>
 Pay a Penalty Charge Notice </div></div>
Debit and credit card payments can be made by phone on 0344-870-5545 (24 hour access).</p>
Payments and appeals</h2>
You can appeal against a Penalty Charge Notice .</p>
If you appeal within 14 days of the issue of the notice, and your appeal is unsuccessful, you will be given a further 14 days to pay at the discounted rate.</p>
If you pay your parking notice whilst your appeal is being considered, your case will be closed and this will bring the appeals process to an end.</p></div></div></div></div>
