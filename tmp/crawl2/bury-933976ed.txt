Public Path Orders</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
Public Rights of Way are diverted, closed or created by a Public Path Order.</p>
The different types of Public Path Orders</h2>
Divert a path or section of path (Section 119 Highways Act 1980)</li>
Close (stop up) a path or section of path (Section 116/118 Highways Act 1980)</li>
Create a new length of path (Section 25/26 Highways Act 1980)</li>
Divert or close a path to allow development to take place (Section 257/258/261 Town and Country Planning Act 1990)</li>
How to apply for a Public Path Order</h2>
Before you apply for a Public Path Order we advise you to contact the Public Rights of Way Officer.</p></div></div>
 Apply for an appointment with the Public Rights of Way Officer </div></div>
What happens next</h2>
The Public Rights of Way Officer will contact you within 10 working days to arrange a convenient time for a site meeting.</p>
Following your meeting, the Public Rights of Way Officer will send you the appropriate application form(s).</p>
