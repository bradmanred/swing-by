Bowls hire</h2>
Sets of bowls and jacks can be hired for:</p>
A season</li>
Overnight</li>
A weekend</li>
The costs of hiring bowling green equipment are:</p>
Per season hire: £39.50 and a £60 deposit</li>
Overnight or weekend hire: 
Bowls set: £8</li>
Jacks only: £4 per jack</li>
