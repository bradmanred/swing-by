Streets for All Radcliffe - Victoria Street/School Street neighbourhood</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We want to improve the street environment and provide better travel options for everyone in the Victoria Street/School Street neighbourhood in Radcliffe.</p>
Our aim is to:</p>
Improve connectivity to local places and services</li>
Provide safe and convenient travel options for everyone</li>
Create high quality public spaces</li>
 </div></div>
 Open<!-- --> All </div>
 Community involvement<!-- --> 
</path> </h2>
Before developing proposals we invited the community to tell us about:</p>
The ways they currently travel</li>
How they feel about travel around their neighbourhood</li>
Any specific challenges and opportunities they think there are</li>
Responses were provided by attendance at a community engagement sessions and by completing online surveys.</p></div></div></div>
 The proposals<!-- --> 
</path> </h2>
We have listened to what the residents and businesses in the area have had to say about improving the area. So far, you have told us:</p>
People do not feel safe walking or wheeling</li>
Vehicle speeds are too high</li>
Vehicles do not comply with one-ways</li>
Safety concerns related to HGV access to Beesley and Fildes builder's merchants on Lord Street</li>
People want more play spaces</li>
More trees and planting</li>
There is antisocial behaviour and littering</li>
Our updated proposals in response to your feedback aim to bring improvements to your area. They include:</p>
Improvements to pavements to make it easier to walk and wheel</li>
New raised tables and speed humps to reduce vehicle speeds</li>
Changes to one-way street system and two new vehicle filters to reduce dangerous vehicle movements</li>
Safer access for HGVs to and from Beesley and Fildes via Lord Street, Coomassie Street, Ulundi Street, and Abden Street.</li>
Improvements to Abden Street Park, the skate park, and alleyways</li>
More plants and greenery across the neighbourhood</li>
 </div></div></div></div></div></div>
Next steps</h2>
We will review and consider the feedback received to finalise the proposals.</p>
Final proposals for the Radcliffe Victoria Street/School Street Streets for All scheme will be subject to council approval prior to implementation.</p></div></div></div>

Background information</h2>
About Streets for All schemes</h3>
 </p>
Streets for All schemes are part of the multi-million-pound investment in the Bee Network that aims to transform the way we travel across Greater Manchester, with joined up services, affordable fares, and cleaner, greener transport.</p>
 A key goal of the Bee Network is to ensure that by 2040, 50% of all trips – equivalent to one million daily journeys – are made by public transport, walking or cycling. Key benefits of more people regularly opting for sustainable travel will mean: </p>
Less road congestion</li>
Improved air quality</li>
Better health and wellbeing</li>
A well-supported local economy.</li>
Funding for Streets for All schemes</h3>
The design of these schemes and future construction work is being funded by UK Government via the Streets for All element of Greater Manchester’s City Region Sustainable Transport Settlement.</p>
If the proposals are approved by Bury Council the work must be completed by 2027.</p></div></div></div></div>
