Main content area</h2>
Drains and grids collect water that runs off the roads and pavements and have a metal grill cover.</p>
Tell us about drains or grids on pavements and roads that are raised or sunken as these may cause a hazard. We also need to know if a drain is blocked, eg if rain water is pooling on the surface and not draining away.</p>
Report a drain or grid issue</h2>
If the issue is any of the following (and an immediate threat to safety) phone us straight away during office hours on 0161 253 5353 or out-of-office hours on 0161 253 6606.</p>
Missing ironworks, for example manholes, gully grills, hydrants and stop taps</li>
Flooding property</li>
If you need information and advice about sewers and water supply pipes, visit the United Utilities website .</p>
Please answer all the questions in our online form as clearly and accurately as you can to help us deal with the issue you are reporting.</p></div></div>
