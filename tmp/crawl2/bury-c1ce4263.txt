About Bury's Big Balsam Bash</h2>
 Volunteer groups, friends of parks groups and members of the public will team up to control the spread, and limit the damage to the bio-diversity in our nature reserves and parks and green spaces caused by the plant Himalayan Balsam . </p>
What is Himalayan Balsam?</h3>
Himalayan Balsam is a non-native invasive plant species that spreads rapidly, outcompeting our native plants and can cause erosion on our riverbanks.</p>
How you can help control the spread</h3>
Himalayan Balsam spreads and flowers during spring and summer.</p>
To combat the growth of this plant successfully we have put together the 'Bury Community Himalayan Balsam Strategy' , by following this plan you can help stop the spread.</p></div></div>
Get involved with Bury's Big Balsam Bash</h2>
People of all ages are invited to take part and do as much or as little as they are comfortable with. No prior experience is needed as it's very easy to pick up.</p>
Wear walking/suitable shoes and protective gloves.</p>
There will be many group meetings at our nature reserves, parks and green spaces in the Bury borough throughout Spring and Summer 2027.</p>
