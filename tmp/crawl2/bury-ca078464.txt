 Report a tree issue </li> </div></div></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
</path> 
We maintain in the region of 6,000 trees growing along the roadside across the Bury borough</div></div></div></div>
These trees are inspected every two years and a programme of pruning work is carried out by specially trained staff every six years. Modern equipment and pruning techniques are used to carry out work on trees. We do not carry out lopping or topping.</p>
Trees are pruned to:</p>
Maintain their health and safety</li>
Prevent physical damage to property</li>
Ensure roads and streets are kept clear</li>
Storm damage to our tree stock and other urgent works can delay the street tree pruning programme.</p>

Issues with street trees</h2>
We do not undertake street tree pruning works in direct response to any natural or seasonal occurrence. For example</p>
falling leaves</li>
sap</li>
falling blossom, fruits, nuts, seeds</li>
bird droppings</li>
sucker growth</li>
seeds germinating from other trees in the borough</li>
blocked or obstructed drains, gutters, flat roofs resulting from tree deposits and leaves</li>
build-up of algae and, or moss</li>
interference with television or satellite reception</li>
blocking sunlight</li>
blocking or obstructing a view</li>
installing or working on solar panels</li>
