Car parks in Bury town centre</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
All council owned car parks are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Parking places are free after 6pm, until 8am the following morning. The charges shown apply Monday to Saturday. On Sundays there is a £1.50 standard charge for all council owned car parks in Bury town centre.</p></div></div>
