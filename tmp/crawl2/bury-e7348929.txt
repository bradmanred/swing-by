Simister Island (Junction 18, M60) improvement works</h2>
Highways England is improving the Simister Island Interchange between the M62, M60 and M66.</p>
Simister Island Interchange is one of the busiest motorway junctions in the north-west used by around 90,000 vehicles each day.</p>
The junction struggles with such high volumes of traffic above what it was designed for, and as a result suffers from congestion and poor journey time reliability.</p>
The project will improve the junction and facilitate smoother flows of traffic along the connecting motorways, contributing to more reliable and safer journeys into and around Greater Manchester.</p>
 M60 junction 18 Simister Island Interchange - National Highways  </p>
Please contact m60j18simisterislandinterchange@nationalhighways.co.uk   with any issues</p>
