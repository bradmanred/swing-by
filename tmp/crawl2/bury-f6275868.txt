About the Fishpool and Pimhole Bee Active Travel Scheme</h2>
The Fishpool and Pimhole Bee Active Travel Scheme is being carried out to: </p>
Enhance pedestrian and cycling facilities </li>
Support active travel throughout the area</li>
Complement other town centre regeneration projects, including Bury Market regeneration.  </li>
The scheme will improve connectivity and safety for people who are walking, wheeling and cycling to local facilities.  </p>
The scheme will be delivered in two phases. </p>
 Phase 1 of the scheme is complete. See below to find out about the new features included in phase 1 and where they are located.</li>
 Phase 2 of the scheme is now underway. See: Fishpool and Pimhole Bee Active Travel Scheme (phase 2) </li>
Funding</h2>
This active travel scheme is funded by UK Government, as part of a wider commitment to encourage healthier and more sustainable modes of transport.</p></div></div>
The new features explained</h2>
The scheme includes new and upgraded crossing points, traffic calming measures and filtered neighbourhood features.</p>
