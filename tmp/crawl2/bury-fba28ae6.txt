Disabled parking spaces</h1></div></div></div>
Main content area</h2>
We provide parking spaces reserved for disabled drivers throughout the borough.</p>
They are for use of drivers with permits under the Blue Badges scheme. If you have a Blue Badge you can park in a disabled space for free and there is no time limit.</p>
If you have a Blue Badge and park in a regular parking space you need to buy a pay and display ticket. </p></div></div>
 Find out more about the Blue Badge scheme </div></div>
