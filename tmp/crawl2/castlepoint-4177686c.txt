Maintenance</h2>
The Council, via its Grounds Maintenance and Street Cleansing Contractor Pinnacle PSG, undertakes the horticultural maintenance and cleansing works of all parks and open spaces to keep them in a good standard. Pinnacle visit the sites regularly to carry out works such as:</p>
Grass cutting (approximately 2-3 week cutting cycle weather permitting from March – October – the height of cut varies according to the type of area but general open spaces are cut to a height of between 10mm – 50mm)</li>
Litter collection</li>
Flower and shrub bed maintenance</li>
Hedge cutting</li>
Maintenance of playgrounds</li>
Maintenance of bowling greens, cricket pitches and football pitches</li>
All are maintained appropriately and standards of cleanliness and health and safety are achieved by regular inspection.</p>
