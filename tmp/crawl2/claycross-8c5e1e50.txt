Sports & Recreation Facilities</h1>
Langwith Parish Council has a range of recreational and sporting facilities.</p>
Whaley Thorns and Langwith Village Hall is available to hire for clubs, groups and parties.</p>
We have three football pitches situated at:</p>
Devonshire Drive Recreation Ground</p>
Whaley Thorns Recreation Ground</p>
Langwith Junctioin Recreation Ground</p>
We also have a free to use 5 A-Side/basketball hardplay area and skate park.</p></div>
