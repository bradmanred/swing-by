 Kenning Park Community Group </p>
Kenning Park Community Group is made up of two Parish Council members, a representative of Kenning Park Forest School and a team of local volunteers. The Group was set up to work towards the regeneration and development of Kenning Park and Meadow Fishing Pond. The Community Group are looking for new volunteers, please contact the Parish Council if you are interested.</p>
The objectives of the Group are:</p> 
To promote the Park as an attractive, functional, and developing amenity within Clay Cross.</li>
To support community activities within the Park.</li>
To raise the profile of Clay Cross.</li>
To undertake any activities that fit within the aims that will benefit the Clay Cross area.</li>
To encourage sustainable wildlife development, habitat enrichment, conservation, and biodiversity of the area.</li> 
 More information about the Community Group can be found on their facebook page. </p>
 Local firm to continue with maintenance at Kenning Park! </p>
After a few early 'hiccups' and a very wet start to 2024, Kenning Park began to look its best and we pleased to confirm that Shirland based Shed Grounds Maintenance will continue to maintain the whole area including around the library building, the flower bed and shrubbery along Holmgate Road.</p>
 Kenning Park Forest School </p>
Kenning Park are very fortunate to have a Forest School.</p>
The Forest School has been run on the Park since 2020, when the Parish Council agreed the use of a corner of the Park for outdoor activities that benefit our local community and wildlife.</p>
The Forest School offers a range of activities and provides equipment such as hammocks, swings and slacklines. Sessions are geared towards free play and experienced based learning. Session details are given in the images below.</p>
Activities offered have included den/shelter building, clay modelling, bug hunts and microscopes, fire lighting, campfire cooking, foraging, tree identification, site and local history, knot-tying</p>
 Please note that the School is NOT a drop-off session, parents and carers are responsible for their children at all times. </p>
For more information contact the Forest School Leader, Michaela Garner on 07743 110 078 or email</p>
