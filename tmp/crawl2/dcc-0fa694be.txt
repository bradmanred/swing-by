 Renew your library items
Visit our new Derbyshire Libraries website to renew or reserve library books online.</p>
Log in to your library account</h2>
Choose 'renew your items' in the catalogue menu. If you have a library card, type the number and your PIN into the user ID box.</p>
 B_line cards and Gold Cards need to be activated at your local library to use them as library cards</p>
Your PIN</h3>
You can change your PIN once you've logged into your account by choosing 'change PIN'.</p>
Renewing your books</h2>
Books can be renewed up to 6 times, for a period of 3 weeks at a time.</p>
Audiobooks can be renewed for a further 3 weeks.</p>
If an item is requested by another user, it can be renewed for 3 days.</p>
Requesting library books</h2>
Books can be requested by visiting any of our libraries or by using our online catalogue.</p>
If we don't have it in stock you can still ask for it. Requests are free. </p>
If we need to borrow the items from another library service, either in the UK or abroad, a charge of £12.50 will apply.</p>
If we don't have what you want in our stock, you can suggest that we buy it and we'll consider your request</p>
Ebooks</h2>
If you're a Derbyshire library member you can also download free ebooks and e-audiobooks from our website.</p>
You can borrow 10 ebooks for up to 3 weeks, and if the book you want is already checked out you can reserve it free of charge.</p>
