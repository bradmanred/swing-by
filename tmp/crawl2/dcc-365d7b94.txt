Welcome to our libraries</h2>
All our libraries are warm welcoming spaces for everyone to use. You don't need to be a member to visit us, but while you're here it's quick and easy to get a library card if you'd like one.  </p>
We also have free online services for all our members, such as  ebooks , e-magazines , e-audiobooks and e-newspapers .</p>
Many of our libraries also have  free, and friendly events for all ages, including reading groups , story and rhyme time for toddlers, and much more.</p>
For ideas on activities and things to read, you can follow  Derbyshire Libraries on Facebook or  Derbyshire Libraries on X .</p>
If you'd like to ask us a question, you can email: ask.library@derbyshire.gov.uk or phone us on telephone: 01629 533444 .</p>
