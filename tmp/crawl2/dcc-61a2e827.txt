Shipley Country Park</h1>
Shipley Country Park is near Derby and Nottingham and offers 700 acres of attractive and varied landscape.</p>

Barbecues are not permitted at any of our sites.</p>
Park information</h2>
Whatever the season, there's always lots to see and do in the park - walking, cycling, horse riding, bird watching, angling, picnicking, kite flying, jogging, wayfaring, photography, exploring the park and much more.</p>
Entry is free but a pay-and-display parking charge applies in our main car parks at the visitor centre and next to Mapperley Reservoir.</p>
The car park on Shipley Hill is for Blue Badge holders only.</p>
Parking for Blue Badge holders is free on all car parks but you must clearly display the permit to avoid a parking fine.</p>
Find out more about car parking at Shipley Country Park .</p>
A free 'Guide to Shipley Country Park' leaflet is available at the visitor centre, and information panels can be found on each car park.</p>
There is another car park for Blue Badge holders only on Shipley Hill Hall site. It is open from 10am to 4pm. Access is from Marlpool, Heanor only.</p>
 Car parking charges apply  (unless you're a Blue Badge holder). You can use 'Pay by Phone'  Cash or Card as a payment method.</p>
For further information email:  countrysideservice@derbyshire.gov.uk .</p>

Booking Shipley for events</h2>
If you'd like to talk to us about booking an event at Shipley Country Park or any of our other sites email: events@derbyshire.gov.uk and we'll be happy to hear from you.</p>
