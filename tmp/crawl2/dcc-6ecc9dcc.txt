Countryside events programme</h1>
We've lined up a range of seasonal events for everyone to enjoy.</p>

Our friendly and experienced teams are on hand to help you safely entertain the kids, learn a new skill and to explore our beautiful countryside destinations.</p>
You can find out about events at:</p>
 what's on at the Cromford Canal and High Peak heritage sites </li>
 what's on at Elvaston Castle </li>
 activities and events at Shipley Country Park </li>
You might also be interested in viewing other council-run events across Derbyshire .</p>
What's on</h2>
Looking for your next outdoor adventure, family activity or seasonal event? Follow Derbyshire Countryside on Facebook and Derbyshire Countryside on Instagram to discover the latest activities, special events, guided walks, trails and updates.</p>
You can also keep up to date with events, activities and news from our parks and heritage attractions on social media:</p>
 Elvaston Castle on Facebook </li>
 Shipley Country Park on Facebook </li>
 Cromford Canal and the High Peak Trail on Facebook </li>
Booking our sites for events</h2>
If you'd like to talk to us about booking an event at any of our sites email  events@derbyshire.gov.uk and we'll be happy to hear from you.</p>
