Buxton Museum and Art Gallery</h1>
Buxton Museum and Art Gallery (BMAG) has moved into its own dedicated temporary building, next to Buxton Library on Kents Bank Road. </p>
Share this page</li>
 Share via Facebook </li>
 Share via Twitter </li>
 Share via LinkedIn </li> 
 Share via Email </li> 
 </div>
The interim home opened to the public on Thursday 15 October 2026.</p>
There is now space to showcase the museum and art gallery's collections, host exhibitions and welcome visitors while work continues to find a permanent home in the town.</p>
As well as the displays of artefacts and artwork, there will be a wider programme of events, educational activities, school visits and community engagement. The museum shop sells a variety of gifts, souvenirs and books, with something to suit everyone.</p>
Suggestions about what you would like to see included in the new service are always welcome. You can email buxton.museum@derbyshire.gov.uk or telephone: 01629 533540 or leave a comment on one of BMAG's websites or social media platforms.</p>
Opening times:</p>
Tuesday to Friday from 10am to 5pm</li>
Saturday from 10am to 2pm</li>
Admission is free – donations are welcome.</p>
