Derwent Valley Mills World Heritage Site</h1>
Derwent Valley Mills in Derbyshire is a World Heritage Site.</p>
Share this page</li>
 Share via Facebook </li>

 Share via LinkedIn </li> 
 Share via Email </li> 
 </div>
 Visit the Derwent Valley Mills website
 Derwent Valley Mills is the birthplace of the factory system, where in the 18th century, water power was successfully harnessed for textile production.</p>
Stretching 15 miles down the river valley from Matlock Bath to Derby, the World Heritage Site contains a fascinating series of historic mill complexes, including some of the world's first 'modern' factories.</p>
We host the Derwent Valley Mills team on behalf of the Derwent Valley Mills Partnership.</p>
Derwent Valley Mills World Heritage Discovery Days Festival</h2>
Around 160 activities are held as part of the Discovery Days Festival every year in October, including the Derbyshire half term holiday.</p>
The festival has attracted up to 20,000 visitors in previous years. Activities vary from rare openings of historic buildings, to guided walks, talks and family events.</p>
