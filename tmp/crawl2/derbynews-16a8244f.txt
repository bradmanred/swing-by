Sinfin Moor Park and Nature Reserve is a wildlife haven with plenty of different habits to see and explore. The extensive area includes scrub, grassland, woodland and ponds, which attract a variety of wildlife to the area.</p>
Learn more about the history of the nature reserve and the wildlife that live there, and enjoy a walk throughout the reserve .</p>
The Pavilion Cafe is open every Saturday from 10.30am to 2pm - run by the Friends of Sinfin Moor Park - with all proceeds going towards improving the park for visitors.</p>
Children can enjoy the play area in the park, or maybe join the Nature Club to learn more about wildlife.</p>
We have a Green Flag Community Award !</p><!-- render [content:37781 0.0001530647277832s --> </div>
