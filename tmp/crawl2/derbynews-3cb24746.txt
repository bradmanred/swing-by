We have a great range of outdoor sports facilities available in our parks. </p>
There are also sports on offer at Markeaton Park Sports Pavilion .</p>
Multisport courts</h2>
 Alvaston Park PlayZone. Bookable via ClubSpark .  A floodlit facility with a Tarmacadam surface (hard-court).  </li>
 Markeaton Park Sports pavilion </li>
Synthetic pitches</h2>
 Springwood Leisure Centre  - 5-a-side pitch filled with sand/rubber, specially designed for football. We also have a court suitable for 5-a-side football. Floodlights are also available. To book, email  parksbookingsadmin@derby.gov.uk  or call 01332 641234</li>
 Chellaston Park -  Chellaston Leisure Limited manage the 3G pitch.  All enquiries to use this site should be sent directly to bookings@chellastonleisure.org .</li>
Normanton Park - Sporting Communities manage both the Playzone pitch and Cruffe Court at Normanton Park. Contact  hire@sportingcommunities.co.uk  for more information. </li>
 Alvaston Park PlayZone, bookable via ClubSpark.   a 3G (artificial grass pitch, made with plastic blades of grass) suitable for football. </li>
Grass pitches</h2>
 Darley Fields </li>
 Alvaston Park  </li>
 Chaddesden Park </li>
 Sinfin Moor Park </li>
 Chellaston Park - Chellaston Leisure Limited manage the football provision on Chellaston Park.  All enquiries to use this site should be sent directly to  bookings@chellastonleisure.org  or visit Chellaston Leisure Limited </li>
To book any of the above grass pitches, email parksbookingsadmin@derby.gov.uk or call 01332 641234. </div>
Tennis</h2>
There are good quality tennis courts at Alvaston Park , Darley Park , King George V Playing Fields and Markeaton Park . All tennis courts are available to book online at Park Tennis Derby . Free sessions are available but must be booked in advance.</p>
 Derby Tennis League  make it easy for adults to play friendly, competitive tennis.</p>
Cricket </h2>
 Sunnydale Park has an artificial cricket wicket and grass outfield</li>
Oakwood Park has an artificial cricket wicket and grass outfield</li>
 Chaddesden Park has an artificial cricket wicket and grass outfield</li>
 Normanton Park has an artificial cricket wicket and grass outfield</li>
 Sinfin Moor Park  has an artificial cricket wicket and grass outfield</li>
 Chellaston Park -  Chellaston Leisure Limited manage the cricket provision on Chellaston Park. All enquiries to use this site should be sent directly to  bookings@chellastonleisure.org  or visit Chellaston Leisure Limited </li>
