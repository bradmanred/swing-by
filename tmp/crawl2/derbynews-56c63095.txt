Family friendly facilities</h1>
 </div>
<!-- render [main_content_image:6717 0.0024631023406982s -->
We have a range of facilities available at our leisure centres for you to enjoy as a family. </p>
We know your leisure time is precious and hope that the the variety of activities on offer at our leisure centres will entice you to spend some of it with us. Whether you want to cycle, work out or play sport, we aim to cater for the whole family.</p>
Activities</h2>
 Gym </h3>
12-16 year olds can use the gym when accompanied by an adult (over the age of 18). Young people would also be restricted to the use of cardio and resistance machines. They would be able to use the free weights area at the age of 16. </p>
Young people can use the gym facilities at Springwood Leisure Centre and Derby Arena , and join with our  Young person's membership .</p>
Group exercise & power cycling</h3>
Group exercise classes are available to customers aged 14 and over. You can join group exercise classes at  Springwood Leisure Centre and Derby Arena . </p>
Sports courts</h3>
Children of any age can play on the sports courts at Derby Arena , when accompanied by an adult.</p>
Offers</h2>
Our Family and Junior Leisure Cards are a great way to make savings as a family. Find out more on our  offers page.</p><!-- render [content:38874 0.00031280517578125s --> </div>
