The £27.5 million Arena is Derby's latest leisure facility offering an exciting range of opportunities from a 250m indoor cycle track , a state-of-the-art 140 station gym , an  Indoor Cycling  and two exercise studios and a sports infield the size of 13 badminton courts.
The Arena also has a café and bar , and offers a range of event & conferencing facilities , as well as meeting and hospitality rooms available for hire.</p>

 up to 3 hours 
 FREE 
</tr>
 over 3 hours

</tr>
Customers using Derby Arena will be asked to put their car registration details into a display screen on entering the building, to provide the 3 hour free parking while using the facilities. </p>
Regular track cyclists can apply for a 12 month parking season ticket -  visit the MiPermit website for more information , selecting buy digital permits, then season tickets.  
 </p>
 Please note the 3 hours FREE parking does not apply during events at the Arena.  The Arena has 1,100 spaces. Spaces are limited on  DCFC  match days, please see individual event pages for details. 6 day an season tickets also available - visit the MiPermit website for more information . </p>
Disabled parking is available on first-come first-served basis.</p></div><!-- render [content:31319 0.00043702125549316s -->
