Frequently asked questions</h2>
Useful information about what to expect at the events.</p><!-- render [content:43829 0.00038290023803711s -->
What happens in bad weather?</h3>
Performances will still go ahead in rainy weather conditions and are never cancelled more than 24 hours ahead of the performance time as the weather can change rapidly. </p>
Performances will only be cancelled in extreme weather conditions e.g. high winds.</p>
Tickets are non–returnable and non-refundable except when an event is cancelled. In the event of a refund, please be aware that we are unable to give cash refunds. If you paid by any other method than card (including a mixed payment of card and something else) please note that the council will issue a cheque refund. We're sorry for any inconvenience this may cause.</p>
Please note that if it is raining please dress appropriately in wet weather gear, we advise you don’t use umbrellas as these could obscure people’s view. </p>
Can I bring a disposable barbecue?</h3>
Barbecues, fireworks and bonfires are strictly not allowed on Markeaton Park.</p>
Can I smoke at the performance?</h3>
Smoking is not permitted within the craft village. We ask visitors who wish to smoke to move outside the enclosed area, out of respect for other audience members. Please note this also applies to e-cigarettes.</p>
What should I wear and bring?</h3>
The performances are held outdoors in our Craft Village so please ensure you wear appropriate clothing. Please bear in mind that it can get chilly later in the evenings so make sure you bring something warm to wrap up in as it is the Great British Weather after all! For the evening performances it is strongly advised that you bring a torch. You may want to bring blankets as it can get cold!</p>
Will food and drink be available on site?</h3>
Throughout the Markeaton Park Outdoor Cinema & Theatre season we will be offering various options for catering within the Orangery café and Courtyard.</p>
The Orangery café will be open to stock up on speciality coffees, infusions and cakes/ice creams and cold drinks.</p>
For Romeo and Juliet, The Rocky Horror Picture Show (12A), Dirty Dancing (12) there will be a limited selection of alcoholic drinks, snacks and popcorn.</p>
There will be a licensed bar at Julius Caesar to grab your favourite drinks.</p>
Do I need to bring a chair?</h3>
The Craft Village area is hard standing. Please bring your own low backed chair but we please ask you don't bring any tables. For Julius Caeser this year there is an option to buy tiered seating meaning you wont need to bring your own chair and it will be supplied for you, see more information about this .</p>
What do I do with my rubbish after the performance?</h3>
When the performance ends please ensure that you put your rubbish in the bins provided when you exit, or take it with you if you wish. </p>
Can I purchase tickets at the performance?</h3>
There will be a box office at each performance, however tickets often sell out for similar events in advance, so we suggest you book early. The Box Office will be open, at the Craft Village entrance, from 1 hour before the performance starts, but please be aware that you will have to join the admissions queue to get tickets. </p>
Can I bring a dog to the performance?</h3>
No dogs are permitted in the performance area unless they are an Assistance Dog. If you plan to bring an assistance dog we’d be grateful if you could inform us when you book or at least 24 hours before the start of the performance.</p>
I have specific access requirements will I be able to attend?</h3>
The whole area of the Craft Village is accessible to all, however we offer limited seating, so please ensure you do bring your own if required. Disabled car parking is available in the Craft Village car park close to the gates when entering the park (Via Mundy Play Centre car park, Markeaton Lane, DE22 4NH)</p>
Is parking available on site?</h3>
There is a Pay and display car park available at Markeaton Park Craft Village, DE22 3BG (Via Mundy Play Centre, Markeaton Lane).  The car park will be open until the end of the performance. Lighting will be provided upon exiting after the film to this car park.  Parking charges apply. </p>
If you are bringing a high-sided vehicle (2.2m or over) to the car park please let us know 24 hours in advance so we can make arrangements (please let us know the vehicle height, registration, and the performance date).</p>
You can use  cashless payment using MiPermit , the location code for  Mundy Play Centre car park  is 754597. </p></div>
