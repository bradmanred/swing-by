 Nor manton Park opened in 1909 as a place people could escape the heavily populated and industrial area. This date is proudly recorded on the clock-tower over the café.</p>
The park linked Normanton Village with the new, industrial, inner-urban Normanton.</p>
The park has been refurbished over the last few years. The roof of the pavilion has been replaced and the clock tower has also been refurbished.</p>
There is a lot to do at Normanton Park, including walks around the traditional parkland, play a game of football on one of our pitches , play a game of chess on our pre-built chess tables, play on the children's playground, or bring a picnic and relax on the grass.</p><!-- render [content:39715 0.00025296211242676s --> </div>
