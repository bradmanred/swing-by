Derby Racecourse Hub is managed on behalf of Derby City Council by  Leisure United. </p>
The project has provided state-of-the-art provision to Derby Racecourse and is part of a national Hubs programme led by the Premier League, The Football Association (FA) and the Government’s Football Foundation.</p>
There is also bookable changing accommodation, community rooms, a cafe, play area and state-of-the-art AGP football pitches.  </p>
Visit the  Leisure United web page  for registration information, bookings and opening times. </p><!-- render [content:41980 0.00016498565673828s -->
The AGP pitches are the fields of dreams, played on by the very best local talent.</p>
Kick off your footie, sign up for our hotly contested local leagues or tackle a tournament. Pay and play for kids and adults, quarter-size pitch hire perfect for five-a-side football, coaching sessions, all-weather training, girls and women’s training and teams, kids afterschool clubs, school holiday camps, free parking, on-site café, football parties and even walking football.</p>
The site acts as the central venue for the Derby Junior League providing grass football fixtures across mini soccer, youth, and senior football pitches. </p><!-- render [content:41979 0.00014400482177734s --> </div>
