Car Parks</h1>
Dronfield Town Council own and maintain the following car parks:</p> 
Church Street (opposite the Parish Church.)</li>
Gorsey Brigg (adjacent to the Scout Hut)</li>
Stonelow (next to the play area)</li>
Coal Aston (Next to tennis courts and bowling club)</li>
Cliffe Park</li>
Dronfield Woodhouse (lower car park at Dronfield Woodhouse recreation ground)</li> 
 Please note, income is not generated from any of these car parks. All of the Dronfield Town Council car parks offer free parking. </p>
 Car Park Spaces </p>
Church Street : 14 parking spaces / 4 disabled parking spaces.</p>
Gorsey Brigg : 11 parking spaces / 1 disabled parking spaces (two hour stay limit)</p>
Stonelow : Designated parking bays are not marked out. 45 - 50 parking spaces (estimated)</p>
Coal Aston : 74 parking spaces / 4 disabled parking spaces.</p>
Cliffe Park : 36 parking spaces / 5 disabled parking spaces.</p>
Dronfield Woodhouse : 20 parking spaces.</p>
 Overnight parking is not permitted in any of these car parks. </p>
 Other Parking in Dronfield </p>
North East Derbyshire District Council are responsible for a number of car parks around the Town, including the Civic Centre, Soaper Lane, bottom of Callywhite Lane and Greendale shops. More information on North East Derbyshire District Council parking and restrictions can be found here. </p>
Please note some car parks in the area also privately owned and operated, such as Pentland Road shops.</p>
If you have an enquiry or complaint about any car park belonging to the Town Council please call 01246 418573 or complete our online form. </p>
If you have an enquiry or complaint about any car park belonging to North East Derbyshire District Council please call 01246 231111 or complete their online form .</p></div>
