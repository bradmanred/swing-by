Stonelow Play Area Refurbishment</h1>
 </div>
The June Town Council meeting saw councillors vote to refurbish Stonelow Play Area on Shireoaks Road with a budget Of £70,000.</p>
Much of the current equipment in the play area has reached the end of its usable service. There has also been recent damage to the soft surface from vandals. The contract for the new play area will go out to tender and will see the introduction of inclusive play equipment.</p>
Chair of Parks Committee, Coun Anthony Hutchinson, commented: "This refurbishment is part of our on-going commitment to provide the very best parks for families across Dronfield. We look forward to seeing new designs put forward by contractors for this much-used play area." </p>
The climbing rock, installed in 2022, will remain in place, as the rest of the park is updated with new equipment and surfacing. A timeline and final plans for the refurbishment will be published after the appointment of the contractor.</p></div>
