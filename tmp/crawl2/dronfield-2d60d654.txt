Current Play Park Equipment Maintenance</h1>
The Town Council manages ten play areas across the town and safety within the play areas is of the utmost importance.</p>
Regular weekly visual inspections are undertaken, along with quarterly and annual operational inspections of all the play areas. If an item of equipment is deemed unsafe or has worn/damaged parts, the equipment will be closed down until the spare parts can be sourced, ordered and installed.</p>
The Standards adopted by the play industry that are expected to be met by providers of children's outdoor play equipment are EN1176 (play equipment) and EN1177 (safety surfacing). By ensuring that regular playground inspections are carried out in accordance with these standards, owners and managers of outdoor play areas can be confident that they're meeting their ethical obligation to protect users of their equipment.</p>
Please notify us of any damaged equipment within play areas by contacting us here. </p></div>
