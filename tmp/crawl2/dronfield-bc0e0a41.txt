Dronfield Junior Parkrun</h2>
Dronfield Running Club, with the support of local sports and community organisations, is seeking the support of Dronfield Town Council for the establishment of an official junior parkrun at Sindelfingen Park on an intial twelve-month licence basis.</p>
 What is a junior park run? </p>
Junior parkrun is a free, inclusive and volunteer-led weekly 2km walk, jog or run for children aged 4–14 years. It is not a race and has no time limit.</p>
 When would it take place? </p>
The proposed event would take place on Sunday mornings at 9:00am and would operate in accordance with parkrun UK's official policies, safeguarding requirements and event-delivery standards. It will be finished by 9:30am.</p>
 What are the benefits of the proposal? </p>
The proposal is intended to provide a free and accessible opportunity for children to be physically active, support healthier and more active families, create volunteering and community involvement opportunities, encourage social connection and promote positive, supervised use of Sindelfingen Park on a non-exclusive basis. The park would be open for everyone to use during the events. It is expected about 40 children will take part.</p>
The proposal is for a twelve-month licence on a trial basis. However, if approved, the Town Council would be committed to keeping an open dialogue with the organisers throughout the year to work together to address any issues which may arise. Dronfield Running Club and supporting organisations recognise that the event must be appropriately managed and that the interests of residents, existing park users and the Council must be properly considered.</p>
Before the licence commences, the necessary arrangements would be agreed with parkrun UK, Dronfield Town Council, Derbyshire County Council and any other relevant stakeholders. These include the proposed route, safeguarding, insurance, risk management, parking, volunteering, funding, park management, stakeholder engagement and arrangements for adverse weather and other operational issues.</p>
The Town Council therefore hosted a public consultation to gain residents feedback - the results of which can be found below.</p></div>
