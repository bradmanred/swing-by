Sports & Recreation Facilities</h1>
 </div>
 Dronfield Sports Centre , operated by North East Derbyshire District Council, is conveniently located at the Civic Centre (Postcode : S18 1PD)</p>
Facilities include:</p>
• Two swimming pools (one large, one small).</p>
• Swimming, gymnastics, Tiny Tumblers and trampoline lessons.</p>
• Extended fitness suite with 75+ stations.</p>
• Studio/multi-use space for health and fitness classes.</p>
• Virtual Instructor classes.</p>
• Over 60 wet and dry fitness sessions/activities.</p>
• A six court sports hall for badminton, bowls, football, basketball and netball.</p>
• Lesser sports hall, with climbing wall. Also used for table tennis, gymnastics classes, martial arts groups, fencing, weight management meetings, fitness classes, kids' parties and netball fitness training.</p>
• Two squash courts.</p>
• Relaxing cafe area.</p>
• Meeting room and halls available to hire.</p>
• Kids' holiday activities and parties.</p>
• RLSS Approved Training Centre delivering National Pool Lifeguard Qualification.</p>
• Baby friendly accredited swimming pools.</p></div>
Local Sports Facilities</h2>
We have a wide range of Sports and Recreation Facilities in the area:</p>
 Football </p>
Coal Aston Playing Fields - football pitches and changing room.</p>
Dronfield Woodhouse x 1 full size pitch.</p>
Multi Use Games Area (MUGA) at Cliffe Park - available for hire.</p>
Please make all football booking enquiries through the Council Offices, using the form at the bottom of the page:</p>
 Cricket </p>
Stonelow - Changing rooms/showers for home and away teams, tea room, pavilion.</p>
Dronfield Woodhouse - Changing rooms, tea room, pavilion.</p>
 Basketball </p>
Outdoor courts at: Sindelfingen Park and Cliffe Park.</p>
 Tennis </p>
Courts at: Cliffe Park, Dronfield Woodhouse, Coal Aston.</p>
 Bowls </p>
Bowling pavilions with tea rooms at: Dronfield Woodhouse, Coal Aston and Cliffe Park.</p>
 Table Tennis </p>
Outdoor table at: Cliffe Park.</p>
 Mini Golf </p>
Course available at: Cliffe Park (use of own putters and balls - no hire facility available)</p></div>
