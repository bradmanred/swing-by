Play Areas</h1>
For the map of the parks, play areas, MUGA or buildings owned by Parish councils, please click on the link below. You will need to enter the postcode of the area you are looking for.</p></div>
 Map of play areas and Council buildings </p>
</li>
 </div>
 Ash Crescent Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Address: Ash Crescent, Birk Hill, Eckington, S21 4AB</p>
Ash Crescent play area can be accessed from Larch Road or between Nos 63 and 65 Ash Crescent. It has a combined slide, balance beam, traverse wall, rope walk, climbing frame for older children and four springers and swings for toddlers. It is a self-contained area surrounded by fences and hedges.</p></div>
 </div>
 Darcy Road Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Darcy Road, Eckington, S21 4BN</p>
The Darcy Road play area can be accessed from the garages site at the top of Darcy Road (Osmund Road end) or from between Nos 22 and 24 Darcy Road. It lies between Darcy Road and Osmund Road. The Play Area has a spinning bowl, seesaw, combined slide and climbing frame, and swings for older children, and swings and a springer for toddlers. The area is surrounded by trees and hedges.</p></div>
 </div>
 Lansbury Playing Field </p>
</li>
 </div>
Lansbury Road, Eckington, S21 4EB</p>
The Ducksett Lane play area can be accessed from Lansbury Road, between the junctions with Mary Street and John Street but on the opposite side of the road. Look out for the Public Footpath sign. It is part of a large recreation ground. The play area has a combined slide and climbing frame, and swings for older children and swings and a springer for toddlers.</p>
There is an area with developed bike 'Pump Track' is designed for bikes and scooters. The track is suitable for older children/youth. E-Bikes/motorbikes are NOT PERMITTED on the track.</p></div>
 </div>
 Emmett Carr Lane Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Emmett Carr Lane, Renishaw, S21 3UL</p>
The entrance to the Emmett Carr play area is next to 68 Emmett Carr Lane in Renishaw. It has a combined slide and climbing frame and swings for older children, and swings and two springers for toddlers. It is enclosed by hedges.</p></div>
 </div>
 Golden Ball Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Main Road, Renishaw, S21 3UW</p>
The Golden Ball play area is behind the Renishaw and Spinkhill Community Hall on Main Road. It is part of a larger recreation area and contains a combined slide, balanced beam and climbing frame, and swings for older children and swings and a springer for toddlers. There is a full sized football pitch with changing facilities available for hire on this site.</p></div>
 </div>
 Ida's Road Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Idas Road, Castle Hill, Eckington, S21 4DT</p>
The Ida's Road play area is at the top of Ida's Road on Castle Hill. It is adjacent to a field and surrounded by a metal fence. It contains an activity set for older children which includes a climbing wall, three-way seesaw, climbing frame, steps and climbing bars. It also has swings for older children and toddlers and two toddlers' springers.</p></div>
 </div>
 Main Road, Ridgeway Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Main Road, Ridgeway, S13 3XR</p>
The Ridgeway play area is on Main Road, opposite the Junior School. It contains timber framed swings, combined slide and climbing frame, and a variety of timber climbing and balancing equipment for older children. The area is part of the Recreation and Sports ground but is surrounded by a wall.</p></div>
 </div>
 Warren Crescent Play Area </p>
</li>
 </div>
Warren Crescent, Marsh Lane, S21 5RW</p>
The Warren Crescent play area is at Marsh Lane - near the junction of Warren Crescent and Ridge Road. It is marked by a Public Footpath sign. It contains a combined slide and climbing frame and swings for older children, seesaw and swings and springers for toddlers. It is on the very edge of the village and is surrounded by hedges.</p></div>
 </div>
 Stead Street Park and Café </p>
</li>
 </div>
Stead street, S21 4FY</p>
Opened in July 2025. It includes large green area, equipment for older children such as climbing frame, climbing tower, zip line, slide, 4-seater swing, spider web. There is dedicated area for smaller children with large sand pit (play zone in sand pit) and bike/scooter track. Other play equipment include swings, double swings, playing house and more.</p>
The park include facilities such as Bluebell Café (toilettes, baby-changing facilities inside) and free car park on-site. Dogs are allowed in the park and Café.</p></div>
