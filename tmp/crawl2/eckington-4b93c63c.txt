Ash Crescent Play Area</h2>
Address: Ash Crescent, Birk Hill, Eckington, S21 4AB</p>
Ash Crescent play area can be accessed from Larch Road or between Nos 63 and 65 Ash Crescent. It has a combined slide, balance beam, traverse wall, rope walk, climbing frame for older children and four springers and swings for toddlers. It is a self-contained area surrounded by fences and hedges.</p></div>
