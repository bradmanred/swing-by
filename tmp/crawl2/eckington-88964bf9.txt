The Ida's Road play area is at the top of Ida's Road on Castle Hill. It is adjacent to a field and surrounded by a metal fence. It contains an activity set for older children which includes a climbing wall, climbing frame, steps and climbing bars. It also has swings for older children and toddlers and two toddlers' springers.</p></div>
