Parking on the road</h1>
Parking on the road, also known as on-street parking is run by the  North Essex Parking Partnership (NEPP)  in Epping Forest.</p>
 North Essex Parking Partnership (NEPP) – Find out about parking on the road </div>
</div>
NEPP is responsible for:</p>
On-street Civil Enforcement Officers, also known as traffic wardens</li>
The enforcement process, including challenges to and payments of parking fines</li>
Administration of parking restrictions and management of permit schemes</li>
Pay for parking (on-street)</h2>
You can pay for parking on-street via MiPermit cashless parking, telephone, SMS, or the MiPermit smartphone app. MiPermit digital payments are ticketless. You do not need to display anything when you park.</p>
 Visit the NEPP MiPermit portal </li>
