Getting to North Weald Airfield</h2>
By road</h3>
Junction 7 on the M11 motorway is 2 miles away and so road access to the M25 and north east London is excellent. Chelmsford is less than 20 miles to the east and Harlow a short distance to the west. Immediate access to the airfield is via the A414, Merlin Way and access through Gate B1.</p>
 North Weald Airfield layout (pdf 434KB) </li>
By rail</h3>
The nearest railway stations are in Harlow, with London Underground’s Central Line terminating 2 miles away in Epping.</p>
 Plan your journey using the Travel Essex website </li>
By air</h3>
North Weald Airfield is an unlicensed aerodrome. Stapleford Aerodrome is the nearest licensed site and is 7 miles away. London Stansted Airport on the M11 is 14 miles to the north. A location plan has been prepared. Copies are available from the operations team upon request.</p>
