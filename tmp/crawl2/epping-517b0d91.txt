West Essex Inclusion Project</h1>
The West Essex Inclusion Project offers short breaks sessions and activities in Epping Forest, Harlow and Uttlesford to children up to 14 with a learning disability, autism and a physical or sensory impairment.</p>
Booking an activity</h2>
All activities must be booked in advance.</p>
Before booking activities, all children must:</p>
Hold a valid Essex Short Breaks ID  – www.shortbreaks.essex.gov.uk for any queries regarding short breaks contact [email protected] </li>
Be registered with the West Essex Inclusion Project (please contact our Disability Inclusion Officers to gain a registration form, contact information below)</li>
Go to our  Activities and events page  and enter  West Essex Inclusion Project  in the search box.</p>
When booking, it is important you give as much detail you can around your child and their needs to help us to make each session more enjoyable for all participants. If your child requires any specialist healthcare support, email our Disability inclusion Officers who will schedule a meeting with you to discuss your child’s needs.</p>
How we can help</h2>
If you have any questions or are unsure of anything, get in touch. Email our Disability Inclusion Officers with any questions you may have:</p>
