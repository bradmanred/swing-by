Electric vehicles</h2>
Car park charges apply in electric vehicle bays. Electric vehicle owners must buy a valid ticket during their stay.</p>
Electric vehicles must be connected to and using the electric charging point to stay in an electric vehicle bay. Only electric vehicles can park park in electric vehicle bays.</p>
On-street parking</h2>
On-street parking in the Epping Forest district is the responsibility of the  North Essex Parking Partnership (NEPP) . Epping Forest District Council is only responsible for its pay and display car parks.</p>
You can view NEPP’s existing traffic regulation orders and contact them for all on-street parking enquiries on the  North Essex Parking Partnership website .</p>
