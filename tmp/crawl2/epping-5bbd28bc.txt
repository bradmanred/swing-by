Treasure Trails</h1>
Whether you’re exploring somewhere new or searching for hidden treasure near you, turn a walk into an adventure with a themed treasure hunt! Discover Treasure Trails in the Epping Forest district and beyond!</p>
What is a Treasure Trail?</h2>
A Treasure Trail is a fun and exciting way to get exploring outside. There are treasure hunts, detective mysteries and spy missions in towns, villages, and cities all over the UK.</p>
All Trails are self-guided, with easy to follow directions, so there’s no need to book, you can set off on your adventure whenever you like.</p>
Who is it for?</h2>
Everyone! Treasure Trails are fun for children and adults of all ages. There are clues of all difficulties to solve, so it’s as much fun for the parents as the little ones.</p>
With fun treasure, detective and spy themes, you will be immersed in the backstory. Can you work together to find the hidden treasure, solve the mystery or complete your mission?</p>
