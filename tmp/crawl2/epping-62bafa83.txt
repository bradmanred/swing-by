Appeal your parking fine</h1>
If you feel your parking fine or penalty charge notice (PCN) was incorrectly issued, or that there are special circumstances in your case, you can appeal or challenge your fine.</p>
 Do not pay the penalty charge if you plan to appeal your parking fine . This will close the case and you cannot appeal, as per the  traffic penalty tribunal guidance .</p>
How to appeal a parking fine</h2>
You can appeal your parking fine online or in writing.</p>
Appeal online</h3>
Appeal your parking fine online by selecting where you got the ticket from.</p>
 Appeal your parking fine from our pay and display car parks (starts with EP or FT) </div>
 Appeal your parking fine from parking on the road (starts with II) </div>
</div>
 North Essex Parking Partnership (NEPP)  are responsible for on-street parking in Epping Forest, including enforcement.</p>
Appeal in writing</h3>
You can appeal your parking fine by writing to us at:</p>
Epping Forest District Council, Leisure and Parking Team, High Street, Epping, CM16 4BZ</li>
Appealing a parking fine</h2>
There are 2 stages to appealing or challenging a parking fine:</p>
Informal challenge</li>
Formal representation</li>
Informal challenge</h3>
An informal challenge can be made within 28 days of the date on which your parking fine was issued.</p>
If you make an informal challenge within 14 days of the date on which your parking fine was issued and your challenge is rejected, the discount period will be reset for a further period of 14 days from the date of the rejection letter.</p>
Formal representation</h3>
A formal representation can be made after a  notice to owner  has been served to the registered owner of the vehicle. This requires a web code which can be found on the notice to owner.</p>
If the web code is not recognised call us on 01992 564000 for assistance.</p>
