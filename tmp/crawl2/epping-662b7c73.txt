Play in the Park</h1>
Play in the Park is a free inclusive children’s play session all about playing games and having fun outdoors. No need to book, just drop in at any time.</p>
About Play in the Park</h2>
 Our Play in the Park sessions are 100% free with no booking required. This makes them a highly affordable, blessing-in-disguise activity for parents and grandparents during expensive school holidays.</p>
We aim to keep children engaged and active during school holidays. The sessions are fantastic for local families due to its accessibility, variety, and community feel. This is a great opportunity for parents and carers to socialise with other families and even bring a picnic while the children safely play.</p>
We bring a wide range of sports equipment and arts and crafts resources including tennis, basketball, football, colouring, mask-making, and glitter.</p>
Many sessions feature the Dr Bike team, who offer free check-ups and minor repairs for children’s bicycles . These can take up to 30 minutes and must be booked in advance.</p>
