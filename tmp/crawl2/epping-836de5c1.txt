Epping Forest district walks</h1>
Walking is a low-impact exercise that’s accessible, affordable, and can be included in daily routines.</p>
Walking offers numerous benefits for physical and mental well-being:</p>
Improved cardiovascular health</li>
Weight management</li>
Stress reduction</li>
Enhanced mood and cognitive function</li>
Essex walks</h2>
The St Peter’s Way is a 45 mile walk meandering through the countryside of Essex, from Chipping Ongar to the ancient chapel of St Peter-on-the-Wall at Bradwell on Sea.</p>
The Essex Way is a long-distance path stretching from Epping to Harwich, a distance of 81 miles.</p>
The Forest Way is a 25-mile walk following the footpaths and ancient green lanes between the unique forests of Epping and Hatfield.</p>
Find details on the Essex County Council website – Essex walks .</p>
Essex short walks</h2>
Picturesque short walks that vary in distance but are usually around 4 miles.</p>
Epping and Theydon Bois – distance 4.8 miles</li>
Find details on the Essex County Council website – Essex walks .</p>
Walking in Epping Forest</h2>
The City of London Corporation owns Epping Forest. They have waymarked trails to help you get out and about in the Forest.</p>
Chestnut trail – distance 3.25 miles – time 2 hours</li>
Gifford trail – distance 1.25 miles – time 1 hour</li>
Golden Hill Trail – distance 1.6 miles – time 30 minutes</li>
Holly trail – distance 2.5 miles – time 1 hour</li>
Hornbeam trail – distance 3.5 miles – time 1.5 hours</li>
Lime trail – distance 1.5 miles – time 30 to 40 minutes</li>
Oak trail – distance 6.6 miles – time 3 to 4 hours</li>
Rowan trail – distance 1.5 miles – time 45 to 60 minutes</li>
Find details on the City of London website – Walking and running in Epping Forest .</p>
London LOOP</h2>
The London Outer Orbital Path, or London LOOP, almost completely circles Greater London. 150 miles are split into 24 bite-size walks.</p>
Taking the London LOOP is a great way to get to know London better. It is made up of flat or gentle sloping sections that cover beautiful open spaces like Hainault Forest Country Park. 2 sections go through the Epping Forest district:</p>
Section 19 – Chingford to Chigwell</li>
Section 20 – Chigwell to Havering-atte Bower</li>
Find details on the Transport for London website – London LOOP .</p>
