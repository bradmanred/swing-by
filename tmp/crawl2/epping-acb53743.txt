Book an MOT</h1>
We MOT cars and small vans up to 3.5 tonnes. This includes:</p>
Servicing</li>
Repairs</li>
MOT failures</li>
Tyres</li>
Diagnostics</li>
 Book an MOT </div>
</div>
You need to book a timeslot for your MOT online, before you arrive at the test centre. Payment will be taken at the time of the test by credit or debit card.</p>
You can book Monday to Friday 8am to 5pm and Saturday 8am to 12 noon, excluding bank holidays.</p>
Free retests</h2>
It is your responsibility to ensure the repairs are carried out and a retest booked. Retests are free providing:</p>
The original test was taken with us</li>
The retest is in 10 working days of the original test</li>
