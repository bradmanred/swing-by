Event organisers</h1>
North Weald Airfield is a busy and active airfield.</p>
Every year it hosts a wide range of successful events including:</p>
Air shows</li>
Antique fairs</li>
Bus rallies</li>
Charity events</li>
Classic and sports car shows</li>
Cycle racing</li>
Drag racing</li>
Fly-ins</li>
Garden shows</li>
Model shows</li>
Road safety and training</li>
Trade events</li>
