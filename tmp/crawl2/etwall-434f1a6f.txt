Welcome to our Parish Council</h1>
Etwall is a small village in Derbyshire, midway between Derby and Burton upon Trent.</p>
Etwall is noted for its historic Almshouses, its Annual Well Dressing Festival and John Port Spencer Academy. John Port is the largest Secondary school in Derbyshire and serves neighbouring villages as well as Etwall itself.</p>
For residents, or those aiming to move to the area, these pages should be able to provide you with some historical background to the area as well as local facilities and contacts for all the groups and activities relevant to the village of Etwall plus important links to the villages and services in our immediate area.</p>
You can also find us on Facebook and the latest news posted to there is also shown on these web pages, to the right (or below) this text.</p>
Etwall Parish Council currently have a vacancy for a 3 Parish Councillors, if you interested please contact the Clerk, Rachel who will be able to provide you with more information.</p></div>
