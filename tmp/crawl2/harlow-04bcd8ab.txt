 Skate park 
</h1>
The Harlow Council skate park plays host to the local community of skaters, scooter pushers, roller bladers and BMX riders.</p>
The 650sq metre park opened in 2008 and was designed by Freestyle Skateparks - with significant input from the local skateboarding community.</p>
The park features:</p>
quarter pipes</li>
banks</li>
hips</li>
blocks</li>
rails</li>
bowl</li>
It is free to use and open all year round.</p></div>
