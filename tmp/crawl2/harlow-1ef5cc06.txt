 Marshes and meadows 
</h1>
Harlow’s marshes and meadows support the rich diversity of wildlife found around the town and provide a peaceful retreat for residents.</p>
Together, Marshgate Spring, Maymead Marsh and Parndon Moat Marsh make up the Stort Valley Meadows.</p></div>
 Hawkenbury Meadow </div>
Long strip of neutral grassland that runs alongside a brook next to Katherine's Way.</div>
 </div>
 Maymead Marsh </div>
Features a large pond with reed beds, marshland and a nesting island in the centre.</div>
 </div>
 Marshgate Spring </div>
A mosaic of marshland and woodland habitats which support a wide variety of wildlife. </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Parndon Moat Marsh </div>
A Scheduled Ancient Monument which has become a haven for wetland birds.</div>
