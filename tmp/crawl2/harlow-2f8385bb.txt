About</h2>
Recorded as a woodland for over 900 years, Parndon Wood is composed of a canopy of oak with coppiced hornbeam growing beneath.</p>
The reserve is a Special Site for Scientific Interest (SSSI) due to its variety of flora and has received a Green Flag Award, which recognises the best parks in the country, for the last 11 years.</p>
Visitors can follow the 1.5km trail, or simply explore the wood’s many tracks and paths. There are three observation hides, where you can wait quietly for a glimpse of a green woodpecker or Muntjac deer.</p>
In the accessible conservation centre, learn about the local wildlife from the classroom full of woodland specimens, taxidermy and information posters.</p>
Dogs are welcome up to the picnic table seating area outside the centre, but must be kept on a lead at all times. Dogs are not allowed on the woodland trails.</p>
