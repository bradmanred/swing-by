 Parking problems 
</h1>
On this page, you can find information on common parking problems:</p>
 Commercial vehicles </li>
 Parking on pavements </li>
 Blocked driveway </li>
 Blocked dropped kerb </li>
 Cars for sale </li>
Commercial vehicles</h2>
There are no restrictions on commercial vehicles parking in residential areas, so we cannot take any action against them.</p>
In permitted areas, the commercial vehicle must display a valid permit.</p>
We recommend you speak to the owner of the vehicle to see if you can agree on a better parking arrangement.</p>
Parking on pavements</h2>
If a vehicle has all 4 wheels on the pavement, you can report this to Essex Police on 101.</p>
Blocked driveway</h2>
If a parked car is blocking your driveway you can report this to the North Essex Parking Partnership and Essex Police on 101.</p>
If you report the blocked driveway and more than 50% of the vehicle is parked across the driveway, we can issue a Penalty Charge Notice to the vehicle. However, we cannot remove the vehicle itself.</p>
Blocked dropped kerb</h2>
If a vehicle is obstructing a dropped kerb you can report this to the North Essex Parking Partnership with the vehicle registration number.</p>
Cars for sale</h2>
There is no law to prevent private individuals from selling their own car.</p>
If a company has left two or more cars for sale on the road within 500 metres of each other, you can report this to us and we can take action against them.</p></div>
