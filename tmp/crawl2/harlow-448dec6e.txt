 Residential or visitor parking permit 
</h1>
The North Essex Parking Partnership (NEPP) is responsible for residential parking - including visitor parking.</p>
To park in residential permit parking areas, you need a permit. If you don't display a valid permit, you may receive a penalty charge notice (fine).</p>
 Resident permits </h2>
To apply for a resident permit, you need to:</p>
 apply for a resident permit on the NEPP website </li>
pay the correct fee</li>
provide your vehicle registration number</li>
You will receive a virtual permit. You do not need to display anything in your vehicle. </p>
 Visitor permits </h2>
To apply for a visitor permit, you need to:</p>
 apply for a visitor permit on the NEPP website </li>
pay the correct fee</li>
provide your vehicle registration number</li>
You will receive a virtual permit. You do not need to display anything in your vehicle. </p></div>
