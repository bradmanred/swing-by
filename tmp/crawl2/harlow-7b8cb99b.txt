 Traffic orders 
</h1>
A traffic order is a legal document that helps manage traffic flow, speed limits and where you can park.</p>
A traffic order is needed for:</p>
parking places</li>
waiting, loading and unloading bays</li>
yellow lines</li>
one-way streets</li>
prohibitions of vehicles</li>
vehicle weight restrictions</li>
bus lanes</li>
cycle lanes</li>

You can also find details of traffic orders affecting the Harlow area on the North Essex Parking Partnership website .</p>
We advertise and consult on all traffic orders. Anyone can object to, or support, any of the proposed orders.</p>
You must submit your comments before the end of the consultation period. Details on how you can submit your comments and the deadline are included in each order.</p>
At the end of the consultation period, we will consider all feedback before making a decision on whether to implement the order.</p>
The proposed traffic order goes through three stages:</p>
Notice of intention -  the formal announcement of the proposed traffic order and the consultation closing date.</li>
Notice of making - the formal announcement that the traffic order will go ahead, which we produce after the consultation if there are no changes required.</li>
Order - this provides details of the traffic order and restrictions, as well as the date the order will come into effect.</li>
