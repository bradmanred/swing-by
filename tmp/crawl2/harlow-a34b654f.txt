 Paddling pools 
</h1>
We have 4 free-to-use outdoor paddling pools and 2 splash parks in Harlow:</p> 
Bush Fair paddling pool</li>
Norman Booth paddling pool</li>
Potter Street splash park</li>
Staple Tye splash park</li>
Sumners paddling pool</li>
Town Park paddling pool</li> 
These are open daily from 10am to 6pm during the summer school holidays. The paddling pools and splash parks are now closed for the season.</p></div>

Safety</h2>
We do not have lifeguards at the pools. We usually have staff on duty to monitor the water quality and report any problems. </p>
Our staff are trained how to deal with an emergency situation but this does not include administering first aid.   Staff have access to a small supply of plasters and antiseptic wipes only, which can be given to the parent of the child to administer.</p>
We have to drain the pools from time to time to make sure they are kept clean. </p>
Please make sure any children under 8 are accompanied by an adult.</p>
You cannot take bikes, dogs or glass into the pool area. </p></div>
