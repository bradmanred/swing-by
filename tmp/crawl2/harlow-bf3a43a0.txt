 Playgrounds 
</h1>
We own and maintain 43 unsupervised playgrounds in Harlow.</p>
Many of our playgrounds are located close to housing estates making it easy for children to access and enjoy.</p></div>
 </picture>
</div>
</div>
</div>
 </div>
</div>
Our largest playground is the adventure playground, in Harlow Town Park, which includes a zip line and giant climbing net.</p>
There is also an inclusive play area in the Town Park, near Pets' Corner, which provides accessible equipment for all children, including a wheelchair accessible suspension bridge and a sensory path.</p>
There are 6 multiuse games areas in Harlow – perfect for a game of tennis or basketball. These are located in:</p>
Blackbush Spring (includes tennis)</li>
Bush Fair (includes tennis)</li>
The Dashes (includes tennis)</li>
Fold Croft</li>
Northbrooks</li>
Staple Tye</li>
There are also tennis courts at Church Langley Playing Fields, which are free but must be booked online .</p></div>

Safety</h2>
The safety and cleanliness of the play areas are checked at least once a week.</p>
The working and safety of the equipment are checked once a month by us, and once a year by independent inspection.</p></div>
