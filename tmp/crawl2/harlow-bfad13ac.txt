 Harlow sculpture trail 
</h1>
Harlow is known for its sculpture collection and became the world’s first ‘Sculpture Town’ in 2010.</p>
The collection includes over 100 public sculptures with works by Henry Moore, Ralph Brown, Elisabeth Frink, William Mitchell, Barbara Hepworth, Auguste Rodin and Nick Hornby.</p>
You can view more information on Harlow's sculptures on the Sculpture Town website, including a map and guide.</p>
For any more information about the sculpture collection, including tours and talks, please email gallery@harlowarttrust.org.uk </p></div>
