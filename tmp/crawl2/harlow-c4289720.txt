 Parks and green spaces 
</h1>
 Town Park </div>
One of the largest urban parks in the country, Harlow Town Park covers over 160 acres of beautiful green space.</div>
 </div>
 Parndon Wood </div>
Parndon Wood Nature Reserve is an area of ancient woodland home to a huge range of birds, small mammals, deer, flora and fungi.</div>
 </div>
 Jean McAlpine Park </div>
Located along Elizabeth Way, the park provides a peaceful escape from the town centre.</div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Skate park </div>
Free to use and open all year round, the skate park is home to skaters, scooter pushers, roller bladers and BMX riders.</div>
 </div>
 Marshes and meadows </div>
Includes Hawkenbury meadow, Maymead marsh, Marshgate Spring and Parndon Moat marsh.</div>
 </div>
 Memorial benches and trees </div>
If you would like to remember a loved one, you can apply for a memorial in Harlow Town Park.</div>
