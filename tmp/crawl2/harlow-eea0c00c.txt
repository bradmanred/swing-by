 Memorial benches and trees 
</h1>
If you would like to remember a loved one, you can apply for a memorial in Harlow Town Park.</p>
There are three types of memorials allowed in the Town Park:</p>
memorial benches</li>
memorial plaques attached to existing benches</li>
memorial trees</li>
Apply</h2>
Before applying for a memorial you need to read through our  Town Park memorial policy .</p>
Once you have read through the policy and decided which memorial you would like, you need to complete a  Harlow Town Park memorial application form  (pdf)</p>
Once we receive your application, we will get in touch with you to approve it and finalise any details. </p>
We normally install benches and plaques within 12 weeks of approving your application.</p>
We normally only plant trees between November and March, to give them the best chance of survival. </p>
Fees</h2>
You should look at the memorial policy for full details of fees and what they include but below is a guide to what you can expect to pay: </p>
memorial bench (1800 Cavendish seat): £1,650</li>
individual bench memorial plaque (being the only plaque on the bench): £750</li>
shared bench memorial plaque (on a bench with up to 3 other plaques): £250</li>
memorial trees: £190</li>
The charges include the engraving or inscription, installation and maintenance.</p></div>
