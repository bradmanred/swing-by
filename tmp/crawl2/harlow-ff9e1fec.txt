Front garden parking</h2>
Front garden parking forms part of the restrictive covenants that apply to most of the properties in Harlow.</p>
If your property has covenants controlled by Harlow Council and you want to change your garden to a paved area so that you can park your car, you need to get permission from us first.</p>
If you are unsure whether there are any covenants on your property, inspect the deeds or seek legal advice.</p>
To be able to park on your front garden, you need:</p> 
direct access to the front garden from the road or public highway</li> 
Your parking space needs to:</p> 
be built to a standard size of 5.5m x 2.9m</li>
not take up more than 75% of the total area of your garden</li>
allow for appropriate drainage of surface water</li>
not affect any sight lines</li> 
We will not allow any vehicle crossing where the depth of the parking space is less than 5 metres, so please do not apply as we will not refund your application fee.</p>
For more information on the criteria your front garden needs to meet, read our open front parking policy .</p>
Apply</h3>
To apply for open front parking, you need to:</p> 
complete the  open front parking application form  (pdf)</li>
send the application form to Open Front Parking team by email or post</li>
include a drawing of your front garden, with the measurements of the garden and area you plan to pave</li>
pay the £102 fee (non-refundable)</li>
get signatures of consent from 2 neighbours</li> 
Once we receive your application, we will process it within 5 to 8 weeks.</p>
Once we have given permission, we will pass the details on to  Essex County Council Highways  for any dropped kerb work. You will need to make a separate application with Essex Highways for this.</p>
You must pay the cost of dropping the kerb and making alterations to the pavement.</p>
If your application is unsuccessful, we will not refund your fee, but you can appeal against the decision in writing to Open Front Parking.</p></div>
