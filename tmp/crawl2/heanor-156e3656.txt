 Shipley Country Park and Visitor Centre are set within 700 acres of rolling hills, wildflower meadows and tranquil lakes teeming with wildlife. The park is a valued local asset, offering residents and visitors alike the opportunity to enjoy a wide range of scenic walks and outdoor activities right on our doorstep. It is also home to the Young People’s Forest, an important initiative supporting environmental sustainability and community engagement.   </div></div>
