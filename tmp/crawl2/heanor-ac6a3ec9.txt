In July 2010 the placing of half pit Winding Wheels were installed at Church Street, Heanor and High Street Loscoe. The Town Council placed these Winding Wheels as a lasting tribute to the sacrifice of local miners in helping the area develop and in recognition of the mining community.  </div>
 </div>
The first Winding Wheel is located on Mansfield Road, Heanor and the second next to St Luke's Church at Loscoe. </div></div>
