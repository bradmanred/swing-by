 Floral Displays   
 </h2>
</div>
 </div>
The Town Council provides summer planting in Heanor, Loscoe and Marlpool. </div>
 </div>
If you are interested in sponsoring a basket then please contact the Town Council office. For the year 2026, the cost per hanging basket is £95, which covers the cost/installation of the planted hanging basket, the required lamp column testing, feeding/watering the baskets and the sponsorship plaque. </div></div>

 Hands Road Play Area   
 </h2>
</div>
 </div>
Heanor & Loscoe Town Council funded improvement works to the existing play area on Hands Road, Heanor at a cost of £30,000.00. This money was derived from the sale of land and remaining balances. The play area is owned and maintained by Amber Valley Borough Council. Should you wish to report any problems with the play equipment please contact Amber Valley Borough Council on 01773 841 320 or email envadmin@ambervalley.gov.uk  </div></div>

 Multi Use Games Areas   Lockton Avenue & Charles Hill   
 </h2>
</div>
 </div>
Heanor & Loscoe Town Council provided the biggest single funding contribution which enabled a Multi Use Games Area to be provided on Lockton Avenue playing fields. </div>
 </div>
The Multi Use Games Area was provided following a survey of residents. The Town Council has also supported the funding of a similar facility on Charles Hill Playing Fields. </div>
 </div>
Both Multi Use Games Areas are owned and maintained by Amber Valley Borough Council. Should you wish to report any problems with the Games Areas please contact Amber Valley Borough Council on 01773 841 320 or email envadmin@ambervalley.gov.uk  </div></div>

 World War 1 Commemorative Benches   
 </h2>
</div>
 </div>
Heanor & Loscoe Town Council purchased two World War 1 memorial benches to commemorate 100 years since the end of World War 1. One bench has been placed in Heanor Memorial Park opposite the War Memorial and the second bench has been placed on the Red River Local Nature Reserve, Loscoe. The benches are a place for people to spend time to reflect, remember and give thanks. </div></div>

 Pentrich Revolution Information Board   
 </h2>
</div>
 </div>
Heanor & Loscoe Town Council financially supported along with Councillors Celia Cox and Paul Jones, the provision of an information board, which is sited on the Old Fire Station on Wilmot Street in Heanor. </div>
 </div>
The information board provides details of the Pentrich Revolution, together with a walk around Heanor, identifying landmarks related to the event. The walk is part of 19 walks around the areas supporting the commemoration. </div></div>

 Heart Defibrillators   
 </h2>

 </div>
