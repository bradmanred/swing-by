 The Town Council were pleased to receive from Heanor Floral Group, a defibrillator for public use, which is sited on the exterior wall of The Old Fire Station, Heanor Market Place.   </div></div>
 </div>
 Since the installation of the first defibrillator, Heanor & Loscoe Town Council have since funded and installed an additional heart defibrillator at Heanor Baptist Church.  </div>
 </div>
 The heart defibrillators which are owned by the Town Council can be found at The Old Fire Station, Wilmot Street, Heanor, Derbyshire, DE75 7EF and Heanor Baptist Church, 117 Derby Road, Heanor, Derbyshire, DE75 7QL.  </div>
