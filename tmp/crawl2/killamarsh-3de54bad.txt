PRESS RELEASE - New playground opens in Killamarsh</h1>
 </div>
New playground opens in Killamarsh ready for the summer holidays.</p>
Families in Killamarsh have something new to celebrate this summer, as the brand-new playground at High Moor has now been completed and is ready for local children to enjoy throughout the rest of the school summer holidays.</p>
The exciting new play area provides a modern, safe and inclusive space where children can play, explore and make lasting memories. The project represents a significant investment in the local community and has transformed the site into an attractive recreational space for families.</p>
In addition to the completion of the playground, permission has been granted by the Cabinet Office for the park to be officially named King Charles III Community Park. The name was chosen to celebrate King Charles ascension to the throne and a newly crowned 21st century monarch, and also recognises His Majesty's commitment to communities and the environment. An official opening ceremony will be arranged at a future date to celebrate both the completion of the playground and the park's new name. Further details will be announced in due course.</p>
The playground has been made possible thanks to grant funding from FCC Communities Foundation, a not-for-profit organisation that awards grants for community projects through the Landfill Communities Fund. Their support has enabled the delivery of a high-quality facility that will benefit local residents for many years to come.</p>
Councillor Steve Clough, Chair of Killamarsh Parish Council said:</p>
"We are delighted to see this fantastic new playground completed in time for the summer holidays. It provides a wonderful space for local children to play, stay active and enjoy the outdoors, while giving families another great place to spend time together. We are extremely grateful to FCC Communities Foundation for their generous support in making this project a reality. We also look forward to celebrating the official opening of King Charles III Community Park with the community at a future ceremony."</p>
Penny Horne, FCC Communities Foundation grant manager, said: "It's wonderful to see something we have funded finally open and ready to make such a difference to so many families within Killamarsh. FCC Communities Foundation is always happy to consider grant applications for projects that benefit local communities and this is a great example of what can be achieved."</p>
Residents are encouraged to visit the new playground over the summer and enjoy the improved facilities.</p></div>
