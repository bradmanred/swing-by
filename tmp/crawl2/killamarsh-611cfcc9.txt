Parks</h1>
There are a number of parks and play areas throughout the Parish but Killamarsh Parish Council are only responsible for the maintenance at Belklane Park, Norwood and High Moor Birchlands.</p>
All other parks and open spaces are the maintained by North East Derbyshire District Council .</p></div>
The Parish Council has been able to rejuvenate the play area at Norwood in 2023 partially using its reserves and also through a successful grant application for a share of the Governments UK Shared Prosperity Levelling Up fund through NEDDC.</p>
</div>
Skate Park</h3>
 </div>
Killamarsh has one of the largest, free to use, outdoor Skate Parks in the County. It is located at the Killamarsh Active and managed by North East Derbyshire District Council.</p>
North East Derbyshire District Council obtained funding to refurbish the skate park.</p></div>
