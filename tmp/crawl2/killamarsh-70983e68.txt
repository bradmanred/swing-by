Leisure & Culture</h1>
 </div>
Killamarsh is well served by a variety of clubs and societies that provide leisure and cultural activities.</p>
There are a number of specialised clubs such as Killamarsh Kestrels Running Club , Bowls, Knit and Natter, Over 60's Club and Women's Institute to name but a few.</p>
The Young People are also catered for with the usual Scouts and Brownies as well as sports.</p></div>
