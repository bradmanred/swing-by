Armley Park</h1>
Armley Park is two miles west of Leeds city centre and is approximately 14 hectares in area. It's the perfect location for people of all age ranges to enjoy a relaxing day out. The park gives local residents and visitors amazing views over Kirkstall Valley.</p>

Disabled access, buses, parking and toilets</h2>
Disabled access</h3>
Parking is free</li>
The main circular route around the park is well maintained and fairly flat</li>
Buses</h3>
Buses to and from Leeds:
 The 72, X6 and X11, all run regularly.</p>
Parking</h3>

Toilets</h3>
The nearest toilets, located in the Golf Club at Gotts mansion, in the next park, are open to the public to use.</p>
Events</h2>
There will be community events throughout the year, please see the notice board in the park, The Wade’s Ranger and the Friends of Armley and Gotts Park Facebook page. .</p>
Volunteering</h2>
The Friends of Armley parks-and-countryside Gotts Park meet twice a month to do work to improve the area, help with gardening, nature conservation and generally look after the park:</p>
every second Sunday for a practical activity/social (families welcome)</li>
every fourth Thursday for a practical task</li>
New members are always welcome, for further information contact us or email countrysiderangers@leeds.gov.uk .</p>
