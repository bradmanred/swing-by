Pudsey Park </h1>
Pudsey Park has a Green Flag award for excellence, and one of the most visited parks in Leeds.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </p>
Location
</h2>
The park is five miles west of the city centre. The main entrance is at Church Lane, Pudsey, Leeds, LS28 7RR.</p>
 Get directions on Google Maps .
</p>
Parking</h3>
Although there are no car parking facilities within the park, there is roadside parking on the streets nearby. Please park carefully and with consideration for local residents.</p>
Buses</h3>
Visit West Yorkshire Metro to find bus information.</p>
