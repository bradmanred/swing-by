Vehicle clamping and removal</h1>
The 
 Traffic Management Act 2004 allows the council to clamp and/or remove vehicles in certain circumstances.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Removal costs
</h2>
If we have removed your vehicle, we will charge you the following to get it back:</p>
penalty charge of £50 or £70 depending on the offence (we offer a 50% discount if you pay within 14 days)
</li>
release charge of £105</li>
storage costs of £12 for every 24 hours
</li>
What to do if your vehicle has been clamped or removed</h2>
If we have clamped or removed your vehicle, contact parking services .</p>
You will be asked to provide proof of ownership/insurance and proof of address before the vehicle is released.​​</p>
