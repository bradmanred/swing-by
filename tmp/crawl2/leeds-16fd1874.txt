Report a lost, stolen or misused blue badge</h1>
Request a badge to replace one that’s been lost, stolen or damaged, or report misuse.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Report a lost or stolen badge</h2>
If you don't know where you lost your badge or think it may have been stolen you must let West Yorkshire Police know by calling 101. You will be given a lost property or crime reference number. If you know you lost your badge at home then you don’t need to let the police know.</p>
You can then report the missing badge by contacting the blue badge team.</p>
You will be charged £5 for a replacement badge.</p>
If your lost or stolen blue badge was due to expire within the next three months you should re-apply for a new badge instead of reporting it online.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Report someone misusing a badge</h2>
If you suspect someone of misusing a Blue Badge please email us.</p>
You can report misuse of a Blue Badge to us in confidence.</p>
We will need to know:</p>
where the misuse occurred</li>
when the use occurred</li>
the Blue Badge number</li>
any further details</li>
