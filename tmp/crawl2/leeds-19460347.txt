Middleton Park</h1>
This green space is over 470 acres and has a cafe, bowling greens, a playground, and a fishing lake.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Location</h2>
Middleton Park is four miles south of the city centre and has several entrances. The address of the main entrance is from Town Street, Middleton, LS10 3SH.</p>
 Get directions on Google Maps </p>
Buses</h3>
Visit  West Yorkshire Metro  to find bus information.</p>
Opening hours</h2>
The visitor centre, cafe and toilet are open from 10.15am to 2.15pm Monday to Friday, and 9am to 3pm on Saturday and Sunday.</p>
Attractions and facilities</h2>
View a  map of the park (JPG, 2 MB) .</p>
The park has:</p>
a cafe</li>

Disabled access</h2>
There are tarmac paths around the parkland that are accessible for wheelchair users. </p>
There is an accessible toilet at the visitor centre.</p>
Events</h2>
There are lots of community events throughout the year, including the community bonfire. </p>
Volunteering</h2>
The Middleton Park Estate Volunteers meet every Thursday between 10am and 3pm. They improve the park through gardening, habitat management and general estate work. New volunteers are always welcome, contact the Middleton Park Estate Officer.</p>
The  Friends of Middleton Park  promote local interest and involvement in the park and its woodland. The group organizes popular community events throughout the year and often opens the cafe at the weekend. New members are very welcome.</p>
Corporate volunteering opportunities are also available at this park for groups and organisations interested in holding a team building day with a difference. For more information please contact the Estate Officer.</p>
