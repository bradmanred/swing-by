LNRs are sites of special interest within the Leeds district for nature conservation, the study or enjoyment of wildlife, geology or landforms. They are a statutory designation operated by the council in consultation with Natural England, the Government’s agency for nature conservation.</p>
</div>
Habitats range from ancient woodland to conifer plantations; from wetland to limestone meadow and from open heathland to river valley.</p>
