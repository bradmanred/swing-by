Cycling strategy</h1>
The Leeds Cycling Starts Here Strategy represents a city-wide partnership approach to developing and delivering improved opportunities for everyone to cycle.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Our vision is:</p>
To inspire more people to cycle more often.</p>
Our aims:</p>
Make cycling a natural everyday choice</li>
Improve safety, convenience for cycling and health and wellbeing across the city</li>
Improve environmental sustainability, better air quality and reduce pollution of all types</li>
 </div>
This will be contributing towards: </p>
Our Vision to be the Best City in the UK 2030.</p>
Cycling strategy themes</h2>
Develop a thriving and active cycling city</h3> 
Objectives </p>
Enable everyone to participate in cycling activities targeting women, children, BME groups and people with disabilities. </li>
Ensure every school pupil has the opportunity to receive cycle training, and that adults too can access training support. </li>
Support the development and participation in sports cycling at all skill levels. </li>
Support the development of cycle clubs and social cycling groups. </li>
Increase access to cycling with support for bike loan and recycle schemes. </li>
Support cycling focused community initiatives and events. </li>
Support people to become physically active. </li>
Practice integrated transport planning for liveable places that is inclusive of both cycling and walking.</li>
 </div>
Promote a cycle friendly city</h3> 
Promote cycling for transport, sport and recreation. </li>
Promote good road safety behaviours, considerate cycling , and driver awareness. </li>
Increase awareness of the wider benefits of cycling to peoples’ mobility and wellbeing. </li>
Build on the city’s reputation for staging major sporting, cycling and mass participation events. </li>
Develop, promote and support events for young people. </li>
Make effective use of social media and marketing to promote and grow cycling. </li>
Embed a positive cycling and walking culture in all educational establishments.</li>
 </div>
Build a great city for cycling</h3> 
Make the city centre and local centres cycle friendly.</li>
Connect educational establishments to cycle networks.</li>
Embed provision for cycling in the planning system and urban design processes to ensure all future school, workplace and residential developments are cycle friendly.</li>
Provide for cycling in the highways network as part of planned development and maintenance opportunities.</li>
Encourage employers to provide cycling facilities at work.</li>
Complete the Leeds Cycle Network incorporating the six planned Superhighways and including Leeds Orbital Route.</li>
Increase the number of leisure routes for cyclists and open our parks to cyclists.</li>
Support the delivery of Cycle Hubs and Training Centres Citywide. including creating a cycle leisure park.</li>
Support the development of traffic-free and off road cycling circuits.</li>
 </div>
Our collective values</h2>
Our collective values are to:</p>
work together collaboratively to support and develop our volunteers, building strong and effective partnerships to bring new resources and investment into cycling</li>
build our knowledge and understanding of cyclists needs using an evidence based approach to inform our progress, including drawing upon the latest technical guidance and information</li>
celebrate city wide successes, locally, regionally and nationally </li>
be inclusive of all groups, individuals and communities, and promote participation across all groups</li>
How we measure success </h3>
increased number of children cycling to school</li>
increased number of utility and leisure journeys made by bicycle </li>
increased take-up Bikeability places in schools </li>
increased number of schools with good quality cycle storage </li>
increased cycle network routes </li>
increased number of cycle club members </li>
reduced rate of fatalities and serious injuries</li>
 </div>
Our partners</h3> 
The Leeds Cycling Starts Here Strategy has been developed by a partnership board involving of a number of key organisations, as well as a large scale consultation with members of the public and stakeholders. The partners listed below, are fully in support of the putting into practice the Leeds Cycling Starts Here Strategy.</p>
