Public rights of way statutory declarations</h1>
Find statutory declarations made by landowners showing any public rights of way over their land.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Landowners can deposit a statement and map under section 31(6) of the Highways Act 1980 indicating any rights of way over their land and then make a statutory declaration to the effect that no additional rights of way exist.</p>
