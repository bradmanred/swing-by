What is Connecting Leeds </h1>
Connecting Leeds is our ambition to transform travel in Leeds for people who live, work in and visit the city.</p>

In 2016, we started our biggest-ever transport conversation in which over 8,000 people told us what is important to them and for Leeds.</p>
This began with our biggest ever transport conversation, where more than 8,000 people told us what matters most to them and to the future of transport in Leeds.</p>
Since then we have been working with residents to provide safer ways to travel around Leeds, including major upgrades to junctions and roundabouts, providing safe cycle lanes, widening pavements, improving bus reliability through priority measures and creating new public spaces, especially in the city centre.</p>
Leeds transport strategy</h2>
Our transport strategy sets out our vision for Leeds to be a city where you do not need a car, where everyone has an affordable zero carbon choice in how they travel.</p>
Vision Zero 2040</h2>
The Leeds Safer Roads Vision Zero 2040 strategy sets out our ambition to eliminate fatal and serious road injuries on Leeds's roads by 2040.</p>
