Otley Chevin Forest Park</h1>
The forest park overlooks the market town of Otley and is a designated nature reserve</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The park's north-facing cliff, or escarpment, rises steeply to a height of 280m above sea level and offers magnificent views of the Wharfe Valley. In recognition of its wealth of wildlife, including the Green Hairstreak Butterfly and the Woodcock, the whole park was designated as a Local Nature Reserve in 1989.</p>
Dogs are welcome, but must be kept under control at all times. Dogs are allowed off the lead, but only if they know to return immediately when called.
 </p>
Location</h2>
Chevin Forest Park is in the Wharfe Valley, on either side of East Chevin Road, Otley. It is 10 miles north-west of Leeds city centre. The post code is LS21 3DD.</p>
 Get directions on Google Maps

Opening hours</h3>
The cafe is open from 10am to 2pm, Monday to Friday (excluding Tuesdays) and 11am to 3pm at weekends.</p>
Parking at the cafe is for disabled visitors only.</p>

Disabled access</h3>
Surprise View is wheelchair accessible. There is an accessible toilet and changing place at the White House Cafe, available during cafe opening hours.</p>
Volunteering</h3>
The  Friends of Chevin Forest Park  are a group of volunteers who work with Leeds City Council to care for and promote the park. They meet on the second Sunday of each month to maintain footpaths, dry stone walling, and to clear scrub. New members are welcome.</p>
