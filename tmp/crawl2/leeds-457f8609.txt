Calverley Victoria Park</h1>
Calverley Victoria Park is 7.5 miles west of Leeds city centre and is approximately 3.8 hectares in area. This is a well-used local park and is the home to Calverley Cricket Club.</p>

Carr Road, Calverley, Pudsey LS28 5RH.</p>

Cricket ground</li>

Disabled access</h2>
A footpath surrounds the park which is accessible to all and suitable for the majority of manual and electric wheelchairs.</p>
