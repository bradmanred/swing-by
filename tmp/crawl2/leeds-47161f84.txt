Report an issue with a road, pavement or cycle lane</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Each issue you report is placed into one of these priority groups:</p>
​urgent - to be attended by the end of the next working day </li>
non-urgent - to be attended within 28 days of being reported</li>
planned works - to be added to a schedule to be repaired at a later date when we are working in the area</li>
</div>
If you are reporting an emergency that poses an immediate danger to people or property (such as a missing manhole cover, a significant road obstruction or a defect related to a recent traffic accident) call our highways emergency number, 0113 222 4407.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Public Liability claims</h3>
If you believe an accident or loss you have suffered is because of actions or negligence by Leeds City Council, you can make a Public Liability claim .</p>

Cannot find a form that matches your problem?</h2>
If you cannot find a relevant topic either on this page or in the Environmental reporting section (which includes blocked drains, overgrown paths and litter), use the Report a general highways problem  form.</p>
</div>
To report issues with one of the City Connect cycle routes contact them directly .</p>
You can also take part in cycling discussions with the Leeds Cycling Consultation Forum.</p>
