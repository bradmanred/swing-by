Great walks in Leeds</h1>
Fantastic rights of way in and around Leeds to explore the green spaces of the city.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Long distance routes</h2>
 Leeds Country Way – a 62 mile circular walking route around Leeds </li>
Trans Pennine Trail – Leeds-Wakefield section. View the Trans Pennine Trail interactive map online </li>
Dales Way Link - a 19 mile feeder route from Leeds to Ilkley. View the Dales Way Link interactive map online </li>
Ebor Way – a 70 mile walk from Helmsley to Ilkley via York. The route passes through the northern part of the Leeds district. View the Ebor Way interactive map online </li>
Shorter routes</h2>
Meanwood Valley Trail – a 7-mile walking route through the Meanwood Valley to Golden acre Park. Find detailed maps and GPS files of the Meanwood Valley Trail on the GPS Cycle and Walking Routes website </li>
Pudsey Link Bridleway – a 6-mile, linear bridleway between Tong village and Apperley Bridge</li>
The Linesway – a 3-mile long disused railway between Garforth and Allerton Bywater</li>
Wetherby Railway Path – a disused railway cycle route linking Wetherby to Spofforth and Thorp arch. View the Wetherby Railway Path interactive map online </li>
Rothwell Greenway – a disused railway route running between Lofthouse, Robin Hood and Rothwell</li>
Wyke Beck Way – a walking and cycling route following the Wyke Beck valley between Temple Newsam and Roundhay Park</li>
Fulneck Loop and Calverley Loop – circular bridleway routes through attractive countryside in Pudsey</li>
Temple Newsam bridle path – a 6-mile circular route around Temple Newsam Estate</li>
