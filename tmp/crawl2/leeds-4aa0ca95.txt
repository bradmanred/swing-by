Banstead Park, Harehills</h1>
Banstead Park is one mile north east of Leeds city centre and is approximately 2 hectares in area. An ideal location for people of all age ranges to enjoy a relaxing day out at this valuable community greenspace location.</p>

Disabled access</h2>
The park is accessible to all and most footpaths are suitable for the majority of manual and electric wheel chairs apart from some sloping paths.</p>
