Visitor parking permits</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Visitor permits are issued to a specific property, and only one visitor permit will be issued per household. You will need to provide proof of address, such as a council tax bill or utility bill which needs to have been issued within the last three months.</p>
Cost</h2>
There is no charge for a visitor permit.</p>
Apply</h2>
</div>
 Apply for a visitor permit online
</div>
Once we receive your application, we will post the permit to the address provided within ten working days.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Multiple visitors</h3>
If more than one vehicle needs to park at your property whilst the zone is in operation, you must ask them to use off-street parking if possible.</p>
Alternatively you can contact us for permission to allow extra vehicles to park for the duration of their visit.</p>
You need to let us know:</p>
the location of the property</li>
dates and times of visit(s)</li>
make and registration number of visiting vehicle(s)</li>
Renew</h2>
You need to renew your permit every year. We will send you a reminder when it is due to expire.</p>
</div>
 Renew a visitor permit online
</div>
Replacement permits</h3>
Replacement permits cost £10.</p>
Lost or damaged permits</h2>
 Contact us  to request a replacement permit.</p>
Stolen permits</h3>
If you are able to provide a crime reference number, you will not be charged.</p>
 Contact us  to request a replacement permit.</p>
