 What is Connecting Leeds 
Connecting Leeds is our ambition to transform travel in Leeds for people who live, work in and visit the city.</p>
</li>
 Bus and park and ride 
Travelling using the bus and park and ride is an important element of our plans for Connecting Leeds, and a crucial way of getting around the city.</p>
</li>
 City centre improvements 
Find out how we're improving transport links in the city centre.</p>

 Cycling strategy and development

</li>
