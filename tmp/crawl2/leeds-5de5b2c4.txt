Annual programme of works</h1>
The Connecting Leeds annual programme of works details the proposed works to be undertaken by Leeds City Council from 2026 to 2027.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The works can be viewed on the interactive map below, where you can search by street, town or area. The programme of works is correct as of 1 April 2026.</p>
 View the interactive map
We aim to deliver the programme as detailed, however, dates may be subject to change. The most up-to date information on roadworks in Leeds can be found on the One.Network website .</p>
The programme of works includes:</p>
 Vision Zero , including safer routes to school schemes</li>
cycling, including extensions to the cycle network</li>
planned highway maintenance schemes</li>
bridges, including essential maintenance schemes</li>
public transport improvement</li>
 flood risk management </li>
street lighting</li>
traffic management</li>
20mph residential zones, including in district centres</li>
