Woodhouse Lane car park</h1>
Five minutes’ walk from First Direct Arena, Leeds General Infirmary, and Leeds City Museum.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Address</h4>
Woodhouse Lane, Leeds, LS2 3AX</p>
 View on Google maps </p>
</tr>
Opening hours</h4>
 Open 24 hours, seven days a week 
</tr>
Spaces</h4>
1240 spaces including 14 blue badge parking spaces available on levels 1 and 7</p>
Electric charging points available on level 3</p>
</tr>
Payment methods</h4>
Pay and Display – cash and card accepted (excludes American Express)</p>
PayByPhone is available at this location. Download the PayByPhone app or call 0330 400 7275</p>
PayByPhone Site ID: 808273</p>
</tr>
Prices</h4>
Charges apply Monday to Sunday (includes bank holidays)</p>
Up to 2 hours: £2.70</p>
Up to 3 hours: £4.90</p>
Up to 4 hours: £7</p>
Up to 6 hours: £8.20</p>
Up to 12 hours: £8.90</p>
Up to 14 hours: £9.20</p>
Up to 24 hours: £16</p>
Up to seven days: £55</p>
First Direct Arena events: £7 (between hours of 6pm and midnight on Arena Events nights only)</p>
</tr>
Height restriction</h4>
 Two metres
