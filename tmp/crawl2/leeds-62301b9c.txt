Gotts Park, Armley</h1>
Gotts Park is three miles west of Leeds city centre and is approximately 30 hectares in area. There is an 18 hole parkland golf course run by The Gotts Park Golf CIC club, who are located within the grade II listed Gotts Mansion.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </p>
The park borders Armley park and is the perfect location for people of all age ranges to enjoy a relaxing day out and gives local residents and visitors amazing views over the Kirkstall Valley.</p>

Armley Ridge Rd, Leeds LS12 2QX.</p>

Disabled access, buses, parking and toilets</h2>
Disabled access</h3>
Parking is free</li>
Certain areas are not suitable for disabled access</li>
Disabled toilets are located at Gotts Mansion</li>
Buses</h3>
The 72, X6 and X11, all run regularly to and from Leeds.</p>
Toilets</h3>
The toilets in the Golf Club at Gotts mansion are open to the public to use.</p>
Events</h2>
There are community events throughout the year, see the notice board in the park, The Wade’s Ranger, and the Friends of Armley and Gotts Park Facebook page .</p>
Volunteering</h2>
The Friends of Armley parks-and-countryside Gotts Park meet twice a month to do work to improve the area, help with gardening, nature conservation and generally look after the park:</p>
every second Sunday for a practical or social activity (families welcome)</li>
every fourth Thursday for a practical task</li>
New members are always welcome, for further information contact us or email countrysiderangers@leeds.gov.uk .</p>
