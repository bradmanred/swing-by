Reporting issues on roads for cyclists and motorcyclists</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The best way to report an issue with a road or cycle path is by using our online forms:</p>
 report a pothole or damaged road surface </li>
 report a road, path or cycle lane that needs sweeping </li>
 report a blocked drain or gully </li>
 report an obstruction or blocked access </li>
 report a problem with signage </li>
For other issues on roads or pavements see the Report an issue with a road or pavement page for more forms.</p>
If you want to report any issues with one of the City Connect routes contact them directly .</p>
You can also take part in cycling discussions with the Leeds Cycling Consultation Forum.</p>
