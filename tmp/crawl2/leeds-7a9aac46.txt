Springhead Park, Rothwell</h1>
Springhead Park is a large public park in Rothwell, Leeds. It has a mix of formal gardens and natural countryside, with a range of options for walking different distances.</p>

Events</h2>
A range of events take place in the park from brass bands to the annual community bonfire. 
</p>
Springhead Park volunteers</h2>
The Springhead park volunteers meet quarterly on weekends to help look after and improve the park and its surrounding wildlife habitats through undertaking activities like planting bulbs, footpath clearance, beck clearance and litter picking. To find out more contact countrysiderangers@leeds.gov.uk .</p>
