LNER Beryl Bikes</h1>
Find out about LNER Beryl, the eco-friendly and affordable public electric bike hire service.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </p>
</div>
How it works</h2>
Unlock – Download the Beryl app .</li>
Ride – Have fun and be safe.</li>
Park – In an LNER Beryl Bay.</li>
Lock – Close the lock to end your ride.</li>
Prices</h2>
LNER Beryl is a fully electric bike and e-scooter fleet that makes conquering the hills of Leeds a breeze. There’s day passes and minute bundles available on the Beryl app; otherwise, pay as you ride is £1 to unlock and 17p per minute for an e-bike, and £1 to unlock and 22p per minute for an e-scooter.</p>
Find the full pricing guide, including extra fees and more information on the Leeds page on the Beryl website .</p>
For safety and insurance purposes, the minimum age for hiring and riding an e-bike within the LNER Beryl Bikes service is 16.</p>
</div>
</div>
Accidents, theft and vandalism</h2>
If you have an accident</h3>
If you are involved in an accident while riding an LNER Beryl Bike, firstly make sure that you and anyone else involved are safe and, if possible, off the road.</p>
Call emergency services on 999 if anyone needs immediate medical attention.</p>
</div>
</div>
 Contact the Beryl team once you are in a safe location and emergency services are at the scene.</p>
Stolen or damaged bike or docking station</h3>
If you discover a vandalised docking station or a damaged or stolen bike, please report this to Beryl’s control room by email, phone or live chat on the app or website. </p>
 If you witness unsafe or illegal behaviour, you can help by reporting it .</p>
 </div>
Background</h3> 
The micromobility operator Beryl provide the public e-bike and e-scooter hire service in Leeds to provide a cost effective and low carbon active travel choice in the city. The scheme was made possible by funding secured by Leeds City Council from the Carbon Mitigation fund. This fund is embedded within the Transforming Cities Fund, part of the West Yorkshire Mayoral devolution deal.</p>
LNER is now the official sponsor of the LNER Beryl scheme, supporting it through to 2027. 85% of all sponsorship income will be directly reinvested into the scheme to enhance service quality and encourage greater use of sustainable transport options.</p>
If you would like a docking station near you, you can suggest locations on the Beryl app .</p>
 </div>
Aims</h3> 
LNER Beryl Bikes are for everyone to travel short distances in a quick, easy and eco-friendly way. The service:</p>
has a network of e-bikes and e-scooters available from docking stations located at close intervals for convenience</li>
offers an accessible and cost-effective travel option</li>
located close to dedicated cycleways that have been built across Leeds, offering safer routes for everyday riding</li>
keeps expanding it's footprint and will continue to grow to meet service demand</li>
consults with communities and key stakeholders before docking stations are created helps ensure the scheme provides the best possible service to users</li>
work closely with accessibility groups to ensure the bikes and docking stations will be available to as many users as possible</li>
following approval from the Department for Transport, both vehicles operate at a maximum speed of 15.5mph. The speed was adopted by the e-scooter fleet in July 2026 to make riding feel more natural alongside people cycling - especially on shared cycle routes</li>
 </div>
Benefits</h3> 
LNER Beryl Bikes is supporting the delivery of the  Connecting Leeds Transport Strategy , to help make Leeds a city where you don't need a car. This includes de-carbonising travel, improving health and wellbeing and promoting accessibility and social mobility along with an affordable, quick and easy transport option.</p>
There are many benefits to the service:</p>
enable people to access the amenities they need, from places of work, education, shopping, and leisure destinations without the need for a car</li>
provide an easier access point compared to conventional bike ownership, especially for larger families or shared households, where cycle storage is limited</li>
support people who want to regain cycling confidence or experience and people who are looking to take up cycling, without the need to purchase a bike ( free cycle training )</li>
provide travel for commuting, with the extra electrical assist power requiring less effort and allowing users to arrive at work quicker, as fresh as when they left the house</li>
be designed for everyday cyclists, with a basket on the front for larger baggage and a rear rack (to support extra baggage) with bungee straps to secure</li>
 </div>
LNER Beryl and app</h2>
Beryl is the UK's leading micromobility company, championing sustainable travel options to help reduce road congestion and improve air quality and public health. As part of a Department for Transport trial, e-scooters have been added to the public hire fleet in Leeds.</p>
E-bikes and e-scooters can be accessed via the Beryl app - available for free download .</p>
The fully docked scheme ensures the bikes are locked in a secure way after use.</p>
Bike street team</h3>
Beryl provide an on-street team to look after the bikes, including general maintenance such as changing the batteries and mechanical servicing.</p>
They are the eyes and ears of the project on the ground, reporting any issues or antisocial behaviour.</p>
No mess or clutter</h3>
Their main role is bike redistribution around the city. The service works within bays and if a bike or e-scooter is not returned to a bay the rider will face extra charges.</p>
Who can hire a Beryl e-scooter?</h2>
To hire and ride a Beryl e-scooter in the UK, you must:</p>
be 18 or older</li>
hold a valid UK driving licence or provisional licence</li>
register via the Beryl app</li>
ride only on cycle lanes or roads where bikes are allowed</li>
