Secure cycle parking</h2>
You can now make use of safe, weatherproof cycle hangars when visiting the city centre. These hangars are provided by our partners Hooplr, hold the highest security rating currently on the market (sold Secure Powered Cycle Diamond), and are operated using an application on your smartphone.</p>
This secure cycle parking operates on a pay-as-you-go basis and starts from just 50p for up to 2 hours. Bluetooth technology enables the hangars to be locked and unlocked without a physical key. There are 9 standard cycle hangars, with 6 parking spaces, across the city centre, and one cargo cycle hangar which can fit 2 adapted cycles or cargo bikes located on Bond Street.</p>
 Find out more by downloading the Hooplr Trips app .</p>
Cycle to work schemes</h2>
Ride to work</h3>
Make a saving on bikes and equipment with Evans Cycles' Ride-to-work scheme .</p>
You can loan a bike for children and families from one of the bike libraries across Leeds to help you get started. There are 13 Bike Libraries in Leeds, and 5 sites where you can donate an unused bike to the scheme.</p>
Bike maintenance and recycling</h2>
The Bikes College</h3>
 The Bikes College at Copley Hill (LS12 1HY) offers bike repairs, free collections for donated bikes, low cost refurbished bikes, spares and accessories and training on bike maintenance. They accept bicycles, parts and accessories in any condition.</p>
Leeds Bike Mill</h3>
 Leeds Bike Mill is a community bike recycling and training co-op . They accept full bicycles, components and tools.</p>
Bike repairs at The Pedallers’ Arms</h3>
Learn how to repair a bike with regular friendly drop in sessions and other bike related events at the Pedallers’ Arms .</p>
