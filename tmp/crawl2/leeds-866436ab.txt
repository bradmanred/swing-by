Yeadon Tarnfield Park</h1>
Yeadon Tarnfield Park is 9 miles North-West of Leeds city centre and is approximately 17 hectares in area.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
As well as the tarn, the park is also an excellent location to get great views of the planes taking off and landing at Leeds and Bradford Airport.</p>

Leeds Sailing and Activity Centre</h3>
For the more active visitor to the park, the tarn is used for various water sports activities and is the base for the Leeds Sailing and Activity Centre. For more information regarding the club and its activities you can contact them on 0113 378 1271.</p>
Fishing</h3>
If you would like to enjoy a day fishing in the tarn, you can get tickets from the Specialised Pleasure Angling Club. Please contact them on 07714 806 735.</p>
Access information</h2>
Car parks</h3>
There are three car parks located at the following sites: adjacent to the Tarn activity centre off Town Street; to the south west of the park off Cemetery Road and at the North West of the park off Cemetery Road.</p>
Disabled parking</h3>
Disabled parking available in the Tarn activity centre car park off Town Street.</p>
Paths</h3>
All paths are wheelchair accessible.</p>
Entrances</h3>
All entrances are wheelchair accessible.</p>
Accessible toilets</h3>
Disabled toilets are available in the Tarn activity centre.</p>
Disabled play equipment</h3>
There is currently no disabled play equipment in this park.</p>
