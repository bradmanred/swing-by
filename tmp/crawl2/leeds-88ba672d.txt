Get travel alerts by email</h2>
To receive notification of roadworks and closures, choose the mail icon on the interactive map.</p>
You will need to provide:</p>
your email address</li>
a description of your alert, for example “John’s travel alert” (this will become the subject title of your notification emails)</li>
Social media</h2>

Bridgewater Place</h2>
Bridgewater Place is susceptible to disruption due to high winds. During these periods, roads around Bridgewater Place may close to vehicles and pedestrians. Diversions will be put in place.</p>
When it is necessary to close both the roads and footways, our staff will be on-site to assist.</p>
