Bramley Park</h1>
Bramley Park is a large community park, ​4 miles west of the city centre, set on a hillside with a lovely views of the surrounding countryside.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 Location
</h2>
Westover Road, Bramley, LS13 3PG.</p>
 ​Attractions and facilities</h2>
Open parkland </li>
Sports pitches</li>
Tennis courts</li>
Playground</li>
Multi-use games area</li>
Picnic tables and benches</li>
Bowling green</li>
Bramley War Memorial</li>
Rose garden</li>
Events</h2>
Community events such as the Bramley Carnival take place in the park throughout the year.</p>
Access information</h2>
Buses and disabled access</h3>
Details of buses travelling to this area can be found by visiting the Metro website .</p>
Car parks</h3>
All parking is on street outside the park, and there are no disabled parking bays.</p>
Paths</h3>
All paths are negotiable by wheelchair.</p>
Entrance</h3>
All entrances are accessible bar one by Westover Road, which has steps.</p>
There are no disabled toilets</p>
Disabled play equipment</h3>
Some inclusive play equipment is available in the playground.</p>
