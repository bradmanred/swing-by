In Bloom competitions</h2>
 Yorkshire in Bloom is a voluntary organisation in North, South, East, West and North Yorkshire that administers the RHS Britain in Bloom Campaign.</p>
 RHS Britain in Bloom is the largest horticultural campaign in Europe and each year involves more people, groups and organisations creating lasting improvements to local environments.</p>
The special categories include public, private and charitable establishments, business premises, visitor attractions, caravan and camping sites, schools, youth environmental projects and further education establishments.</p>
Britain in Bloom is an inclusive campaign and any community, no matter how small or large, can get involved. There is a category to suit every group, from villages, towns and cities to parks, cemeteries and public gardens, railway stations and transport hubs, businesses large and small, schools and educational establishment, young people's gardening clubs and lots more.</p>
 Find out more and enter on the Yorkshire in Bloom website .</p>
In Bloom Groups</h2>
There are over 50 local in Bloom groups across Leeds. These volunteers take pride in where they live. They enhance the area, raising funds for flower displays, creating and maintaining flower beds and litter picking.</p>
To see if there is a ​local group near you or to establish one in your area, please email countrysiderangers@leeds.gov.uk .​​​​
</p>
The Arium - Supporting community groups</h2>
 The Arium supports many community groups across the city with their gardening needs.</p>
Leeds has many In Bloom groups ranging from small close knit communities to very large groups - many of whom have enjoyed success both locally and nationally. The Arium is proud to work with these groups each year. They make the city a greener place to work and live.​</p>
