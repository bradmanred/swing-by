Business parking permits</h1>
Businesses located within a permit scheme area may be able to apply for up to 3 permits.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Apply</h2>
</div>
To apply, please contact us . You will need to explain why you need business permits.</p>
</div>
We will ask you to provide a copy of one of the following:</p>
</div>
a tenancy, leasehold or mortgage agreement issued within the last 3 months</li>
a business rate account which includes the name and address of the business</li>
</div>
Cost</h3>
</div>
Business permits cost £95 each per year.</p>
</div>
If your application is successful, we will contact you to arrange payment.</p>
</div>
Renewing your business permit</h2>
</div>
Your business permits will last for a maximum of a year.</p>
</div>
If you would like to renew your business permits for another year, please contact us .
</p>
