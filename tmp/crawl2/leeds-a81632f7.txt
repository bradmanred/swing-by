Request a vehicle crossing outside your house</h1>
Find out how to request a dropped kerb outside your home to allow vehicle access from a road, across a footpath and to your driveway.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
A vehicular crossing is an area of lowered pavement and kerb which is used to give access to vehicles from a road across the footway and onto a driveway or parking area.</p>
It is against the law to drive across a public footpath without an authorised vehicular crossing as damage will be caused to the footway or services underneath.</p>
</div>
</div>
If this is not what you want to do, find out how to request:</p>
 report the need for an accessible kerb in your local area  </li>
 request a disabled parking bay outside a property </li>
 </div>
Costs</h2>
Site inspection</h3>
We charge an initial fee of £55.06 to carry out a site inspection.</p>
Our inspection fee is non-refundable and there is no guarantee you will be permitted to construct a new crossing.</p>
Construction</h3>
You will need to arrange your own New Roads and Street Works Act (NRSWA) accredited contractor to provide a quote for the construction costs and carry out the works at your own expense.</p>
A standard crossing usually costs around £1,500.</p>
Additional charges</h3>
If there is an obstruction to the proposed crossing such as a street lamp, BT column or similar, the utility company may consider relocating the obstruction if possible. This is a chargeable service.</p>
Following a successful initial application, a NRSWA accredited contractor will need to apply for a Section 171 licence to gain permission to excavate on the public highway. This should be submitted to  RASWA.forms@leeds.gov.uk  and will incur a fee of £345.90.</p>
Before you apply</h2>
There are some things to consider before applying that can help speed up the process:</p>
there must be enough space for a hard standing to be constructed within the boundary of your property. There must be a minimum of 5 metres distance between the boundary and the property</p>
</li>
if you are a current council tenant, you will need to  contact your local housing office  to get permission before applying and paying the Highways admin fee</p>
</li>
if your property is a former council property, you will also need to  contact your local housing office  to get permission</li>
if the crossing will be accessed from an A, B or C classified road (for example, the A58) or passes over non-highway land, you will need approval from planning services. For help and advice, call planning services on 0113 222 4409</li>
if a tree obstructs your proposed vehicle crossing, you will need to contact our forestry team for further advice on 0113 395 7400</li>
How to apply</h2>
If you wish to proceed, you can request an inspection for a vehicle crossing, using our online form.</p>
 Request a vehicle crossing site inspection
What happens next</h2>
Once we receive your application, a highways inspector will contact you to advise you of the next steps. They will also provide details on how to make the required inspection fee payment of £55.06, which must be received before the inspection can take place.</p>
An inspector will then arrange to visit the property and if all requirements are met, you will receive a commencement letter giving permission to proceed with construction.</p>
