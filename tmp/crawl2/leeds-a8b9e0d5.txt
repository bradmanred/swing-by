Problems with a public right of way
</h2>
Use our online form to report problems with:
</p>
public footpaths (not roadside pavements) - for use by pedestrians</li>
public bridleways - for use by people on foot, horseback or bicycle</li>
byways open to all traffic - these have full vehicular rights, but are mainly used as bridleways
</li>
restricted byways - these have rights on foot, horseback, bicycle and non-mechanical vehicles such as horse and cart
</li>

Problems taking place on public rights of way</h2>
The following problems should be reported to our teams in other areas.</p>
Dog fouling</h3>
 Use our online form to let us know of an issue with dog poo on a public right of way .</p>
Leaves</h3>
Public rights of way do not get swept for leaves and other natural waste, unless they are also classed as an adopted highway with the highways team.</p>
 Use our online form to make a request for leaves to be cleared from a public right of way .</p>
Antisocial behaviour</h3>
Many types of antisocial behaviour should be reported both to West Yorkshire Police and to ourselves. This allows both of us to gather accurate data on problem areas, which helps us to create plans to tackle antisocial behaviour. This includes the use of motorbikes and quadbikes along a public right of way.</p>
Please check both websites below for further information:</p>
 antisocial behaviour and crime </li>
 how we can tackle anti-social behaviour at the West Yorkshire Police website </li>
Fallen or broken trees and branches</h3>
 Use our online form to report fallen trees or branches that do not present an immediate risk to the public, but which are across or hanging beside a public right of way .</p>
Dangerous trees or large broken branches that are hanging above a public right of way, and which do present an immediate risk, must be referred to our forestry team: Tree emergencies </p>
