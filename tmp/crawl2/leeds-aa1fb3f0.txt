Report a change in circumstances</h1>
Find out what to do if you no longer need your Blue Badge.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Make sure your address, phone number and email address are up to date so we can contact you.</p>
</div>
</div>
If you no longer need your blue badge, it has expired, or the holder of the badge has died please send it back to the Blue Badge Team or hand it in to a community hub .</p>
Moving address</h2>
If you are moving or have already moved house, we need you to provide evidence of both your old and new address and the date of the move. You can take evidence documents into one of our community hubs . Or you can send the proof and your new details to the Blue Badge Team .</p>
Change email address or phone number</h2>
We send renewal reminders by email, so it’s important to make sure your details are up to date. Get in touch to let us know your latest contact information.</p>
For any other changes in circumstances please contact the Blue Badge Team.</p>
