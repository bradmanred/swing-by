Report the need for an accessible kerb in your local area </h1>
Find out how to report a high kerb that is a barrier to accessing the local area, and request accessibility improvements.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Dropped kerbs enable disabled people to safely cross the roads. These are automatically provided during full street refurbishment (re-kerbing and re-surfacing) works.</p>
If high kerbs make it hard for you to use a wheelchair or a mobility scooter to get around your local area you can report this and request that accessibility improvements are considered at locations where you experience issues.</p>
This service cannot be used to provide a vehicle crossing to access a driveway or provide bespoke individual adaptations directly in front of private properties.</p>
</div>
</div>
If this is not what you want to do, find out how to request:</p>
 a vehicle crossing outside your house </li>
 a disabled parking bay outside a property </li>
 </div>
Our improvement programme</h2>
We manage a programme of dropped kerb improvements to prioritise locations where disabled people require level access on the public highway. This helps improve accessibility and better facilitate pedestrian journeys in local areas.</p>
This means that these improvements can be delivered well ahead of future maintenance works in locations where they are urgently required by disabled residents.</p>
Identify a location</h2>
You can let us know where you are currently trying to cross a road (or roads) and pinpoint the locations that do not have accessible dropped kerbs.</p>
To best utilise our limited resources, we will prioritise the provision at locations where there is no suitable alternative route.</p>
</div>
</div>
If you wish to proceed, you can identify a location to be investigated, using our online form.</p>
Completing the form helps us to prioritise locations where disabled people are encountering  barriers on their frequent journeys as part of our improvement plan. There is no guarantee a dropped kerb will be provided for each request.</p>
</div>

What happens next</h2>
Assessment</h3>
An engineer from our team will visit the site to determine the extent of the works required and if they are deliverable before scheduling the works with the appropriate contractors.</p>
Works</h3>
Improvements can take around 6 months to be implemented, depending on resourcing and demand.</p>
