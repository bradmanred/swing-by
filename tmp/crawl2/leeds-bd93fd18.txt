Appeal a parking fine or penalty charge notice</h1>
Find out how to view evidence and appeal against parking, bus lane or gate, moving traffic and school street fines.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
View evidence</h2>
In most cases, our civil enforcement officers and camera enforcement will record photographic or video evidence of parking, bus lane or gate, moving traffic and school street offences.</p>
</div>
You will need your:
</p>
</div>
vehicle registration number
</li>
PCN number (starts with "LS" followed by eight numbers/letters)
</li>
</div>
If you have lost your ticket, contact us. 
</p>
</div>
 View evidence online
</div>
How to appeal</h2>
</div>
If you don’t think you should have been issued with a PCN, you can appeal against it.
</p>
</div>
If we receive your appeal within 14 days of the PCN being issued, the charge will be frozen at the lower amount shown on your ticket or letter.</p>
</div>
If your appeal is unsuccessful you will be given a further 14 days to pay the lower charge from the date of the decision.
</p>
</div>
Online</h3>
</div>
​You will need your: 
</p>
</div>
vehicle registration number
</li>
PCN number (starts with "LS" followed by eight numbers/letters)
</li>
</div>
You will receive email confirmation that we have received your appeal. We aim to respond within ten working days. </p>
</div>
If you have lost your ticket, contact us . 
</p>
</div>

Post</h3>
</div>
Write to us explaining why you shouldn't have been issued with a Penalty Charge Notice. </p>
</div>
Please include:</p>
</div>
your PCN number (starts with 'LS' followed by eight numbers/letters)</li>
any evidence that will support your appeal</li>
</div>
If you have lost your Penalty Charge Notice, contact us .
</p>
</div>
Parking services
 P.O. Box 139 
 Leeds
 LS9 1AA
</p>
 </div>
</div>
Please allow time for postage to ensure that your appeal reaches us within 28 days of the PCN being issued.
