Renew a Blue Badge</h1>
When your badge is due to expire you will need to apply for a new one, which you can do up to three months before your current one expires.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Please re-apply as early as possible so we can re-issue your Blue Badge before your current one expires.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
When you reapply you will still need to supply proof that you meet the eligibility criteria , even if you’ve had a Blue Badge before.</p>
You do not need to supply proofs of address and identity again unless these details have changed since your last application, but you will need to tick those boxes in the "Evidence" section of the online application form.</p>
 Re-apply for your Blue Badge
If you have recently renewed your badge, you may still get a renewal reminder from the Department for Transport (DfT). You can ignore this reminder.
