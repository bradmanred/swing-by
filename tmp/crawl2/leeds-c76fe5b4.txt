Landlord parking permits</h1>
Parking schemes and permits</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Landlords who are members of the Leeds Rental Standard can apply for a parking permit. This can be used to park when visiting properties they own or manage.</p>
How to apply</h2>
 Contact us to request a permit. Please include your:</p>
name</li>
address</li>
Leeds Rental Standard landlord number - if you are unsure of this, please contact Leeds Rental Standard </li>
Cost</h3>
There is a charge for landlord permits - this will be discussed when you contact us to request a permit.</p>
