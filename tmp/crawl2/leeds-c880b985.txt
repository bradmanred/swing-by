Mass transit</h1>
The West Yorkshire Combined Authority has launched its consultation on how to deliver the first phase of its mass transit scheme.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The Combined Authority is proposing to build a tram line across Leeds, and a tram line from Leeds to Bradford. They are asking for people's views on each of the potential routes.</p>
 Find out more and have your say on the mass transit for West Yorkshire website .
