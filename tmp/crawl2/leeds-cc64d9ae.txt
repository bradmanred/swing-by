Travel to work survey</h1>
The travel to work survey is an annual survey that helps us to identify commuting trends and helps us to inform future transport investment decisions.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The survey also helps businesses and organisations to meet travel plan obligations at individual places of work.
</p>
The 2022 survey ran between Monday 31 October and Friday 9 December 2022.
</p>
To register your business to take part or to find out if your business is already registered, please email  ITBtravelsurvey@leeds.gov.uk .
</p>
Previous survey results
</h2>
Over 9,000 individual responses were received in 2021. The results showed a glimpse of how COVID-19 will have a lasting impact on how people work, and how often they will commute to the workplace. </p>
Over 25% of those surveys were working from home, with around two-thirds of those responding indicating they will no longer go to their workplace 5 days a week, instead working a hybrid mix of home and workplace working. </p>
 See the full travel to work survey 2021 results on the Connecting Leeds website .
