Open access</h1>
The Countryside and Rights of Way (CROW) Act 2000 gave people new rights of access on foot to areas of open country and registered common land.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
The access rights, sometimes referred to as ‘the right to roam’ were introduced by the government on a regional basis, and came into force in the Leeds area on 31 October 2005.</p>
Access land in Leeds includes:</p>
Hawksworth Moor</li>
Land adjacent to Reva Reservoir</li>
parts of the Otley Chevin</li>
Townclose Hills in Kippax</li>
Buckstone Fields in the Meanwood Valley</li>
The access land is shown on the latest editions of the Ordnance Survey Explorer maps:</p>
289 Leeds</li>
288 Bradford and Huddersfield</li>
297 Lower Wharfedale</li>
Alternatively, the Conclusive Maps of Open Access for the Leeds area are available to view by appointment only. Contact us to arrange an appointment with a member of our Public Rights of Way team.</p>
