Meanwood Park</h1>
Meanwood Park is 4 miles north of Leeds city centre and is approximately 29 hectares in area.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Location</h2>
Green Road, Meanwood, LS6 4LE.
</p>
Attractions and facilities</h2>
Meanwood Valley Trail</li>
Meanwood Nature Reserve
</li>

The landscape, which is rich with the remnants of the industrial heritage of the area includes woodland, meadows, a stream and areas of mown grass.</p>
Volunteer</h2>
If you would like to join Meanwood Valley Volunteer Rangers or would like more information, please phone 0113 336 7554 or email MeanwoodValley.Ranger@leeds.gov.uk .</p>
Access information
</h2>
Car park: One car park off Green Road at the South West of the park.</p>
Disabled parking: There are two disabled parking bays in the main car park off Green Road.</p>
Paths: The main path through the park is suitable for disabled users, but there are several woodland paths with uneven surfaces.</p>
Entrances: All entrances are wheelchair accessible.</p>
Accessible toilets: No disabled toilets.</p>
