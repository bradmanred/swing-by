Tree emergencies</h1>
Report a tree or branch that is causing immediate danger to the public or a property.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
If a tree is causing an immediate danger to highway users, please call the police on 999.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
We provide a 24 hour emergency service. If a tree or branch is causing immediate danger to the public highway, a person or property, call 0113 378 1855.</p>
You will need to provide:</p>
the exact location of the tree or branch</li>
a contact name and telephone number</li>
We do not deal with trees on private land, unless they are causing an immediate danger to the highway or to public-owned land.</p>
