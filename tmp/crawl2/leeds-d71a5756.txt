Potternewton Park</h1>
Potternewton Park is a popular park in the heart of Chapeltown, just 2 miles north east of Leeds city centre. It is just over 32 acres in size and has facilities for people of all ages.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>

Harehills Lane, LS7 4HA</p>

West Indian Carnival </h2>
Potternewton Park is home to the annual West Indian Carnival​ and Reggae Concert which takes place every August Bank Holiday weekend.
</p>
Access information </h2>
 Paths: All the paths are negotiable by wheelchair; although there are differing gradients around the park, no issues have been reported.</p>
 Entrances: All bar one of the entrances are accessible; there is a small pedestrian entrance at the top of Avenue Hill which has a step and so is not accessible for wheelchairs.</p>
 Accessible toilets: There are no public toilets on site.</p>
 Disabled Play Equipment: Some inclusive play equipment is available in the playground.
