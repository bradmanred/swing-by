School Streets permit</h1>
How to apply for a School Streets permit.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
A School Streets permit allows you access to drive your vehicle during restricted times in a School Street zone . School Streets permits are free.
 </p>
</div>
If you have a Blue Badge , you do not have to apply for a permit. You can access School Streets by displaying your badge.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Who can get a permit
</h2>
</div>
If you live on a School Street
</h3>
</div>
If you're a resident of a School Street, you can apply for a:
</p>
</div>
resident permit (for up to three vehicles registered at your address)
</li>
visitor permit for any vehicle visiting your property (one per household)
</li>
</div>
If you run a business on a School Street
</h3>
</div>
You can apply for up to 10 permits for your employees or vehicles registered to your business.
</p>
</div>
These permits are not parking permits. If your street has restricted parking, you'll need to apply for a parking permit separately .</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Applying
</h2>
</div>
You can apply for a permit online or by visiting one of our community hubs .</p>
</div>
You need to attach some documents to your application:</p>
</div>
proof of address issued within the last three months (such as a council tax, business rates or utility bill)
</li>
proof your vehicle is registered to someone at the address (your vehicle log book, insurance document or a letter from the CEO for businesses)
</li>
</div>
If applying for a visitor permit, you only need to provide proof of address.
 </p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
 Apply for a permit
</div>
After you apply</h2>
We aim to get your permit to you in 10 working days but it might sometimes take a little longer.</p>
</div>
We are asking people to avoid driving on School Streets during restricted times if possible, even if you have a permit. If you do need to use your car during restrictions, please:</p>
display your permit or Blue Badge in your windscreen</li>
drive at no more than 5 miles per hour</li>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Contact us</h2>
Email: schoolstreets@leeds.gov.uk
