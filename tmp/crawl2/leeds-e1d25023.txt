Street lighting</h1>
Before reporting, note that street lights along certain roads will be switched off between midnight and 5.30am as part of the selective part-night street lighting scheme.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Report a street lighting problem</h2>
We do this to:</p>
help reduce carbon emissions for street lighting by 4.7% per year</li>
save around £1.3 million over 10 years</li>
All street lights will continue to be lit until midnight and the majority of lights will remain operational throughout the night. Only those selected to be part-night switched will go out at midnight.</p>
For further information,  use the general highways reporting form .</p>
To report a problem with a street light, use the  Report a lighting fault form .</p>
Emergency faults</h2>
Emergencies include</p>
missing street light doors</li>
electrical wires showing</li>
top of lights hanging loose</li>
damage</li>
vandalism</li>
Report these on 0800 032 5349 as soon as possible. We aim to attend to these problems within one hour.</p>
General street lighting enquiries</h2>
Contact  Lights on in Leeds  for other street lighting enquiries.</p>
