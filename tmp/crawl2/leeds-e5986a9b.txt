Take part in cycling discussions</h1>
The forum is open to everyone and discusses anything related to cycling. The meetings are also attended by people from Leeds City Council. It does not replace or supersede other consultation processes operated by the council.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Leeds Cycling Consultation Forum</h2>
Meetings are in-person at Leeds Civic Hall with the option to join online through Microsoft Teams.
</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
Main forum working group meetings</h3>
The forum is now scheduled to meet on:</p>
Wednesday 29 July 2026, 6pm to 8pm in Committee Room 4 (Civic Hall) and via MS Teams.</p>
Wednesday 27 January 2027, 6pm to 8pm in Committee Room 4 (Civic Hall) and via MS Teams.</p>
 Find Leeds Civic Hall on Google Maps .</p>
You don't need to register to attend the forum in-person.</p>
</div>
</div>
Online meeting details</h4>
If you are attending the forum through Microsoft Teams, the link will be sent on the day of the meeting to the cycle forum mailing list. </p>
To be included in the mailing list and receive the link, email Connecting Leeds connectingleeds@leeds.gov.uk .</p>
