Report changes for parking permits</h1>
Let us know about changes so that your permit stays valid and up to date.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Failure to display a valid permit may result in a parking ticket .
</p>
</div>
Change of vehicle</h2>
If you have changed your vehicle, we will need to update your permit.</p>
If you have transferred your number plates to your new vehicle you do not need to notify us.</p>
Online</h3>
You will need to apply for a new permit under the permit application tab.</p>
 Change vehicle details online .</p>
You will need to upload a copy of your new vehicle log book (V5C) as proof of ownership so that we can send you an updated permit.</p>
 Upload proof online .</p>
Post</h3>
If you are unable to let us know about a change of vehicle online, write to us and include a copy of your new vehicle log book (V5C).</p>
Parking Services
PO Box 139
Leeds
LS9 1AA</p>
Return a permit</h2>
If a permit is no longer needed, return it to us.</p>
Parking Services
PO Box 139
Leeds
LS9 1AA</p>
Replacement permits</h2>
Replacement permits cost £10.</p>
Lost or damaged permits</h3>
 Contact us to request a replacement permit.</p>
Stolen permits</h3>
If you are able to provide a crime reference number, you will not be charged.</p>
 Contact us to request a replacement permit.</p>
