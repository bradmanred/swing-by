Resident parking permits</h1>
How to apply for or renew a resident parking permit.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Cost</h2>
There is no charge for a resident permit.</p>
</div>
Apply</h2>
You will need to provide proof of your address and proof that you own the vehicle.</p>
Proof of address</h3>
Documents showing proof of address (for example council tax or utility bills) need to have been issued within the last three months.</p>
Rented properties</h3>
If you rent a property on a short-term basis, you must provide a copy of the signed original tenancy agreement which shows the start and end dates of your tenancy.</p>
Proof of vehicle ownership</h3>
Please provide a copy of one of the following showing the permit address:</p>
vehicle log book (V5C)</li>
vehicle insurance document</li>
</div>
 Apply for a resident permit online
</div>
Once you have applied, make a note of your application number and your PIN – you will need these to upload copies of your proof documents. </p>
</div>
 Upload proofs online
</div>
Renew</h2>
</div>
You need to renew your permit every year. We will send you a reminder by email when it is due to expire.</p>
When you receive your reminder you will need to follow the instructions shown in the email.</p>
</div>
 Find out what supporting documentation is needed if you have changed your vehicle .</p>
</div>
 Renew a permit online
</div>
Replacement permits</h2>
</div>
Replacement permits cost £10.</p>
</div>
Lost or damaged permits</h3>
</div>
 Contact us to request a replacement permit.</p>
</div>
Stolen permits</h3>
</div>
If you are able to provide a crime reference number, you will not be charged.</p>
</div>
 Contact us to request a replacement permit.</p>
