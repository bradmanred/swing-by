Boost your team’s spirit with a green team away day</h2>
Leeds Green Spaces offer outdoor team away days, unique team building experiences for all. Benefiting your team, the local community, and green spaces across Leeds.</p>
Whether you're looking to boost team morale, strengthen communication or make a positive impact on the environment, our team away day offer will deliver an unforgettable experience.</p>
The offer, the cost, and what is included</h2>
Your team will have the opportunity to work hands-on in the city’s stunning parks and green spaces. An experience that brings employees closer together and lets them feel a tangible connection to the Leeds environment.</p>

Benefits</h2>
Our green team away days go beyond just helping the environment. They are designed to build stronger, more motivated teams while fulfilling your environmental, social, and governance objectives.</p>
Corporate away days have been shown to:</p>
improve employee motivation and boost team spirit</li>
enhance skills and self-confidence in a non-work environment</li>
foster better communication and collaboration</li>
align with your company’s corporate social responsibility and sustainability goals</li>
Spaces fill up quickly, so don’t wait! Book your away day today and be a part of something special.</p>
