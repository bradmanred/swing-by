Coach parking</h2>
There is dedicated coach parking for approximately 12 coaches between Wade Lane/Lovell Park road. </p>
 View coach parking on Google maps .
</p>
Hours of operation</h3>
You must pay to park in coach bays between 8:30 am to midnight, seven days a week.
</p>
Charges
</h3>
Up to 5 hours: £12
</p>
All day: £17
</p>
Pay by phone by downloading the PayByPhone app or by calling 0330 400 7275 when you want to start and stop your parking.
</p>
