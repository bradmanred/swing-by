LeedsWatch</h1>
LeedsWatch response officers provide ad-hoc patrols of parks and open spaces as part of its broader community safety services in Leeds.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
Uniformed officers provide a visible deterrent to antisocial behaviour, helping to reassure the public and promote a sense of security. These officers are deployed on an ad-hoc basis to areas where issues have been identified or where additional support is needed. By patrolling parks and green spaces, they contribute to a safer, more welcoming environment for visitors and residents around the area alike.</p>
Problems with bikes and off-road vehicles</h2>
Issues such as nuisance bikes and off-road vehicles should be reported directly to the Police by phone on 101 or you can report it on the West Yorkshire Police website .</p>
