West Park Firework display 2026</h1>
</div>
Tickets are now available for the West Park bonfire and fireworks event.</div>
Image</div>
</div>
</div>
</div>
 </div>
 </div>
The Greatest Show in the Sky takes place on Thursday 5 November at West Park, Long Eaton.</p>
Enjoy a magical night packed with fairground rides, a roaring bonfire from 6.30pm and a dazzling fireworks display from 7.30pm. Family tickets are available for a great value evening of entertainment and fun. Gates open 5pm.</p>
Ticket Prices:</h3>
 Buy tickets </p>
Adults: Prepaid and online: £8. (Tickets will cost £10 on the day)</p>
16 years and under and over 60s: £5 (Tickets will cost £7 on the day)</p>
Family - Two adults and up to three children: £20 (Tickets will cost £25 on the day)</p>
Children five years and under: Free</p>
You can now follow the council's WhatsApp channel . </p>
