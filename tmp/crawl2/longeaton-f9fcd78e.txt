 Partner Services
 </h1>
Some services are provided by other councils or organisations. You can find details on this page.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
We are responsible for certain services across Erewash, for example council tax, waste collection, environmental health, housing and planning.  </p>
Derbyshire County Council is responsible for services across the whole of Derbyshire, responsible for services including education, roads/highways and social care.  </p>
Some of their key services you may wish to access include:</p>
 Report a road issue or a pothole </p>
 Services to support children, help you to live independently and stay healthy </p>
 Search and apply for school places </p>
 Apply for a Gold Card </p>
To apply for housing, visit the Home Options website.  Please note that the council does not have any housing stock.
 Home-Options - Home 
For help to make an application contact EMH
 Contact us - Emh Homes
