Car Parking Fines</h1>
 Penalty charge notices (PCNs) </h2>
If you do not display a valid pay and display ticket, pay by phone, or have a virtual permit in one of the Council's car parks or park in restricted areas, you could be issued with a penalty charge notice (PCN).</p>
 How much is the parking fine? </p>
The charge is £70, however for minor infringements (for example exceeding the maximum time limit or not displaying a Pay and Display ticket), the charge is generally £50.</p>
Payment of a PCN must be received within 28 days of the date of issue. If payment is received within 14 days, a discounted rate of £25 or £35 depending on the contravention will be accepted.</p>
 Penalty charge notices for parking on the road in a restricted area (notices starting with XP) </h2>
Penalty charge notices issued for parking on restrictions such as double yellow lines are dealt with by South Essex Parking Partnership; Essex Parking Partnership </p>
The South Essex Parking Partnership can be contacted at:</p>

 Penalty charge notices issued in a Council car park (notices starting with ML) </h2>
 How to pay </p>
Penalty Charge Notices (PCN's) issued by Maldon District Council can be paid via:</p>
 Online:                  Maldon District Council Parking Portal .</p>
 Telephone:          Card payments may be made by calling 0333 1216666.</p>
 Post:                    By cheque or postal order (crossed 'a/c Payee only'), payable to Maldon District Council.</p>
                             Please write the PCN number on the back of the cheque or postal order and send the payment slip with your payment to:  Maldon District Council, PO Box 3584, Chippenham, Wiltshire SN15 9EF.      </p>
Please quote the PCN number, the vehicle registration, and your address in all correspondence.</p>
</div>
 Making an appeal </h2>
All 'challenges' against the issue of a penalty charge notice (PCN) must be made in writing. Verbal 'challenges' are not acceptable unless you have a valid reason for not being able to put your claim in writing.</p>
Before submitting a challenge, check the reasons for issue on the PCN which can be found beneath the time and date recorded, please ensure your challenge addresses these issues.</p>
Providing the challenge is received within 14 days of issue, the PCN will be held at the discounted rate of £25 or £35 depending on the contravention. All challenges will be responded to in writing outlining the Council's decision.</p>
If the grounds for the challenge are rejected, you will be notified in writing and the discount period will be reset for a period of 14 days from the date of the reject letter. Any challenges received outside of the 14-day discount period will be considered at the full rate of £50 or £70 depending on the contravention.</p>
Challenges should be made in writing to: Maldon District Council, PO Box 3584, Chippenham, Wiltshire SN15 9EF  or online via:  Maldon District Council Parking Portal </p>
If you are unable to put your claim in writing, please telephone 0333 1216666.</p>
 Please ensure that you quote the full 10-digit ML, vehicle registration number and the full postal address on all correspondence. </p>
</div>
 Traffic Penalty Tribunal </h2>
If your challenge is rejected, the registered owner/keeper or hirer of the vehicle can make a formal representation against the issue of the PCN by responding to the 'notice to owner' which will be sent 28 days after the letter of rejection.</p>
Depending on the response of the Council you may decide to appeal against the PCN to the Traffic Penalty Tribunal.</p>
This is an independent body specifically set up to hear appeals.</p>
Information about the Traffic Penalty Tribunal is available HERE </p>
</div>
