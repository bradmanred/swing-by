Parks and Recreation</h2></div>
 </div>
</div></div>
 The Maldon District offers visitors huge choice in terms of stunning landscapes and picturesque coastline, speckled with quaint villages and fantastic public parks for all the family to enjoy.  </p>
The Maldon District’s parks are great places to play, get close to nature and make memories that last a lifetime. With interactive trails, splash parks and play areas plus plenty of open spaces, they are perfect for creating a magical escape for the whole family. Village parks often have exciting play sites, and even outdoor gym equipment.</p>
From cooling off at the   Splash Park   in   Promenade Park   to enjoying being by the water at   Riverside Park   in Burnham-on-Crouch, there is something for everyone all year round.</p>
Find your nearest park use the list and map below.</p>

Promenade Park</h2></div>
 </div>
</div></div>
Welcome to Promenade Park in Maldon, the largest waterside park in Essex, providing a fun-filled, family friendly destination all year round with amazing events and activities throughout the year. Nestled between the historic high street and Blackwater Estuary, the park features a water Splash Park, beach huts and deckchairs for hire, sandpits, galleon play ship and aerial zip-wire, model boating and crabbing lake as well as cafe kiosks and picnic areas. There’s a packed programme of events and activities planned each year, or just come and enjoy the peaceful walks and beautiful sea views. </p></div>
