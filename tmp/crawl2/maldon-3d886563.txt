Tesco Free Bus</h1>
The 288 provides a free, circular bus service around Maldon.</p>
The route and timetable can be viewed at  bustimes.org/services/288-central-maldon-circular </p>
Vehicles can park at Tesco's car park (just off A414) and board the Bus from there . </p>
Pedestrians can get on and off the bus at any bus stop on the route.</p>
Please be aware that the Tesco car park is privately operated by Tesco, as such all drivers parking to use the 288 bus service should ensure that they comply with parking restrictions on site. This information can be found on signage within the car park.</p>
