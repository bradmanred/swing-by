Residents' town centre off-peak parking permit</h1>
Residents living adjacent to one of our town centre car parks can purchase an annual off-peak permit at a cost of £102.50 (Each permit is valid for the parking of one vehicle only) which will allow parking at the nominated car park at the following times:</p>
Monday – Saturday (inc. Bank Holidays) 8am to 9am & 5pm to 10pm </li>
Sunday – All day </li>
Normal pay & display charges will apply Monday to Saturday 9am - 5pm</p>
If you wish to apply for this off-peak resident permit, please apply  https://secure.mipermit.com/maldon/  </p>
Please make sure that you have two pieces of proof of your residency to upload with your application.</p>
If you have any queries, please contact parking.services@maldon.gov.uk .</p>
