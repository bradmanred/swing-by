Parks and Open Spaces</h1>
</p>

From Promenade Park in Maldon, to Riverside Park in Burnham‑on‑Crouch, and community open spaces across our towns and villages. On this page you can find all the information you'll need to plan and enjoy your visit to our parks and open spaces, no matter the weather!</p>
