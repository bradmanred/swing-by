Disabled parking</h1>
How to apply for a blue badge</h2>
Information on applying for a blue badge can be found on the website for Essex County Council .</p>
 The Blue Badge scheme: rights and responsibilities in England - GOV.UK </p>
Parking in council owned car parks</h2>
Disabled parking bays are provided FREE in all the Council's pay and display car parks in Maldon town centre and Promenade Park. </p>
If the disabled bays are full, you may also park in a regular space for FREE.</p>
A  Blue Badge must be displayed on arrival.</p>
 Parking is FREE and there is no time limit in our car parks. </p>
Please note that the 30-minute only rule in the marked limited waiting bays in Butt Lane and High Street East car park also applies to blue badge holders.</p>
Parking on the road</h2>
If you are a Blue Badge holder, you can park for free in an on-street disabled bay. You can park for free all day unless otherwise stated on the sign by the disabled bay.</p>
If you are a Blue Badge holder, you are entitled to park for free anywhere there is legal on-street parking.</p>
You should clearly display a valid disabled person’s badge and time clock, or we will issue a parking fine (Penalty Charge Notice).</p>
