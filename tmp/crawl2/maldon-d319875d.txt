Car parking refund</h1>
Car parking machine faults and refunds</h2>
 Car parking machine faults and refunds </p>
 </p>
 Report a faulty machine </p>
To report a faulty car park machine please contact us with the details by emailing: parking.services@maldon.gov.uk or calling 01621 854477.</p>
If the fault has resulted in you being overcharged, please accept our sincere apologies for your inconvenience and use the following guidance to request a refund.</p>
 </p>
 Request a refund </p>
Evidence showing all payments you have made will be required with every refund claim, failure to provide this information will result in refund requests being rejected and a delay to your refund being processed.</p>
Please do not upload copies of tickets, only copies taken from bank statements/online banking showing dates of transactions will be accepted, copies of pending payments will not be accepted, only send refund request forms once payments have been debited from your account.</p>
We are unable to refund direct to a Credit Card, all refunds will be via a BACS transfer, therefore we require your Bank name, sort code and account number.</p>
 </p>
Please request a refund ensuring you upload evidence of the overpayment (a copy of your bank statement).</p>
 </p>
 Please be advised that we will only be able to issue you a refund once we have received all this information. </p>
