Parish Council Meetings</h2>
Meetings of the Parish Council take place on the first Tuesday of every month (exc. August when no meeting is held). Details of time and location can be found on advertised agendas - which are located on the website, or in the noticeboards located on Potter Street in Melbourne and Main Street in Kings Newton. These are placed at least 3 working days before the meeting.</p>
All members of the public are welcome and encouraged to attend meetings, as the Parish Council appreciates input from residents in helping make decisions.</p></div></div>

Welcome to Melbourne Parish Council</h1>
Melbourne Parish Council is delighted to launch its new website although inevitably some sections are still to be developed. The website provides a wealth of information through the use of the search menus and quick links. The A-Z of services provides information linked through to South Derbyshire District Council (SDDC) and Derbyshire County Council (DCC). This enables the public to report concerns directly to the responsible body eg. R - Road maintenance - report a pot hole.</p>
We have set up a Community Directory which publishes contact details and associated website links where applicable. If you would like to add to this page, please apply to the Clerk using the contact us page.</p>
If you are unable to find what you are looking for or would like to recommend any improvements to our website then please contact us . Thank you for your support.</p></div>
