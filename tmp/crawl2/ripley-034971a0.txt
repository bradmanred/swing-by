Delivered in partnership with People, Potential, Possibilities (P3), Derbyshire Federation for Mental Health (DFMH) and Derbyshire Mind the service will help people improve and maintain their mental health and wellbeing and reconnect with their local community by offering access to targeted one-to-one staff support, telephone support and community groups, and is open to anyone over the age of 18 living in Derbyshire who is experiencing mental ill health. </div>
 </div>
In conjunction with our partners, the service also delivers the voluntary sector element of the Living Well service. This is a collaborative delivery with the NHS and Adult Social Care, where VCSE Managers, Wellbeing Coaches and Peer Support Worker with lived experience are part of a multi-disciplinary team. </div></div>

 Cost to attend: Free first visit but £5 thereafter and a £10 annual membership fee </div></div>

 </div>
Hearing Help UK is a registered charity organisation (1163380) established over 30 years ago. We provide the Derbyshire Hard of Hearing Service and are contracted by Derbyshire County Council and the Clinical Commissioning Group, to offer friendly practical help, information and support to individuals with hearing loss and their families and carers. We are ideally located in Marehay, Ripley and operate throughout the County in the regions of Amber Valley, Bolsover, Chesterfield, Derbyshire Dales, Erewash, High Peak, North East Derbyshire and South Derbyshire. </div>
 </div>
Our staff are supported by a team of dedicated volunteers who help us provide services within the community. We achieve this by carrying out assessments to provide equipment such as hearing loops, personal listeners, TV listeners, door bells, and alarm clocks. We also host social events and activities for our members and provide peer support groups. </div></div>

 Lauren's Legacy  
 </h1>
 Tel: 07769 250723 </div>
 </div>
 Email: laurenslegacy99@gmail.com </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
Lauren, affectionately known as “Loz” by her family and friends, was an extraordinary daughter, sister, godmother and young woman. Full of energy and known for her love of life, she was the kind of person who lit up every room — with a reputation for partying hard, but also for being incredibly loyal, compassionate and deeply loved. </div>
 </div>
She was a passionate Sheffield United supporter and completely dedicated to her career as a nurse. In September 2014, Lauren was diagnosed with a rare sarcoma — treatable, but not curable. Over the following five years, she faced every challenge with courage, humour and unwavering strength. </div>
 </div>
Despite the toll of cancer, Lauren never complained. Instead, she took her family and friends on a rollercoaster of laughter, fundraisers, festivals and meaningful moments. During this time, she raised over £90,000 for Teenage Cancer Trust alongside her beloved motorcycle club, League of Zeal MCC. </div>
 </div>
Lauren sadly passed away on 26 June 2019, but her legacy lives on through the charity formed in her name — a charity built with love, purpose and a little lemon-zest spirit. </div>
 </div>
Lauren’s Legacy supports teenagers and young adults aged 13–24 who are currently undergoing cancer treatment. </div>
 </div>
We grant meaningful wishes that help make a difficult time a little easier — from emotional boosts to practical support. </div>
 </div>
All donations go directly to those we support. There are no admin costs, no salaries and no overheads. Just pure giving — exactly as Lauren would have wanted. </div></div>

Have you been diagnosed with Liver Disease? </div>
 </div>
We are creating a community support group for adults, who are affected by a diagnosis of Liver Disease, and their loved ones/carer's and especially if you have lost a loved one to Liver Disease, please come along and join us. </div>
 </div>
Liver Disease can leave you feeling very isolated, lonely and unsupported, we want to help change that in creating a community group that gives you the opportunity to meet others that are following a similar path. </div>
 </div>
So come join us for a cuppa and help us build support within our community for those affected by Liver Disease. </div>
 </div>
Meetings will run on the first Tuesday of each month. </div>
 </div>
Our Tuesday meetings are 10am-12pm at the Marehay Methodist Church, 202 Warmwells Lane, Marehay, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 8JE. </div>
 </div>
Have you been diagnosed with a fatty liver? Not sure what changes to make? Then come join us... The Liver Lantern Hub is now running an additional support group session on a Monday evening to help encourage lifestyle changes to improve your livers health. </div>
 </div>
The first sessions are Monday 5th October and Monday 2nd November 2026 at 7pm to 8.30pm. </div></div>
