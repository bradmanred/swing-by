 Waingroves Show  
 </h1>
 Main contact name: Lynsey Booth (Show Secretary) </div>
 </div>
 Email: info@waingrovesshow.com </div>
 </div>
 Location: Waingroves Community Centre, 136 Church Street, Waingroves, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 9TE and Waingroves Primary School Field, Waingroves Road, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 9TD </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
Every year, on the second weekend in September (12th, 13th, & 14th September 2025), Waingroves hosts their yearly village show. Split up into three days, we host many activities and events that we always make sure there is something everyone can enjoy! From face painting and bouncy castles, to live music and horticulture there is something that the whole family can enjoy. </div>
 </div>
We also host several mini events throughout the year too. We have a Daffodil Show every year in Easter and our Brass Band Concert every year in July. </div></div>
