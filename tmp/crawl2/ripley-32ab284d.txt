 Amber Valley CAMRA    Main contact name: Nora Harper    Email: secretary@ambervalley.camra.org.uk    Meeting address: Various locations. Decided upon at end of last meeting- published on social media/website.    Meeting day/time:  6 meetings a year = bimonthly (end of Jan, Mar, May, July, Sept, Nov)    
 </h1>
 Cost to attend: Nil (membership of national organisation) </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
The branch promotes real ale and cider actively encouraging and supporting the 92 pubs and micros within its boundaries and recognising their importance in social cohesion and well-being.  </div>
 </div>
We organise the running of real ale and cider at local beer festivals e.g. Belper Goes Green with our own equipment (stillages, beer ordering and labour).  </div>
 </div>
The branch is run entirely by volunteers who visit and monitor real ale /cider outlets; annual pub + cider pub of the year competitions take place with winners going ahead into county, regional and national heats.  </div>
 </div>
We also, carry out pubs’ surveys such as in Aug. 24 around Belper’s 42. </div></div>
