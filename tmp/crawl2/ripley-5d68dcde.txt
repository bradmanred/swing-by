 FREE Music in Crossley Park events  
 </h1>
Ripley Town Council is once again proud to present a summer of FREE live music in Crossley Park, School Lane, Ripley. </div>
 </div>
On selected Sundays, performers will be taking to the Crossley Park bandstand between 2:30-4:30pm to entertain crowds and put on a free show for our community. </div>
 </div>
We are also excited to announce that we have increased the performances this year to include two additional dates. This year, we have something for everybody - from brass bands to smooth jazz, acoustic vocalists, pop-punk bands, and Latin-inspired melodies. </div>
 </div>
So we encourage you to grab a chair, some snacks, and some friends and join us in Crossley Park for a summer of live music. </div>
 </div>
See you there! </div>
