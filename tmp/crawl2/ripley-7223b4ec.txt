The Friends of RJS are a group of parents and staff who work together to improve our school. We do this through fundraising - such as the Summer and Christmas Fair, charity cake sales etc. - and organising events for the children at our school (such as the Valentines Disco, leavers' ball etc.) Our events are usually a great success! The money we raise goes straight back into school to improve the resources and facilities available for our children, ultimately improving their learning experiences! </div>

 Friends of Ripley Nursery School (FoRNS)  
 </h1>
 Main contact name: Nicola Groom </div>
 </div>
 Tel: 01773 745014 </div>
 </div>

 </div>
 Location group meets: Ripley Nursery School, Sandham Lane, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 3HE </div>
 </div>
 Day/time group meets: Sometimes half termly depending on what we have planned. </div>
 </div>
 Cost to attend: None </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
Our motto is ‘Many Hands Make Light Work’ and we are always trying to encourage new parents to join us. You can be involved as much or as little as you like. We hold regular meetings, but we understand that sometimes it is hard to come to all of them. Therefore, you could take one project on board and make it your own or put your name forward to help with at least one activity during the year. </div>
 </div>
We are passionate about our school and helping the children be the best they can be. Over the years we have raised money to help the school in numerous ways, FORNS plays an important role in making our school a fun place for the children and their families. But we cannot do it alone. We need all the help that we can get to make every event a successful one. We are extremely lucky to have so many parents and friends of FORNS who are generous with both their time and resources. This has enabled us to hold some really successful fundraising initiatives like:- Disco's, parties, Christmas Fair's, Summer Fair's, Easter Fun Week, Bags 2 School, visit from White Post Farm, a Flower Display Workshop, hampers and much more..... </div></div>
