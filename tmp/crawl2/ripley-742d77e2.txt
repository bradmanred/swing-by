 </div>

 Derbyshire Army Cadet Force (Mercian Regiment)  
 </h1>
 Meeting address: ACF Building, Ripley Academy, Wright Avenue, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 3EY </div>
 </div>
 Day/time group meets: Thursday's 7-9pm </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
Derbyshire Army Cadets offers adventure, skills and leadership opportunities to young people aged 12 – 18. With detachments across the county, cadets experience fieldcraft, first aid, shooting and navigation to name just a few activities – all while making friends and having fun. Our passionate team of adult volunteers, aged 18+, help guide and inspire cadets while gaining their own skills and qualifications. Whether you’re a young person looking for challenge and personal growth, or an adult wanting to give back, there’s a place for you in Derbyshire Army Cadets. </div></div>
