Amber Valley CVS offers free advice on forming or developing volunteering activities, whether you're an individual or part of a group. We also provide support with funding, governance, volunteer management, a Befriending Service, and household support. Additionally, we have access to Social Prescribers who are linked to local GP surgeries. </div></div>

The Amber Valley Access Group have been operational for approximately 25 years. </div>
 </div>
We are a small friendly group who meet on the second Thursday of each month at 2.00pm- Meeting Room 2- Amber Valley Borough Council, Market Place, Ripley. </div>
 </div>
We look at giving advice and assistance to elderly and disabled people in Amber Valley. </div>
 </div>
For example: </div>
Ramps instead of steps </div>
Dropped kerb issues </div>
Narrow doorways and raised threshold strips </div>
Accessible toilets </div>
Car parking for Blue Badge holders </div>
Pathways and footways </div>
 </div>
Please do not hesitate to contact Secretary Dee Bligh on 07866 162462 or email av.access@yahoo.com </div></div>
