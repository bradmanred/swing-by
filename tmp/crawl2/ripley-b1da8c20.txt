 Derbyshire Trusted Traders  
 </h1>
 Derbyshire Trusted Trader can help you find honest and reliable traders who commit to doing a good job for a fair price.  </div>
 </div>
Derbyshire Trusted Trader was set up to: </div>
 </div>
help local people find traders and businesses who agree to do a good job at a fair price and commit to providing good customer service </div>
protect older and vulnerable adults by making it easier for them to use Trusted Traders rather than rogue traders who typically cold-call and use high pressure sales techniques </div>
support good local businesses </div>
It's run by Derbyshire Trading Standards. </div></div>
