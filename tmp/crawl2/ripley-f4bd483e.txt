Amber Sound FM is a Community Radio station based in the Amber Valley, Derbyshire. </div>
 </div>
It was awarded a five-year community license by OFCOM in 2008 after several Restricted Service License broadcasts, and then successfully applied for a 5 year extension in 2013. </div>
 </div>
On the first Saturday of October 2008 the station began broadcasting. The first ever song to be played on Amber Sound was "Easy" by Commodores. </div>
 </div>
Amber Sound said it would "encourage local people, young and old, to become part of" the station. Chris Knight is the Station Manager at Amber Sound FM. </div></div>

 Ripley Green Garters  
 </h1>
 Meeting address: St Joseph's Church Hall, Butterley Hill, Ripley, Derbyshire, DE5 3LW </div>
 </div>
 Day/time group meets: Tuesday evenings (7.30pm to 9.30pm) from September to May </div>
 </div>
 Cost to attend: The first few sessions are free. Please contact for more information. </div></div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div></div>
 </div>
Ripley Green Garters was formed as a North West Morris dance side in March 1982. After three months of practice sessions a group of ladies who wanted to keep North West Morris dances alive, began, with two of their daughters to dance out some of the dances they had learned. The first dance out was in May 1982. </div>
 </div>
With a small number of the original members along with others who have joined over the past 40 years, we continue to enjoy learning dances to share with the public. </div>
 </div>
We have regular weekly evening practices throughout the autumn and winter months and we go out and about, now mainly in Derbyshire but occasionally further afield for weekend events. </div>
 </div>
Over the years the side has travelled far and wide in Great Britain and has hosted weekends of up to 25 visiting teams giving us an opportunity to show off some Derbyshire traditions and beautiful countryside. </div>
 </div>
Ripley Green Garters have been seen performing in France on five occasions, at an International Festival in Germany and in the Netherlands at Maastricht. These trips combined sharing dances in a sociable way and enjoying excellent holidays together. </div>
 </div>
The dancers could not do any of this without our wonderful and dedicated musicians. </div>
 </div>
Sometimes we leave the clogs, bells, garlands, sticks and costumes at home and head off for walking weekends and theatre trips. </div></div>
