Abandoned vehicles</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 It is the responsibility of the council to deal with, and provide, an abandoned vehicle service within the confines of the city of Salford. </p>
 What legislation does the council use? </h2>
Abandoned vehicles are removed under the Refuse Disposal (Amenity) Act 1978 Sections 3 and 4 and the Removal and Disposal of Vehicles Regulations 1986.</p>
How to report an abandoned vehicle</h2>
We will need the:</p>
location</li>
registration number</li>
car make, model and colour</li>
length of time it has been there (if known)</li>
 Report an abandoned vehicle </p>
