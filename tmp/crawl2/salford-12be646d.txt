Visitor information</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 </p>
Opening times</h2>
The park is open all day everyday, including bank holidays.</p>
Getting here</h2>
Peel Park is conveniently placed in the centre of Salford, meaning we have a number of handy transport links, pedestrian and cycle routes that will allow you to come and visit the park.</p>
 If you're using a stat nav please use the postcode: M5 4WU. This is the postcode for the nearby Salford Museum Gallery car park. You can also use the what3words: driver.remain.beam </p>
</div>
At the main entrance to the front of the park you’ll see the University and Salford Museum and Art gallery. Here you’ll also find the car park. Please note there is a small charge.</p>
Use Transport for Greater Manchester’s Journey Planner to find the best way to get to Peel Park by public transport.</p>
Map of park location</h2>
</div>
More information about this area .</p>
Parking</h2>
You can use the car park in front of Salford Museum and Art Gallery when visiting. It’s a pay and display car park and will cost you £2.50 for up to three hours, £5.50 for 3 to 6 hours and £9 for over 6 hours.</p>
Disabled parking</h3>
There are three disabled parking spaces available in Peel Park which are accessible all the time.</p>

Facilities</h2>
You can find toilets and baby changing facilities at Salford Museum and Art Gallery.</p>
A toilet is also available at organised events, activities and volunteer days at the Park Keepers Base within the park.</p>
