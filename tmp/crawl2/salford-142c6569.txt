Devolved budgets</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Salford City Council is committed to involving local people in decisions about the city. One example of this is the devolved budgets which are allocated to local communities. </p>
In 2026 to 2027, £160,000 has been allocated across the eight neighbourhoods, by head of the population, where decisions are made on how the budget should be spent.</p>
Each neighbourhood elects a sub-group from among its members. They assess applications and make recommendations/decisions about how the funding should be spent. Budget sub-groups include local residents and local councillors. They discuss ways in which the funding could best be used to improve health outcomes for the local population and may approve, refuse or request further information in relation to funding applications.</p>

 Devolved highways budgets </p>
In addition to the devolved budgets, a further £50,000 per year funding for highways and transport improvements is devolved to each neighbourhood. This gives local people an opportunity to have a say on highways improvements and road safety in their areas.</p>
You can apply for funding for your area by downloading and completing the following application form:</p>
 Highways devolved budget funding request form (Microsoft Word format, 30kb) </p>
