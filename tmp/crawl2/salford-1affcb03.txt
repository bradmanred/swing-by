Ordsall Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Opened in 1876, Ordsall Park is the second oldest park in Salford. </p>
The park's open areas of grassland provide excellent opportunities for many types of leisure activities and includes; a bowling green, outdoor gym equipment, picnic area and children's play area.</p>
The Friends of Ordsall Park are a local community group who are actively involved in the future development of the park.</p>
 Address: Trafford Road, Ordsall, M5 3AD
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times : 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Astro turf pitch (all weather)</p></li>
Bowling green and pavilion</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Community garden </p></li>
Football pitches</p></li>
Outdoor gym equipment</p></li>
Nature trail including braille plaques</p></li>
Picnic area </p></li> 
 Friends group: Yes</p>
Ordsall Park proposed refurbishment of the outdoor gym</h2>
Salford City Council ran a consultation on the proposed refurbishment of the outdoor gym, the consultation closed on 21 September 2026. </p>
The outdoor gym is an important feature within the park, providing free and accessible opportunities for people to improve their health and wellbeing. Over time, the existing equipment has become worn and items have been removed as they've reached the end of their useful life, creating an opportunity to refresh and modernise the facility.</p>
Refurbishing the outdoor gym will:</p> 
Provide a safe, modern, and welcoming space for exercise and recreation.</p></li>
Encourage people of all abilities to be more active and spend time outdoors.</p></li>
Improve physical and mental wellbeing by increasing opportunities for free outdoor fitness.</p></li>
Support healthier lifestyles and help reduce barriers to participating in exercise.</p></li>
Enhance the overall park environment, making it a more attractive place to visit.</p></li>
Encourage greater use of the park and provide opportunities for social interaction and community engagement.</p></li>
Protect and improve a valued community asset, ensuring it can continue to be enjoyed for years to come.</p></li> 
By investing in the outdoor gym, we aim to enhance the park's role as a place where residents can come together, stay active, and enjoy high-quality public open space.</p>
Please be aware the Outdoor Gym is designed for adults.</p>
