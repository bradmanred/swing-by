Metal detecting</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Metal detecting is not allowed on Salford City Council land. </p>
This is due to problems associated with digging to recover any finds and damage to wildlife habitat.</p>
There are lots of open spaces in Salford that don't belong to us, so before you set out with your metal detector please get permission from the landowner.</p>
If you are metal detecting please make sure you don't interfere with or cause a nuisance to other land users or cause damage to plants and wildlife. You should hold appropriate public liability insurance and report any significant finds to the landowner.</p>
