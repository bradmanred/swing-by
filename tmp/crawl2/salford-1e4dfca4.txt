Hidden gems walks in Salford</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 There are great opportunities to walk around the city. Salford is made up of several smaller towns and villages which were once surrounded by countryside and you can travel between these places on foot, using footpaths which were once used by people travelling to and from work. </p>
There is still a lot of green space left and these walk leaflets help find out some of these lesser known little gems, their history and how they have helped create the Salford we know today.</p>
We've produced five route maps for you to download below.</p>
