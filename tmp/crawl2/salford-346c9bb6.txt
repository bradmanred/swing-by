Peel Park contact details</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 You’re more than welcome to contact Jess, our park keeper, by sending her an email using the details below. </p>
Park keeper: Jessica Britch</p>
Email:  rangers@salford.gov.uk </p>
See our visitor information page for the park location details.</p>
Leave a review of the park</h2>
Pleas let us know what you think of the park, complete our visitor survey on Survey Monkey.</p>
Have something to report?</h2>
For all non-urgent reports please use one of report it forms.</p>
To report something that needs our urgent attention outside of office hours please call our out of hours contact number 0161 794 8888.</p>
However, if it is a genuine emergency please contact the relevant emergency service, police, ambulance service or the fire services. For non emergencies call 101.</p>
