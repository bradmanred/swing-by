Salford Ranger team</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 The Salford Ranger team work in a number of Salford's parks, country parks and local nature reserves. </p>
Our aim is to make these places somewhere for everyone to enjoy. We do this through providing outdoor education, volunteer days, events, guided walks and nature conservation.</p>
We work closely with friends groups, schools, voluntary organisations, local people and partner organisations to make Salford's parks, countryside and nature reserves safer, cleaner and greener.</p>
Like parks, wildlife and nature?</h2>
Why not take a look at Salford Ranger Team on X  and Salford Ranger Team on Facebook . A great way to find out about things to do, places to explore, and discover Salford's wildlife!</p>
 As part of our ongoing commitment to the environment and biodiversity, we are supporting Plantlife's 'No Mow May' campaign . Find out more. </p>
