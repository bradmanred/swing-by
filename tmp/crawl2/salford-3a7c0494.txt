Dukes Drive</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Dukes Drive is located next to the historic Bridgewater Canal. Situated in the centre of Monton, this green oasis has excellent links to surrounding areas and is linked by canal tow paths and cycle routes (66) to Worsley and further afield. </p>
Dukes Drive is a perfect spot for picnics, dog walking, education and events.</p>
 Address: Dukes Drive, Monton
 Opening times: 24 hour access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Education pond</p></li>
Car park</p></li>
 Permanent orienteer course </p></li> 
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
