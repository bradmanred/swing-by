St Stephen's Gardens</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 St Stephen's Gardens is located between Lamb Lane and St Stephen Street, next to Trinity Way. </p>
The gardens were once a burial ground for St Stephen's Church which closed in 1956.</p>
 Address: Lamb Lane and St Stephen Street
 Classification:  Local park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
