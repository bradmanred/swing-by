St Mary's Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 St Mary's Park lies to the north of Walkden Town Centre and was first opened in 1894. </p>
Among the park's main attractions include; a bowling green, children's play area, veterans' pavilion, five-a-side football pitch and multi-use games area.</p>
 Address: St Mary's Road, Walkden
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Bowling green</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Five-a-side football pitch</p></li>
Multi-use games area</p></li>
Veterans' pavilion</p></li> 
 Friends group:  Yes - Friends of St Mary's Park </p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
