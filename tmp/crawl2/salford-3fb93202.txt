Parking, car parks and bus lanes</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Salford City Council is responsible for most aspects of parking in the city. </p>
This includes the maintenance of pay and display car parks and the enforcement of parking regulations under the provisions of the Traffic Management Act 2004, known as civil parking enforcement.</p>
Bus lanes</h2>
We use cameras to monitor the city's bus lanes for unauthorised use.</p>
Vehicles authorised to use these lanes are buses, hackney carriages, pedal cycles and emergency services vehicles.</p>
If you have received a penalty charge notice for being in a bus lane and wish to view the photographic evidence, please visit our parking portal  and select 'Challenge a PCN' then 'Formal Representation'. Once you've entered your notice details you'll see the images.</p>
