Langworthy Park (Chimney Pot Park)</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Langworthy Park is a small but popular park built on the site of a former reservoir. </p>
Located at the crossroads of Langworthy Road and Liverpool Street, the park is perhaps better known locally as Chimney Pot Park due to its raised position, which provides a magnificent view of the surrounding chimney pots.</p>
Along with attractive floral displays, the parks main attractions include; a crown bowling green, children's play equipment and five-a-side football facilities.</p>
 Address: Langworthy Road, Langworthy, M6 5PP
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Bowling green</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Football pitch (five-a-side)</p></li> 
 Friends group:  Yes</p>
The park is supported by the Friends of Chimney Pot Park. Follow the group's Facebook page  or email focpp@outlook.com .</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
