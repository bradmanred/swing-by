Albert Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Albert Park is an important and valued piece of open space which first opened in 1877. </p>
Among the park's main attractions is a fantastic all-weather astro turf pitch, which is managed by Albert's of Salford and is available for general hire. For more information and to make a booking you can email Aosbookings@yahoo.co.uk or call 0161 792 6076.</p>
Friends of Albert Park is a local community group actively involved in the future development of the park.</p>
 Address : Grecian Street, M7 1JF
 Classification: District Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
