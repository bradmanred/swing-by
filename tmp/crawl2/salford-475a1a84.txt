Lightoaks Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Lightoaks Park is one of the larger parks in Salford and is popular with both local people and visitors. </p>
The park was officially opened in 1925 and is a focal point for the Claremont community, where an active  friends group regularly helps organise a host of community based fun days and activities throughout the year.</p>
 Address: Claremont Road, Claremont, M6 8NP
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
