What is the Litter Picking Locker?</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 The Litter Picking Locker is a dedicated space for community volunteers to access the tools they need to help keep Salford’s parks and green spaces clean and tidy. It’s designed to make volunteering easier and more convenient. </p>
The locker contains essential equipment for litter picking, such as:</p>
Litter pickers</li>
Bin bags</li>
Gloves</li>
Important information</h2>
 The locker does not contain anything of monetary value — only equipment for community litter-picking activities</li>
It’s accessible to registered volunteers only </li>
How to get involved</h2>
If you’re interested in using the Litter Picking Locker and joining our community of volunteers, visit: </p>
 Volunteering with the rangers </li>
 Litter Heroes </li>
