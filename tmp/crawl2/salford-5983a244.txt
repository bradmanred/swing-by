Victoria Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 The  Green Flag award winning Victoria Park, which was first opened in 1897, is one of the most popular parks in Salford. </p>
It boasts a wide range of facilities for all ages to enjoy including; bowling greens, tennis courts, outdoor gym equipment, a children's play area and some of the city's finest floral displays.</p>
The main feature of the park is its beautifully restored bandstand - a grade two listed building which dates back to 1897 to commemorate the 60th year of the reign of Queen Victoria.</p>
 Address: Swinton Hall Road, Swinton, M27 4UR
 Classification: District Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>

Friends of Victoria Park</h2>
We are always interested to hear ideas about how we can improve and develop Victoria Park. Local residents and park users are welcome to get involved via the Friends of Victoria Park.</p>
The friends group is currently looking for new members, if you would like to get involved please email the Salford Ranger Team .</p>
