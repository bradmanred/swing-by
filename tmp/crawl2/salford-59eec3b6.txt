Mandley Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Mandley Park is situated on Leicester Road in Broughton and is set on two distinct levels. </p>
The upper level of the park accommodates children's play areas and outdoor gym equipment whilst the lower level is home to a large picnic area and more children's play facilities.</p>
Access to the upper level is via steps near to the ‘old Library House' or the periphery path.</p>
 Address: Leicester Road, M7 4DU
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Children's play areas</p></li>
Outdoor gym equipment</p></li> 
 Friends group: No</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
