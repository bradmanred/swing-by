Green Grosvenor Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Green Grosvenor Park is a new green space, located on the junction of Clarence Street and Great Clowes Street. </p>
The park was created as part of the redevelopment of Lower Broughton and contains a mix of grassland and woodland along with a children's play area and outdoor gym.</p>
For over 100 years the site was the location of the old Grosvenor Square Park, which was once the centre of the local community.
The park's name was chosen by local residents and school children who felt the name represented both the old and new aspects of the Lower Broughton area.</p>
 Address: Clarence Street, Lower Broughton, M7 1QZ
 Classification: Local Park 
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access
 Facilities: </p> 
Children's play area</p></li>
Outdoor gym equipment</p></li> 
 Friends group:  Yes, see the  Green Grosvenor Park Facebook group .</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
