Parks and open spaces</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 </div>
 With over 60 parks and open spaces to choose from Salford's parks are well worth a visit! </p>
 Watch a video of drone footage and discover how green Salford is.</p>
As well as beautiful scenery, you can find a wealth of fantastic lakes, picturesque landscapes, vibrant wildlife, excellent sports and children's facilities and even the hidden remnants of Salford's industrial past.</p>
 Please note that we do not allow fires, barbecues or overnight camping on our land. Fires can easily damage the ground and set fire to the grass, posing a danger to people and our wildlife. Have a picnic instead. </p>
