Brookhouse Playing Fields</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Brookhouse playing fields are primarily used for Sunday league football. </p>
Proposed play area for Brookhouse Community Centre</h2>
Salford City Council is asking for your thoughts on the new, young children’s play area proposed for Brookhouse Community Centre. You can give your views by completing the questionnaire below. Please be aware that the play area is for children under 14 years old. The deadline to complete the questionnaire is  4 April 2025 .</p>
 Complete the questionnaire </p> 
 Download the plan of a proposed play area (Adobe PDF format, 416kb) </p></li> 
The proposed play area is located next to Brookhouse Community Centre on Buckthorn Lane. Proposals show a range of play equipment that includes:</p> 
A rope pendulum (group) swing</p></li>
Junior Swings with flat, cradle/toddler and inclusive seats</p></li>
Junior climbing frame</p></li>
Two single, spring mobiles</p></li>
Spring mobile suitable for up to four children</p></li>
See-saw</p></li>
Young children’s activity panels and net</p></li>
An agility trail featuring a Balance beam, Rocket Roller and Balance Bridge</p></li>
Inclusive roundabout</p></li>
1.8m high slide</p></li>
Benches</p></li> 
</p>
</div>
The park is also home to a multi-use games area.</p>
 Address: Buckthorne Lane, Patricroft, M30 7QH
 Classification: Local Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Car parking</p></li>
Changing rooms </p></li>
Football pitches</p></li>
Multi-use games area</p></li> 
 Friends group:  No</p>
