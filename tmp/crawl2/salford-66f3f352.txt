Guidance on Private Bookings or Events
Applications for Parks and Open Spaces in Salford
1 Definition of a Private Event
A private event in a public park is defined as any organised activity where access is
restricted to invited individuals, members of a specific group, rather than being open
and accessible to the general public. This includes, but is not limited to, private
birthday parties, weddings, corporate functions, invitation-only gatherings,
group-exclusive celebrations, private community functions, or events that limit
participation to a closed membership.
2 Purpose of Public Parks
Public parks are designated as shared community spaces intended for inclusive
public use. These areas must remain accessible to all members of the public and
cannot be reserved solely for the benefit of one group, organisation, or community.
Event organisers must respect the principle that public parks serve the wider
community, not exclusive interests.
3 Ineligible Bookings
To maintain fairness, inclusivity, and public access, the following applications will not
be accepted:
   •   Private, group-only or membership-restricted events that exclude the general
       public.
   •   Events that seek to limit access to a closed community, organisation, or social
       group.
   •   Bookings that would result in full or partial exclusion of the general public from
       park areas during normal operating hours, unless specifically approved for
       large-scale, publicly accessible events.
4 Eligibility Requirements for Event Approval
Events held in public parks must:
   •   Apply online via the event applications process agreed by Salford City
       Council.
   •   Provide approved levels of public accessibility, whether free or ticketed.
   •   Ensure general park access is preserved where possible.
   •   Demonstrate that the event does not unfairly restrict public use of the space.
   •   Comply with all safety, licensing, and operational requirements set by Salford
       City Council.
   •   In some cases, site fees, refundable bonds, and charges for services will
       apply.
5 Exceptions
Exceptions may be considered only for large-scale events, charitable activities, or
community festivals where public rights of way at the location remain open to the
public or general park users during the event. Exclusive use of parks will not be
permitted. These applications will be assessed on a case-by-case basis by the
council.
