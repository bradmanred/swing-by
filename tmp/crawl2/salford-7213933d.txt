Friends groups</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 There are many different friends groups across the city who are involved in helping to develop and improve Salford's parks for local people. </p>
Current active friends groups include:</p>
 Friends of Boothsbank Park </li>
 Friends of Green Grosvenor Park </li>
 Friends of Irlam and Cadishead Parks </li>
 Friends of Buile Hill </li>
 Monton Village Community Association </li>
 Friends of Lightoaks Park </li>
Clifton Country Park Forum</li>
Friends of Victoria Park</li>
 Friends of Kersal Dale </li>
 Friends of Kersal Moor </li>
 Friends of Blackleach </li>
 Friends of Albert Park </li>
 Worsley Civic Trust </li>
 Friends of Ordsall Park </li>
 Friends of Wardley Park </li>
 Friends of Peel Park  </li>
 Friends of Parr Fold Park </li>
If you are interested in joining a friends group please contact the Salford Ranger Team using the form below.</p>
