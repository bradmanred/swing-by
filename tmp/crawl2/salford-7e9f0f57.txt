Car hire in Salford</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Salford City Council has partnered with Co-wheels, a community interest company (CIC), to offer cheap car hire. </p>
As Co-wheels is a CIC, they reinvest profits into the car club. This gives you the opportunity to use a car only when needed, without the cost of owning, maintaining and running your own vehicle.</p>
Cars can be booked online or through a smart phone and then accessed and used as required. Fuel cards are provided in the cars and you simply get in and drive. </p>
