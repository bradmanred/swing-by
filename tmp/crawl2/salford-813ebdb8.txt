Advertising and sponsorship on the highway</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Have you ever thought about advertising on the highway in Salford? If not then this is your opportunity to reach a wider audience. </p>
We have a number of opportunities for your organisation to promote itself within the city. For further information, please email adsconcession@salford.gov.uk .</p>
Salford manage a number of platforms to support local advertising opportunities in the city, which include billboards, street lighting columns, bridges, roundabouts, city boundary/gateway signs as well as pavement advertising.</p>
Pavement advertising units provided by Adshel</h2>
Adshel is a division of Clear Channel, and specialises in the management of the six sheet advertising units located on the city's footways.</p>
