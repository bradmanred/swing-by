Outdoor gyms</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Pay a visit to the gym - outdoors!</h2>
Salford has 16 outdoor gyms in parks across the city. Suitable for adults and children aged 12 years and above, they are a great way to improve your health and fitness - for free !</p>
Jointly funded by the council and NHS Salford, each outdoor gym has a mixture of easy to use fitness equipment including rowing machines, steppers and cross trainers.</p>
Where are they located?</h2>
Outdoor gyms can be found in the following parks:</p> 
 Albert Park </p></li>
 Bolton Road Playing Fields </p></li>
 Buile Hill Park </p></li>
 Cadishead Park </p></li>
 Dolbey Park </p></li>
 Eccles Recreation Ground </p></li>
 Green Grosvenor Park </p></li>
 Islington Park </p></li>
 Lightoaks Park </p></li>
 Mandley Park </p></li>
 Ordsall Park </p></li>
 Parr Fold Park </p></li>
 Peel Park (Little Hulton) </p></li>
 Princes Park </p></li>
 St Simon Street Play Area (Irwell Riverside) </p></li>
 Victoria Park </p></li>
 Whittle Street Playing Area </p></li>
 Winton Park </p></li> 
All outdoor gyms are inspected regularly to ensure they are safe for use. However, if you are unsure about undertaking physical exercise, please consult a doctor beforehand.</p>
