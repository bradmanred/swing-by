Clowes Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Situated next to New Hall Road, Clowes Park is a popular park with a central lake that includes several fantastic viewing points. </p>
 Clowes Park updates and activities </p>
To the north of the site is a large play area and a small area of deciduous woodland. </p>
 Address: New Hall Road, M7 4HS
 Classification: Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Car parking (on-street) </p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Lake/pond (please note that fishing is not allowed)</p></li> 
 Friends group: A new joint working group is in the early stages of development</p>
 Life for a Life memorial forest </p>
Located in Clowes Park, the Life for a Life memorial forest opened in 2016 with the purpose of memorial tree planting.</p>
As you enter the memorial forest you are greeted by a windfall orchard that is made up of a selection of apple trees. The orchard was planted by local schools in conjunction with Manchester City of Trees as part of their 'trees for learning' programme.</p>
A Memorial Stone was installed by Life for Life in celebration of Broughton House Home for Ex-service Personal's centenary. The home is just 250 metres away from the memorial forest and continues to serve Greater Manchester's veterans.</p>
For more information, please contact eastsalford.nmt@salford.gov.uk </p>
