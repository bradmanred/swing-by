Journey planning</h2>
Visit the  TFGM journey planning homepage  for information on journey planning,the latest service changes and timetable information. Alternatively you can call 0871 200 22 33 for travel information.</p>
Travelshops</h2>
Located at many bus stations throughout Greater Manchester,  Travelshops provide detailed, impartial public transport information. As well as free leaflets, maps and timetables, tickets can be purchased for bus, coach, train and tram services.</p>
Concessionary travel passes</h2>
For information about concessionary passes please visit the  concessionary bus passes  page for more information.</p>
