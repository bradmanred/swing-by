Monton Green</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Monton Green is a key feature of the Worsley area and is a focal point for community activities. </p>
Since 1998, the rose, shrub and flower beds have been maintained by community volunteers with financial support from local businesses.</p>
Next to the green is Boddan Lodge bowling green - a popular bowling club which is run by its members.</p>
 Address: Monton Green, Eccles, M30 9LE
 Classification: Local Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access
 Facilities: </p> 
Bowling green</p></li>
Veteran's pavilion</p></li>
Public shelter</p></li> 
 Friends group : No</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
