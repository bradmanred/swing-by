Patting Close Play Area</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Patting Close Play Area is located within a residential development on Patting Close in Irlam and is a large piece of open space which also links to Prince's Park. </p>
Patting Close Play Area proposed refurbishment</h2>
Salford City Council is asking for your thoughts on the proposed refurbishment of the children's play area. You can give your views by completing the questionnaire below. </p>
 Questionnaire for refurbishment of the children's play equipment at Patting Close Play Area </p>
The deadline to complete the questionnaire is 12 June 2026.</p>
The new play equipment will be placed within the footprint of the current fenced play area and offers a range of play opportunities for children of different ages and abilities to play together. The existing play equipment will be removed with the exception of the young children's multi-play unit. New equipment will include a toddler activity unit, wheelchair accessible roundabout, springies, swings fitted with seats suitable for junior, toddler and disabled children, a large climbing tower complete with a slide and climbing net and a new basket swing. The existing basketball posts will be replaced and upgraded to a basketball court complete with a painted, non-slip surface and line markings to Basketball England recommendations.</p>
