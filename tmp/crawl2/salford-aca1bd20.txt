Request a temporary road closure</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Sometimes works on the highway (including building construction work), large deliveries or even certain sporting events require a road to be closed temporarily to general traffic. </p>
Such closures will require a traffic regulation order to be published if more than five days. All costs involved for both the closure and diversion signing have to be met by the person requesting the closure.</p>
As there is a legal and consultative process that needs to be undertaken, at least eight weeks' notice is required - for some closures this period may be up to three months in order to publicise a diversion route properly.</p>
 Temporary traffic regulation notice/order (TTRN/TTRO): </p> 
Emergency 21-day TTRN: £480 – lead time not applicable </p></li>
Up to five days TTRN: £480 – lead time six weeks</p></li>
TTRO for planned works over five days: £2,400 – lead time 12 weeks</p></li>
Street party for Salford residents – lead time six weeks</p></li> 
</p>
