Report a highways problem</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Salford City Council maintains adopted roads and pavements across the city. </p>
You can use this service to report certain defects with the road or pavement.</p>
What you can report?</h2>
Please use this form to report:</p> 
 Collapse or subsidence – an area of road or pavement that has collapsed or sunk</p></li>
 Missing or damaged manhole cover or grid </p></li>
 Dangerous or damaged paving slab </p></li>
 Dangerous or damaged kerbstone </p></li> 
Before reporting a problem</h2>
Please check the Highway Status Map below to confirm whether the road or pavement is maintained by Salford City Council.</p>
Routes shown in green are maintained by Salford City Council. Routes shown in red are not maintained by the council.</p>
 View the highway status map </p>
When reporting a problem, please provide the exact location and as much detail as possible. This will help us identify and assess the defect.</p>
Report a highway problem</h2>
 Report a highways issue </p>
Once your report has been submitted, you will receive a reference number which can be used to track the progress of your enquiry.</p>
