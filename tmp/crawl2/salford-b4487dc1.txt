Roe Green Play Area</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Roe Green is a suburb of Worsley and was once a hamlet built around the ancient village green. </p>
Today it is the largest of Salford's conservation areas, selected because it has the character of a village green - an unusual feature in the surrounding industrialised settlements.</p>
A children's play area is situated to the north of the site.</p>
 Address: Roe Green, Worsley
 Classification: Local Equiped Area of Play (LEAP)
 Opening Times : 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Children's play area</p></li> 
 Friends Group: No</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
