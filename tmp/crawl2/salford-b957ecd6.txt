Charlestown Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Charlestown Park is a small park located in the Charlestown area of Salford. </p>
The park provides a children's play area and a number of five-a-side football pitches. </p>
An area of the park called 'Amber's Garden' is dedicated to the memory of Amber Lok, a 13-year-old girl who tragically passed away in 2005 after being knocked over by a car as she tried to cross Langley Road.</p>
 Address: Douglas Green, Irwell Riverside, M6 6ES
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access
 Facilities: </p> 
Children's play area</p></li>
Football pitches (five-a-side)</p></li>
Memorial garden </p></li> 
 Friends group:  No</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
