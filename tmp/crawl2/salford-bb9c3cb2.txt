Blackfriars Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 One of Salford's smaller parks, Blackfriars is popular with many local residents as it provides an attractive location to get some fresh air and take gentle exercise. </p>
 Blackfriars Park updates and activities </p>
It is equipped with seating areas, a multi-use games area and a small playing field.</p>
 Address: Mount Street, M3 6LU
 Classification: Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Football pitch</p></li>
Multi-use games area</p></li> 
 Friends group: No</p>
Read about the history of Blackfriars Park , including some information about the history of the name of the park.</p>
