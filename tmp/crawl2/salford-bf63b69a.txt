Winton Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Green Flag  award winning Winton Park was first opened in 1906 and has recently undergone a major refurbishment which included the design of a new central grassed area, installation of public toilets and improvements to the bowler's pavilion. </p>
The park also features a bowling green, children's play area, multi-use games area and outdoor gym equipment.</p>
The  Salford Ranger Team with support from an active  friends group provide an exciting programme of events and activities  throughout the year.</p>
Schools and community groups are welcome to use the site and a range of  outdoor education  and  volunteering opportunities can be organised to suit different needs.</p>
 Address: Dover Street, Winton, M30 8LW 
 Classification: District Park
 Opening times:  Dawn to dusk </p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Bird feeding</p></li>
Bowling green</p></li>
Car parking</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Multi-use games area</p></li>
Outdoor gym equipment</p></li>
Toilets (subject to availability)</p></li>
Nature and reading area</p></li>
Tree trail</p></li> 
 Friends group: Yes</p>
 </p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
