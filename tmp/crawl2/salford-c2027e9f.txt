Discover our green space</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Discover our green and luscious secret - 60% of the city is green space. Amazing isn't it? As well as farmland and sports pitches, we have ten green flag parks and open spaces, five local nature reserves, and over 20 public parks so there's plenty to keep you occupied. </p>
 </div>
<!-- version 2 -->
 Salford parks </p>
You can explore one of the beautiful country parks such as  Clifton Country Park's  meadows, play hide and seek in  Worsley Woods , turn cartwheels in the fields at  Blackleach Country Park , enjoy a brass band concert with an ice cream in the summer at Victoria Park. If you prefer a more wild approach, try some real wilderness at Chat Moss.</p>
We’re also very proud of the RHS Garden Bridgewater in Worsley.</p>
