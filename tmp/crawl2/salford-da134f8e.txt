Oakwood Park</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Oakwood Park is located on Swinton Park Road and is home to Langworthy Reds ALFC . </p>
The mansion house located in the park, which was given to the city by the Pilkington family, is now the Ingleside Residential Hostel.</p>
A children's play area is also located within the park.</p>
 Address: Swinton Park Road, M6 7WR
 Classification:  Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Rubgy pitches</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li> 
 Friends group : No</p>
Oakwood Park proposed refurbishment of the young children's play area</h2>
Salford City Council is asking for your thoughts on the proposed refurbishment of the young children's play area. You can give your views by completing the questionnaire below.</p>
 Questionnaire for refurbishment of the young children's play equipment at Oakwood Park </p>
The deadline to complete the questionnaire is 12 June 2026.</p>
The new play equipment will be placed within the footprint of the current fenced play area  and offers a range of play opportunities for young children of different abilities to play together. The majority of the existing play equipment will be removed except for the wheelchair accessible roundabout. New equipment will include swings suitable for toddlers and young children including disabled children. An inclusive multi-play unit and a nature themed springy and see-saw will offer a range of play opportunities for young children. New, timber seating will be provided and the black, rubber safety surface replaced with a green, artificial grass type surface.</p>
