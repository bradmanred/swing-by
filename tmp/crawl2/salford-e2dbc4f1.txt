Road safety</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 </div>
 Salford City Council Road Safety Service are committed to improving safety on the roads in the city through a combined approach of improving the road network and infrastructure, providing training and education as well as supporting regional and national publicity campaigns to improve awareness around key areas of road safety. </p>
Working together with the community, the aim of the group is to make our roads safer, to reduce road injuries and to promote healthy transport opportunities such as walking and cycling.</p>
The team can provide road safety training, resources and advice to pre-school children, primary and secondary schools, colleges and youth, community or voluntary groups. For more information please contact the Salford City Council Road Safety Team - email  roadsafety@salford.gov.uk </p>
