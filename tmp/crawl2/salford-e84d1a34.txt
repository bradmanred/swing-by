Grounds maintenance service standards</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Our role is to deliver a cleaner, greener and sustainable environment.</p>
Our service standards are the procedures to which our parks and green space team works to and the levels of service to which we aspire to.</p>
They cover the maintenance and management of parks, green spaces and play facilities within Salford. Our service standards are in line with Customer Service Excellence and the Green Flag standards , which we have adopted and work towards.</p>
Service standards</h2> 
Play areas will be inspected at least once per week and dangerous equipment will be removed within 24 hours.</p></li>
Grass will be cut to agreed specification during the mowing season March to October.</p></li>
Paths and walkways will be cleared of all grass cuttings and debris following mowing.</p></li>
Hedges will be cut back at least once per year. Grassed areas inaccessible to mowing, shall be trimmed as required.</p></li>
Grassed areas will be chemically edged at least once per year.</p></li>
Reports of dangerous trees will be inspected and rectified within 24 hours.</p></li>
Weeds on beds, shrubberies and paths, will be sprayed and controlled as required.</p></li>
Grass will be litter picked prior to mowing.</p></li>
We aim to deal with 85% of requests for service within target.</p></li> 
Meeting our standards</h2>
Requests for service</h3> 
2016/17 of 874 requests - 76% were completed on target</p></li>
2017/18 of 527 requests - 79% were completed on target</p></li>
2018/19 of 1,684 requests received 75% have been completed within target</p></li>
2019/20 (April to November) 2,645 requests received (combined Street Cleansing, Grounds Maintenance and Arboriculture) 100% acknowledged within target, 96% completed within target</p></li>

Partnerships</h2>
To help us provide the best possible service, we work with partners who share an interest in grounds maintenance, these include:</p> 
Friends of the parks</p></li>
Salford Allotments Federation</p></li>
Association of Greater Manchester Authorities (AGMA)</p></li>
Myerscough College</p></li>
Reed Employment Agency</p></li>
APSE</p></li>
YMCA training</p></li> 
Our grounds maintenance staff also work with other groups, these include:</p> 
youth offenders</p></li>
people with special needs</p></li>
people on incapacity benefit</p></li>
