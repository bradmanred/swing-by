Moorside Park and Beech Farm</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Moorside Park and Beech Farm are separated by a small footpath that runs between Victoria Lane and Browning Road. </p>
Thank you for having your say</h2>
We ran a consultation on proposals for Moorside Park and Beech Farm which ended on 24 July 2026. This followed large amount of damage to the grass football pitches. We asked for your views on measures to prevent this damage and restrict unauthorised vehicle access. The results from the consultation can be viewed below.</p>
An arboricultural impact statement is currently being produced, a copy of which will be provided on this web page. </p> 
 Proposals for Moorside Park and Beech Farm (Adobe PDF format, 503kb) </p></li>
 Consultation results for Moorside Park and Beech Farm (Adobe PDF format, 224kb) </p></li> 
</p>
</div>
Moorside provides opportunities to take part in a variety of leisure opportunities including both bowling and football.</p>
Beech Farm is primarily used for organised football and is home to both Moorside Rangers and Beechfield United football teams.</p>
The park also has an active friends group .</p>
 Address: Victoria Lane, Swinton, M27 9LP
 Classification: Neighbourhood Park
 Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access</p>
 Facilities: </p> 
Astro turf pitches (all weather)</p></li>
Bowling green</p></li>
Children's play area</p></li>
Car parking</p></li>
Changing rooms</p></li>
Football pitches</p></li>
Skate park </p></li>
Toilets (dependent on opening times)</p></li> 
 Friends group: Yes</p>
If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page .</p>
