Roadworks and road closures</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
 Sometimes, works on the highway, or some large deliveries, require a road to be closed temporarily to general traffic. Such closures require a traffic regulation order issued by the highway authority. </p>
All costs involved for both the closure and diversion signing will have to be met by the person requesting the closure.</p>
As there is a legal and consultative process to be undertaken, at least eight weeks' notice of closure is required (in some cases up to three months will be needed in order to publicise a diversionary route effectively).</p>
These pages provide information on types of closure that are possible and the steps that need to be taken to effect them.</p>
