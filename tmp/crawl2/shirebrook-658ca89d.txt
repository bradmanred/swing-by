Roseland Park & Crematorium - Open Day</h1>
 </div>
Roseland Park & Crematorium are having an open day on Saturday the 3rd of October 2026, from 10:00am till 2:00pm.</p>
Join the team at the new facility for a behind the scenes look at the crematorium, learning about the cremation process along the way.</p>
You will be able to meet the team, who will be on hand to answer your questions and show you around the crematorium as well as the Gardens of Remembrance.</p>
After the tour, complimentary refreshments in the Chapel View Team Room will be available.</p></div>
