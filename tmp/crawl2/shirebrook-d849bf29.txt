Public Consultation - Shirebrook Village Hall</h2>
Shirebrook Village Hall is located on Park Road next to the Recreation Ground and is owned and managed on behalf of the Community by Shirebrook Town Council.</p>
Built in the early 1970's the hall has played a central part in the social and recreational life of the Shirebrook Community for the best part of 50 years.</p>
However more recently, changes in community preferences and the availability of other excellent local social, leisure and community facilities (for example Shirebrook Miner's Welfare and Shirebrook Leisure Centre) we have seen a year-on-year decline in the demand for the village hall.</p>
As a consequence, the amount of revenue generated has also declined year-on-year and is at a point where the cost to the local taxpayer does not represent good value for money.</p>
In response, the Town Council held a Public Consultation event on the 21st September 2026 at which several ideas for the reimaging of this facility were put forward by those public in attendance including offering a long-term lease to a local Cheerleading business on a commercial basis significantly reducing operating costs and associated building liabilities to the taxpayer.</p>
Before any decisions are made by the Town Council we would welcome approaches by any other Groups, Clubs/Societies or other stakeholders who may also be interested in using this space commercially either exclusively or on a shared basis. As such we invite expressions of interest together with an outline business plan explaining your ideas.</p>
 The Council aims to have any new arrangements in place by the 31st December 2026 subject to formal approval by the Town Council. </p>
 Please submit all comments, expressions of interest and views to enquiries@shirebrooktowncouncil.gov.uk </p>
 Consultation period: 16th of October 2026 to 1st of November 2026 </p>
