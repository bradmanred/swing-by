 Playgrounds
 </h2>
Thurrock has over 70 playgrounds and recreation grounds that are free for all to use.</p>
Much of the equipment in our play spaces is designed to be used by children of all abilities. Grays Beach Riverside Park and Dilkes Park has equipment designed specifically for wheelchair users.</p>
We have outdoor gyms that are free to use 365 days a year.</p>
The features of our largest playgrounds are listed below.</p>
