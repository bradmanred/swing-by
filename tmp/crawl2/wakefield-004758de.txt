Parking permits</h1>
Residential Parking Permits</h2>
Residential Parking Schemes are in place to help residents park near their homes. They are used where parking is limited, due to non-residents parking their vehicles.</p>
They are not used to reserve spaces or to limit the number of vehicles any individual can have. They are also not there to create conflict between neighbours.</p>
If you live in a permit area, you will need to apply for the relevant permits to park on the road where you live.</p>
Resident/Visitor Parking Permits</h2>
Resident Parking Permits are issued to specific vehicles belonging to residents. Visitor Parking Permits are issued to residents to be used by their visitors. Subject to conditions.</p>
How do I apply for Resident/Visitor Parking Permits?</h3>
 Submit a Resident/Visitor Permit Application form and provide the required supporting documentation. Resident Parking Permits and the initial Visitor Parking Permits are free. There is a £5 charge to replace a lost or damaged visitor permit. </p>
Business Parking Permits</h2>
Business Parking Permits are for eligible businesses located in a residential permit scheme. They are permits that can be given to a motorist visiting a business. Subject to conditions.</p>
How do I apply for a Business Parking Permit?</h3>
 Submit a Business Permit application form and provide the required supporting documentation. There is no charge for a Business parking permit.</p>
Season tickets (formally long stay parking permits)</h2>
Season tickets are available to purchase for use on long stay parking areas throughout the district (not including Merchant Gate). These permits are “virtual” and are provided through MiPermit . We no longer offer a paper permit to display in a vehicle. The MiPermit system alerts enforcement staff when a vehicle has a valid “virtual” parking permit in operation.</p>
