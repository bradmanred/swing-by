Report Blue Badge fraud</h1>
 Before you start your request
 </h2>
 You will need to tell us: </p>
the name on the badge, the badge's serial number, and the person using it</li>
the location of the vehicle, its registration, and the time of day it can be found there</li>
 </p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </picture>
 </div>
 We take allegations of Blue Badge fraud seriously. </p>
It is a criminal offence for non-disabled people to use a blue badge and you can be fined up to £1,000.</div>
It is a criminal offence to drive a car that is displaying a Blue Badge without the badge holder in the vehicle.
</div>
Report Blue Badge fraud</h2>
If you wish to report Blue Badge fraud, please fill in our online form, you will receive an application number. If you provide us with an email address an automated confirmation of your request will be sent to you.</p>
 Please also note that this form can be filled out anonymously. </p>
