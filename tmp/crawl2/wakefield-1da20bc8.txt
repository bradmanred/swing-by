Park Rangers</h1>
The Wakefield Park Rangers maintain district and town parks to meet Green Flag standards.</p>
Maintenance responsibilities</h2>
Litter</h3>
We empty bins and litter pick twice a week from March to October and as necessary during winter.</p>
Grass cutting</h3>
We help to cut grass in high footfall areas such as around playgrounds and also assist with grass cutting in wider park.</p>
Floral displays</h3>
We will maintain bedding and herbaceous plants if there are any.</p>
Planting</h3>
We help plant trees in winter and create meadows and enhance existing natural areas for wildlife and flowers.</p>
Ponds and water features</h3>
We do a weekly schedule of pond/water feature cleansing in the three destination parks:</p>
Thornes</li>
Pontefract</li>
Vale Head</li>
Shrubs and hedges</h3>
We maintain shrub beds and hedges from August to March.</p>
General maintenance</h3>
We also inspect, maintain, clean, and do minor repairs of park furniture each week such as:</p>
signs</li>
benches</li>
gates</li>
fences</li>
We seek out and remove graffiti and report anti-social behaviour.</p>
We perform small repairs and maintenance tasks like painting park buildings and pruning trees using hand tools from the ground.</p>
Community assistance</h3>
We provide support and assistance for local parks volunteer initiatives. Contact us at Parksandgreenspaces@wakefield.gov.uk to request assistance with your project.</p>
Report a problem</h3>
You can report the following problems that you spot in the parks and green spaces:</p>
trees in parks</li>
vandalism or damage</li>
litter or fly tipping</li>
dog fouling</li>
graffiti</li>
grass cutting or weeds</li>
How to report it</h3>
Use MyAccount to conveniently report a problem online day or night.</p>
Or, email us at customerservices@wakefield.gov.uk </p>
Emergencies or dangerous problems</h3>
For emergencies or dangerous problems (e.g., a tree that is in danger of falling in a park or a broken piece of play equipment) contact us on 0345 8 506 506 (24 hours).</p>
