Parking policy, parking and traffic restrictions</h1>
Parking restrictions</h2>
Parking restrictions take many forms and help maintain safe traffic flows around the highway network.</p>
Double yellow lines prevent parking or waiting for 24 hours a day with single yellow lines doing the same for part of the day.</p>
Other restrictions can limit the length of time a vehicle can be parked or restrict usage to such as loading only or for blue badge holders.</p>
All restrictions require what is termed a Traffic Regulation Order (TRO) which is a statutory process that can take around 18 months to complete.</p>
This timescale limits the number that can be introduced each year with around a dozen or so completed annually.</p>
Consequently a priority system is used to determine those schemes in the greatest need.</p>
Traffic movement restrictions</h2>
These come in various forms such as turn bans, permanent road closures or HGV restrictions.</p>
However, HGV restrictions cannot prevent access to properties within restricted routes. Once again movement restrictions are governed by Traffic Regulation Orders and fall into the same priority system.</p>
To request an investigation into any of these services please contact us.</p>
