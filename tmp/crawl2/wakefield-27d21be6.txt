Suspending parking bays and getting permission to park where there are parking restrictions in operation</h1>
Suspending an on-street parking bay</h2>
We may suspend the use of on-street parking bays for specific reasons. We will consider applications for:</p>
building operations</div>
</li>
domestic removals</div>
</li>
weddings, funerals or special events</div>
</li>
delivery of goods taking longer than the specified time</div>
</li>
filming (essential vehicles only such as lighting generators, prop vehicles)</div>
</li>
Charges apply which are detailed on the application.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>

Getting permission to park where there are parking restrictions in operation</h2>
With a parking dispensation you can park for a limited time when there are parking restrictions in operation.</p>
For example, a parking dispensation allows a specific vehicle to park temporarily on single or double yellow lines during restricted hours in a particular location to undertake essential works where a vehicle is needed.</p>
Permission to park on yellow lines is called a dispensation.</p>
A permit is issued which must be displayed clearly in the vehicle.</p>
Charges apply which are detailed on the application.</p>
