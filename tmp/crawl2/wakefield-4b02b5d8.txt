Other information</h2>
Autumn leaves</h3>
Falling leaves in autumn are part of the natural cycle and can't be avoided by pruning.</p>
Extra teams work to clear leaves from roads and paths through autumn.</p>
In parks and green spaces leaves that fall on paths and paved areas are regularly cleared. Leaves on grass or hedges will be left unless they could cause damage to the area.</p>
