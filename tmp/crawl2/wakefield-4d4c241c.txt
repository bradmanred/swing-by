Free parking</li>
Network of paths through the park for walking and cycling</li>

Nature conservation</h2>
This is a nature reserve that is managed to protect and improve habitats for wildlife. The wildlife changes with the seasons, and visitors can see different animals like the Cuckoo in the spring and Grass Snakes basking in the summer sun.</p>
The park boasts a variety of wildflowers including Common Spotted and Southern Marsh Orchids.</p></p>

Volunteers</h2>
The Friends of Walton Colliery Nature Park assist the Countryside Officers  to manage and maintain the site. They have previously done site clean-ups and installed a way-marked trail. If you're interested in volunteering, you can join them.</p>
 Visit the Friends of Walton Colliery Nature Park Facebook page for further details about the Friends group. If you are interested in becoming a member, please contact friendswcnp@gmail.com .</p>
If you are interested in local volunteering opportunities on this and other sites please get in touch.</p>
