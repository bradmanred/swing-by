Story trails</h1>
Room on the Broom Adventure Trail at Anglers Country Park</h2>
Haw Park Lane
Wakefield
WF4 2EE</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
The Room on the Broom Adventure Trail is a magical experience for all the family! With new statues and lots of activity to keep you entertained along the way, this is something you do not want to miss out on.</p>
Find footsteps, activities and key scenes from one of your favourite stories as some familiar characters lead you on your way. Why not add a little extra fun into your adventure by grabbing a Room on the Broom trail activity pack from the Visitor Centre. Packs can be purchased for £2.</p>
The trail is a circular route around the lake and is accessible by foot, cycle, buggy or wheelchair.</p>

Gnome Roam at Newmillerdam</h2>
Barnsley Road
Newmillerdam
Wakefield
WF2 6QP</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Have you ever wondered what Gnomes do and don’t like? Take you little ones on the Gnome Roam through Newmillerdam!</p>
This simple walking trail encourages children to use signs and clues to spot carved statues of gnomes and wildlife. And you may even spot a gigantic bear!!!!</p>
*The statues are due to be replaced this year. Follow Wakefield Wellbeing Facebook for updates on the trails improvements.</p>

Blown Away at Pugneys Country Park</h2>
Asdale Road
Denby Dale Road
Wakefield
WF2 7EQ</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Pugneys Country Park is ready to blow you away, with our improved adventure trail. The original Blown Away story has been rewritten for younger children. Windy The Dog is now the main character, and let me tell you he’s a cheeky little scamp!! (or dog) First grab your Windy The Dog Map from reception.</p>
Read the story, complete the activities and see if you can find all 5 sculptures of him as you walk around. What cheeky things has he been up to?
What are you waiting for? Start at grandma’s car and explore all the fun activities along the way, we can’t wait to hear what music you will make.</p>
Take in the view of Sandal Castle from the boat, or use your imagination to play pirate games. Turn over the map to continue your day and find out what else you can do at Pugneys.</p>

Pete’s Peregrine Trail</h2>
Pontefract Park
Park Road
Pontefract
WF8 4QD</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Join Pete the Peregrine on a high-flying adventure around Pontefract Park! As one of the fastest birds in the world, Pete will guide you on a fun and educational trail full of curious facts, challenges, and surprises.</p>
Spot hidden clues, learn about local wildlife, and stretch your wings as you explore the great outdoors with family and friends.</p>
Keep your eyes peeled, Pete’s got plenty to show you!</p>

Robin Ranger Story Trail</h2>
Robin Ranger needs your help! The Planet Punishers are hiding across Fitzwilliam, Havercroft, Hemsworth and Ryhill and they’re up to no good! Follow the trail, find the special bird boxes, and unlock the secret clues inside.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </picture>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Each clue helps you crack the code and track down the Planet Punishers one by one. It’s fun, free and full of adventure, every step you take helps Robin save the planet! Are you ready to become a planet saving code breaker?</p>
Trail maps can be picked up from local venues across the area, including Rycroft Cand LC, Solid Rock Church, Havercroft Family Hub, Havercroft Community Centre, Hemsworth Library, Hemsworth Water Park Café (TBC), Fitzwilliam Red Roof, and the Fitzwilliam Centre.</p>
