The Big Parks and Play Area Project</h1>
Over the next two years, parks and play areas in the district will see a range of improvements.</p>
Some parks will see small changes. Like new fencing or extra seating.</p>
Others will have major revamps. Like new play equipment, better inclusive features, and more greenery.</p>
