Wakefield Council Allotments</h1>
Renting an allotment is an inexpensive way of getting your hands on valuable gardening space.</p>
With an allotment you get to produce cheap home-grown organic food, with the knowledge that it is fresher than anything you can buy from a shop. Nothing compares to the taste of your own freshly picked tomatoes: full of vitamins, free from harmful chemicals and not genetically modified.</p>
Wakefield Council controls 60 sites across the District.</p>
If you are interested in a site other than those listed on this website you must contact the site operator for further information.</p>
