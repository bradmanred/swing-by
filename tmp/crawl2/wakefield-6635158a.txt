                     Heath Common Byelaws
Made by the Rural District Council of Wakefield, in pursuance of a
scheme made by the Rural District Council of Wakefield and approved
by the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries under Section 1 (1) of the
Commons Act 1899, with respect to Heath Common.
1    Throughout these Byelaws the expression “the Council” means the
     Rural District Council of Wakefield; the expression “the Common”
     means the piece of land with ponds, streams, paths and roads
     thereon, commonly known as Heath Common, situate in the Parish
     of Warmfield – cum – Heath, in the County of Yorkshire (W.R.) and
     referred to as the “the common” in the Scheme approved by the
     Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries under the Commons Act
     1899, on the 2nd day of February 1955, and the expression “the
     Scheme” means the aforesaid Scheme.
2    An act necessary to the proper execution of his duty on the
     Common by an Officer of the Council, or any person or servant of
     any person employed by the Council shall not be deemed an
     offence by these Byelaws.
3    No person shall without lawful authority camp on the Common.
4    No person shall without lawful authority shoot or chase game or
     other animals on the Common.
5    No person shall without lawful authority turn out or permit to graze
     on the Common, any cattle, sheep or other animals.
6    No person, shall without lawful authority dig or take any turf, sods,
     gravel, clay, sand, or other substance on or from any part of the
     Common.
7    No person shall without lawful authority, cut, fell or injure any
     gorse, timber or other tree, shrub, brushwood or other plant
     growing on the Common.
8    No person shall remove or carelessly or negligently injure or
     deface any fence, seat, barrier, or other property maintained on
     the Common by the Council.
9    No person shall without lawful authority draw, drive or place on the
     Common any carriage, cart, caravan, truck, motorcycle or other
     vehicle, or any aircraft (except in the case of accident or other
     sufficient cause).
10   No person shall without lawful authority light any fire on the
     Common.
11   A person shall not wilfully obstruct disturb or annoy any other
     person in the proper use of the Common.
12   Every person who shall offend against any of the foregoing
     Byelaws shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not
     exceeding Twenty Pounds.
13   Any Officer of the Council after due warning may remove from the
     Common any vehicle or animal drawn, driven or placed, or any
     structure erected or placed thereon in contravention of the
     Scheme, or any byelaw made under the Scheme, or any person
     who within his view infringes any such byelaw, or any provision of
     the Vagrancy Acts.
