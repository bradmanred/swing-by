New play area for Ferrybridge children</h1>
Wednesday, January 21, 2026</p>
Wakefield Council has completed improvements to Ferrybridge play area. Providing a fun new play area for children.</p>
The project has been developed in partnership with local ward members. And is part of the Council’s commitment to improving play areas and parks across the district.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
“It’s great that the work has now been completed at Ferrybridge Park. Children will be able to enjoy it for many years to come.</p>
“The new play area provides a welcoming space for local families. It has been designed to spark imagination for younger children and provide more adventurous challenges for older ones.”</p></p>
 </div>
Cllr George Ayre</p>
Cabinet Member for Environment and Civic Services</p>
 </div>
 </picture>
 </div>
The project has been designed to promote creativity and imagination. Which supports both physical and mental development in children.</p>
It includes several inclusive play features for those with restricted mobility, sensory impairment and neuro-developmental conditions. Including:</p>
low level, easy access points to help children with restricted mobility</li>
the use of contrasting colours to aid visually impaired children in navigating getting on and off the equipment</li>
single user activities to allow children to play independently and to reduce sensory overload</li>
Brand new for the park is a 20m single zipline, allowing children to glide through the air. And helping to develop skills like balance and coordination.</p>
Other upgrades include new slides, a variety of swings, a roundabout and two climbing towers suitable for different age ranges. Children will be able to enjoy a wide range of play experiences including climbing, swinging, gliding, sliding, traversing, spinning, rocking, and rotating.</p>
