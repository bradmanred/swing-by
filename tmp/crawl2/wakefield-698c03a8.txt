​​​​Historical Interest</h2>
The Pine and Larch contained within the woodland were commercially planted. Part of the wood was felled by Italian prisoners of war in World War Two.</p>
Nature Conservation</h2>
The site was designated a local nature reserve in 1992.</p>
This mixed woodland attracts a variety of wildlife; species of particular interest include:</p>
both the Greater Spotted and Green Woodpeckers</li>
Grey Heron</li>
Sparrow-hawk</li>
Kestrel</li>
In the woodland, watch out for Grey Squirrel playing in the trees. In spring you can enjoy the Bluebells before the trees come into full leaf.</p>
