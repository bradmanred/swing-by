Trees enquiries</h1>
Wakefield council manages trees on various sites across the district. If you have any concerns about a tree then please see the below on how to report this.</p>
You will need to tell us:</p>
the location of the tree using a map or what 3 words </li>
what type of issue the tree is causing</li>
 </div>
 </div>

If you wish to make any type of tree enquiry, please fill in our online form. You will receive an application number. If you provide us with an email address an automated confirmation of your request will be sent to you.</p>
Tree emergencies</h2>
Wakefield Council will quickly respond to trees in immediate danger or needing urgent attention. We aim to attend within 24 hours of us being informed.</p>
General tree enquiry</h2>
For non-emergencies, Wakefield Council will normally carry out a tree inspection. This will take place within ten working days of us being informed.</p>
Private trees</h2>
In relation to any private trees please see the below before contacting the council.</p>
Many trees in the district belong to private landowners such as farmers, businesses and the general public.</p>
Landowners have a duty of care to ensure that any reasonably foreseeable cause of harm or damage from trees on their land is abated.</p>
The Occupiers’ Liability Act 1957 and Occupiers Liability Act 1984 applies to private landowners. The Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974 applies to businesses.</p>
 It should be noted that some trees are protected, and therefore any proposed works would need permission. Details about applying to carry out works to trees with a Tree Preservation Order or within a Conservation Area can be found on Protected Trees . </p>
Should you have a safety concern for the general public/Highway in relation to a private tree, then please follow the procedure set out above.</p>
