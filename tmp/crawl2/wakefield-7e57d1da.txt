Anglers Country Park is a Green Flag location with countryside, woodland, wetland, and trails to explore. Wildlife enthusiasts can enjoy bird hides, feeding stations, and a central lake.</p>
Come to the Visitor Centre at Anglers Country Park to learn how we manage and improve the habitats for wildlife. You can view our full Management Plan there.</p></p>

Volunteering</h2>
The Friends of Haw Park Wood and Wintersett help to manage and maintain the park.</p>
