Nature Conservation</h2>
Formerly a drift mine, the area was turned into a country park in 1991. The park has open spaces, woodland, grassy areas, and ponds, and is managed to protect and improve the habitats for wildlife.</p>
Water voles have been seen on the park and visitors can spot Skylarks, Buzzards, and Meadow Pipits. The park is also known for its variety of wildflowers and dragonflies.</p>
