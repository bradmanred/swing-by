Parking with a Blue Badge</h1>
 The Blue Badge Scheme: rights and responsibilities in England are on Gov UK .</p>
Please note that local parking restrictions and schemes may vary. Blue Badge holders are advised to check some restrictions before parking. If you are unsure, contact Wakefield Council before travelling.</p>
A disabled persons Blue Badge must be used properly. The badge and its concessions are for the Blue Badge holders use only.</p>
It is a criminal offence for the badge holder or anyone else to misuse a Blue Badge. Doing so could lead to a £1,000 fine and confiscation of the badge. Making sure that the scheme is not abused will benefit genuine badge holders.</p>
Permit Only Parking Bays</h2>
A Blue Badge is not valid to park in a bay reserved for permit holders only.</p>
Loading Bays</h2>
A Blue Badge is not valid to park in a bay reserved for loading only.</p>
Wakefield Council Off-Street Pay and Display car parks</h2>
Off-street car parks operated by local councils aren't included in the national Blue Badge Scheme.</p>
Blue Badge holders can park free of charge in Wakefield Council Pay and Display car parks. And for an unlimited period .</p>
Vehicles must:</p>
be parked correctly wholly within the markings of a parking bay - unless the car park is unlined</li>
be clearly displaying a valid Blue Badge - where it can be easily seen from outside the vehicle</li>
Blue Badge holders can park in any designated parking bay free of charge. Not just designated Blue Badge bays.</p>
As parking is permitted for an unlimited time there is no need to display a time clock.</p>
Restrictions can be found advertised on the car park notice boards.</p>
Wakefield Council Off-Street Permit Holders Only car parks</h2>
The council operates some permit only car parks such as at County Hall and Sun Lane Swimming Baths.</p>
There are Blue Badge parking bays provided at these car parks. Blue Badge holders parking at these car parks must also display a valid permit to park.</p>
Restrictions can be found advertised on the car park notice boards.</p>
Merchant Gate Multi Story car park</h2>
There are designated Blue Badge parking bays on levels 1, 4 and 5 at Merchant Gate car park. Vehicles parking in these bays must clearly display a valid Blue Badge when parking.</p>
 Normal charges apply to Blue Badge holders at this car park.</p>
Restrictions and charges can be found advertised on the car park notice boards.</p>
