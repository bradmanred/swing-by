Car parks in Wakefield</h1>
 MiPermit service
 </h2>
You can pay for parking at our pay and display car parks using your debit or credit card, or Apple Pay, using the MiPermit service. We do not use RingGo.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
Up to two hours free parking is available in some off-street car parks from 17 November 2025</h2>
A ticket when parking for up to two hours must be obtained from the car park machine and clearly displayed in the vehicle. Please check car park notice boards for current charges and restrictions.</p>
