 Wakefield Countryside Events

 </div>
Thornes Park is Wakefield's main open space, less than a mile from the city centre.</p>
Thornes Park  has 60 hectares of open space with a 2-mile circular path around it. The park also has exercise stations along the route and a mound in the middle which was once a castle site and offers great views of Wakefield.</p>

Play area improvements</h2>
In December 2022 a new play area in Thornes Park opened.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
It has equipment for children of different abilities, such as an inclusive swing seat and basket swing, a wheelchair-accessible roundabout and low-level sensory panels.</p>
It also has a train-themed toddler area, a ‘giant’s unit’ nearly 9m tall, a motte and bailey castle-styled area with embankment tube slide and a double zip line.</p></p>
