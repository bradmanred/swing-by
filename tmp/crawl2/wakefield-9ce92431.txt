Grounds maintenance service</h1>
We are the leading grounds maintenance and tree management company in the whole of the Wakefield District. We pride ourselves on the quality of service we provide to our customers. Our responsibility to our employees, who are passionate about delivering the best.</p>
We offer high-quality grounds maintenance and tree management services. Our teams of gardeners, prestige gardeners, tree surgeons are experts in their field and provide high standards of work to all our customers.</p>
Our services</h2>
grass cutting - lawned areas, large gardens,</li>
sports pitch marking</li>
bedding management including weeding</li>
tree and shrub management and maintenance</li>
tree inspections and surveys</li>
gritting and snow clearance and more!</li>
Our current customers</h2>
schools</li>
sports clubs</li>
leisure centres</li>
hotels</li>
factories</li>
community groups</li>
Benefits</h2>
The benefits of the council acting as your grounds maintenance and tree management contractor include:</p>
a large and skilled workforce familiar with the local area and dedicated to raising standards</li>
machinery available to carry out grounds maintenance tasks to the highest of standards</li>
guaranteed legislative compliance</li>
compliance with current health and safety standards</li>
all works carried out to nationally recognised industry standards and best practice</li>
bespoke services to suit your needs</li>
