Allotments and leisure gardens</h1>
Renting an allotment is an affordable way to have your own gardening space.</p>
With an allotment, you can grow fresh, organic food that tastes better than anything you can buy in a shop. </p>
But, maintaining an allotment takes effort, time, and dedication. It requires a lot of work to get started and keep it going.</p>
