Schools and groups</h1>
 School or group visit application form </p>
We are here to help schools and groups such as Beavers, Brownies and Scouts to learn about the environment. We also provide talks for social and friends groups.</p>
We can work with your school or group both within the school/club environment and in our country parks and nature reserves.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
We offer:</H2> 
pond dipping - identify pond creatures, learn about life cycles and food chains</li>
mini beasting - look for bugs in different habitats</li>
natural art - use different materials gathered in our parks to create collages</li>
making homes for birds, bees, bugs or bats</li>
talks</li>
We can adapt to your curriculum and also offer a pre-visit meeting to discuss details of the day's activities.</p>

Fees</h2>
At a Countryside site with two members of our staff - morning or afternoon 2-hour session:</p>
£2 per child with a minimum charge of £30</li>
At a Countryside site with 2 members of our staff - 4-hour session:
£3 per child with a minimum charge of £45</li>
Outreach in schools - morning or afternoon 2-hour session:
£3 per child with a minimum charge of £45</li>
Outreach in schools - 4-hour session:
£3.50 per child with a minimum charge of £52.50</li>
Brownies, Scouts or other group at a Countryside site - 60-90 minutes:
£2 per child with a minimum charge of £20</li>
Brownies, Scouts or other group at other venue - 60-90 minutes:
£2.50 per child with a minimum charge of £25</li>
Adult groups (slide shows, talks given by Ranger) at a Countryside site - 60-90 minutes: £60</li>
Adult groups (slide shows, talks given by Ranger) at other venue - 60-90 minutes: £70</li>
If the session involves making items such as nest or bat boxes, bird feeders or bug condos there will be an additional charge for materials.</p>
