Pay or appeal against a parking fine</h1>
Parking Fines (PCN)</h2>
​Parking fines are called Penalty Charge Notices (PCN)</p>
Motorists whose parking contravenes the regulations will be issued with a Penalty Charge Notice which costs £70 or £50 depending on the seriousness of the contravention.</p>
This charge is reduced by 50% if it is paid within 14 days of the PCN’s service date. Failure to pay a Penalty Charge Notice will result in further action, including an increased charge.</p>
Pay a par​​​king fine</h2>
​Be​​fore you start</h3>
 You will need: </h4>
A PCN number (the 10-digit number starting with WP) which is at the top of the Notice</li>
The vehicle registration mark (VRM)</li>
A credit or debit card</li>
 We accept Visa, Mastercard, Switch/Maestro and Electron. </p>

Paying by telephone</h2>
 Phone 0345 603 1539 , select the parking notices option, and follow the simple instructions. Phone lines are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.</p>
Paying by post</h2>
Wakefield Council, Parking Services, P.O. Box 47, Wakefield, WF1 2QW</p>
Cheques should be made payable to Wakefield Council. Please make sure you write your PCN number (the 10-digit number starting with WP) which is at the top of the Notice on the back of your cheque. We do not issue receipts for cheque payments unless you ask for one. Post-dated cheques are not acceptable. Please do not send cash through the post.</p>
In person</h2>
Payment can be made in person using chip and pin at Wakefield One, Burton Street, Wakefield WF1 2DD</p>
Opening times are 8:30am – 5pm Monday to Friday.</p>
Fixed penalty notices (FPN)</h2>
To pay a fixed penalty notice, go to  our payment site , select "Fixed Penalty Notices (e.g. littering, dog fouling)" and follow the instructions.</p>
Appeal against a fine</h2>
 Appealing against a Penalty Charge Notice is called making a challenge/representation </p>
Your challenge/representation should be made within 28 days from the date the Notice was issued.</p>
 Further information about appeal timescales ​</p>
Before you start</h3>
 You will need: </p>
A PCN number (the 10-digit number starting with WP) which is at the top of the Notice​</li>
The contravention code number (01 for example) shown on the Notice</li>
</div>
Enter your PCN Number (the 10-digit number starting with WP) and contravention code number then click 'Continue'. ​</p>
