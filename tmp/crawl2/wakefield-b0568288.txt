Completed Parks</h2>
Acacia Walk play area</h3>
completed: June 2026</li>
what’s new: wheelchair accessible roundabout and inclusive springie. Toddler and junior climbing units. A seesaw and swing set. New benches</li>
address: Acacia Walk, Knottingley WF11 0PS</li>
Agbrigg Park play area</h3>
completed: April 2026</li>
what’s new: two multi play units; Junior and toddler. A flywheel roundabout and a new swing set. A zipline and a large Turtle seat basket swing</li>
address: Agbrigg Rd, Wakefield WF1 5AS</li>
 Agbrigg play area gets a fresh new look </li>
Kettlethorpe Park play area</h3>
completed: February 2026</li>
what’s new: a multi-play-unit with a slide, ladder and low-level steps. A bee springie and an emoji discus roundabout</li>
address: Standbridge Close, Wakefield WF2 7NU</li>
 newly refurbished play area opens in Kettlethorpe </li>
Ferrybridge Play Area</h3>
completed: January 2026</li>
What's new: new slides and a variety of swings. A roundabout and two climbing towers. A 20m single zipline and flywheel spinner</li>
address: Ferrybridge Bypass, Knottingley WF11 8QS</li>
 new play area for Ferrybridge children </li>
Valley Gardens (Castleford)</h3>
completed: November 2025</li>
what's new: wheelchair-friendly roundabout, transfer-height seesaw, play panels, and a central pathway.  A nest group swing and a four-tower junior multi-play unit. Track ride and cliff rider. New safety surfacing, bins and benches</li>
address: Lower Oxford St, Castleford WF10 4AQ</li>
 new state-of-the-art play area opens at Valley Gardens in Castleford - Wakefield Council </li>
West End (Hemsworth)</h3>
completed: October 2025</li>
what's new: new swings, new rotators, a seesaw and a roundabout. A basket seat swing and an accessible springie. A junior multi-play unit with four towers, rock paper scissors and tumble turn play panel. New safety surfacing, bins and benches</li>
address: West End Park, Hemsworth, WF9 4QY</li>
 work completed on West End Park’s revamped play area - Wakefield Council </li>
Vale Head Park (Hemsworth)</h3>
completed: July 2025</li>
what's new: a flagpole installation, MUGA Jungle Tower, a rope climb and scramble net</li>
address: Wakefield Road, Pontefract, WF9 5JA</li>
 parks awarded Green Flag status - Wakefield Council </li>
did you know: Vale Head Park has the prestigious Green Flag status</li>
Coppers Lake Park (Crofton)</h3>
completed: July 2025</li>
what's new: new footpath installation</li>
new improvements: equipment installed with accessible features</li>
address: Crofton, WF4 1PR</li>
Gorton Street (Kinsley)</h3>
completed: May 2025</li>
what's new: climbing frames with slides, a seesaw and a carousel-style roundabout. New safety surface, bins, benches and safety gates</li>
address: Gorton Street, Kinsley, Pontefract, WF9 5ES</li>
 Gorton Street Park play area improvements complete - Wakefield Council </li>
Dunderdale Recreation Ground (Castleford)</h3>
completed: January 2025</li>
what's new: new MiniTown Quadrangle multi-play unit.  Four tower junior multi-play unit. New Ladybird Springie. New swing frames and seats. Quad Climber frame. Wipe Out balance board, Spinning Curlicue, roundabout and a seesaw. New wheelchair-friendly central 1.5m wide pathway. New bins, repainted benches, new play panels and signage and gate</li>
address: Dunderdale Crescent, Castleford, WF10 2NB</li>
