Parking</h1>
Learn more about where to park, how to apply for parking permits, how to apply for a blue badge, information on parking fines, and much more.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
 Pay or appeal against a parking fine
 </h2>
 To pay a fixed penalty notice, go to our payment site, select and follow the instructions. Your challenge/representation should be made within 28 days from the date the Notice was issued.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Apply for or renew a Blue Badge
 </h2>
 Blue Badges are for drivers, passengers or children with severe mobility problems and for those who are registered blind. Read more about how to apply for or renew a blue badge in Wakefield council.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Parking permits - apply and renew
 </h2>
 To obtain a parking permit submit an application form with all the relevant supporting documentation. There is no charge for a resident or visitor parking permit.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Car parks across the district
 </h2>
 Find out about Car parks in Ossett, Castleford, Normanton, Pontefract, South Elmsall, Wakefield and Coach and Lorry parks.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Parking policy, parking and traffic restrictions
 </h2>
 Learn more about many forms of traffic restrictions and parking policies that help maintain safe traffic flows around the highway networks in and around Wakefield.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Cashless parking
 </h2>
 You can pay for parking at our pay and display car parks using your debit or credit card, or Apple Pay, using the MiPermit service.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Public transport and car-sharing
 </h2>
 The Wakefield Free City Bus service provides residents and visitors an alternative to using the car around the city centre along with wycarshare.com which provides a free car sharing service for all those who live in and around Wakefield.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Report Blue Badge fraud
 </h2>
 It is a criminal offence for non-disabled people to use a blue badge and you can be fined up to £1,000 or drive a car that is displaying a Blue Badge without the badge holder in the vehicle.
 </div>
 </div>
 </div>
</li>
 Suspending parking bays and getting permission to park on yellow lines
 </h2>
 We may suspend the use of on-street parking bays for specific reasons. We will consider applications for: building operations, domestic removals, weddings, funerals or special events, delivery of goods taking longer than the specified time and filming.
 </div>
