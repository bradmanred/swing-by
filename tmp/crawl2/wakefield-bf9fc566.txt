The Park has facilities for families to enjoy a brilliant day out in the fresh air. You can eat at our Boat House café, or just grab an ice cream. Enjoy your own picnic, but please do not bring BBQs. These are not allowed due to the fire risk.</p>
We’re focusing on the wildlife habitats at Pugney’s, which is now a countryside site. </p>
This means we’re no longer offering water sport activities or fishing at the park. But you can still enjoy plenty of family friendly activities. Including the Pirates’ Cove play area, Blown Away adventure trail and miniature railway. Plus the park has plenty of open spaces to play outdoor games, walk and cycle. </p>
For your own safety, please do not attempt to enter the lake by any means. This includes:</p>
swimming</li>
paddle boarding</li>
kayaking </li>
and all other water activities</li>

J's Cycles Now Open at Pugneys Country Park</h2>
We’re delighted to welcome J’s Cycles to Pugneys Country Park, now officially open and ready to support your next active adventure.</p>
Whether you’re visiting for a leisurely stroll, a family bike ride or a more dedicated training session, J’s offers a friendly, expert-led space to help you get the most from your time outdoors. The team is on hand to provide advice, support and a warm welcome, so feel free to pop in and say hello.</p>
Cycle hire for everyone</h3>
J’s offers a wide range of cycle hire options to suit all ages and abilities. From children’s bikes and balance bikes to electric bikes, adaptive options, and child and doggy buggies, there’s something for everyone. It’s the perfect way to explore the park, whether you’re riding solo, with family or bringing your four-legged friend along.</p>
To plan ahead, bikes can be booked online here: https://www.jscycleshack.com/ </p>
Support for runners</h3>
If running is more your pace, J’s also provides professional gait analysis alongside a high-quality range of running footwear and equipment. Whether you’re just starting out or looking to improve performance, the team can help you find the right fit and feel confident in your stride.</p>
Visit us</h3>
This exciting new addition brings even more opportunities to get active at Pugneys. Drop in, explore what’s on offer and meet the team.</p>
Friendly, inclusive and open to all.</p>
