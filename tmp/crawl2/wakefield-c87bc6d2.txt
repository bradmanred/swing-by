Newly refurbished play area opens in Kettlethorpe</h1>
Thursday, February 26, 2026</p>
The Standbridge Lane play area in Kettlethorpe has been revamped. Providing a welcoming and attractive space for local families to enjoy.</p>
 </div>
 </div>
</div>
“The newly upgraded play area in Kettlethorpe has been built with fun and durability in mind. This investment in the park means it will remain a long-lasting part of the community.</p>
“Having spaces where children and families can spend quality time is an important priority for the Council. I am glad we have been able to revamp the play area for the next generation.”</p></p>
 </div>
Cllr George Ayre</p>
Cabinet Member for Environment and Civic Services</p>
 </div>
 </picture>
 </div>
Wakefield Council has invested £67,000 into the refurbishment as part of its ongoing commitment to improving parks and play spaces across the district. The project has been supported by local ward councillors.</p>
Designed for children aged 1-14 years, the upgraded park is equipped with a new multi-play-unit with a slide, ladder and accessible low-level steps. To support inclusive play, a bee springie and an emoji discus roundabout have also been fitted.</p>
The upgraded play area has been carefully designed to get children climbing, swinging, sliding, traversing, rocking, and rotating.</p>
A new floor-based trim trail has been installed to encourage challenge and coordination. Whilst the new dice daisy provides children with interactive play opportunities. Additional seating has also been added in the play area for parents and carers.</p>
