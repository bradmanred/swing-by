Upton Country Park sits 7 miles south of Pontefract and 12 miles southeast of Wakefield. It is made up of wetland areas, fishing ponds, woodland, scrub, and grassland. The park sits on a belt of magnesian limestone that runs from South Shields in the north to Nottingham in the south. </p>
At nearby South Elmsall Quarry you will find one of the UK's best visible examples of Permian stromatolite reefs.</p>

​Nature conservation</h2>
The park was made a local nature reserve in 2008 and is located on limestone soil. The soil's richness allows for the growth of many colourful wildflowers and orchids.</p>
You can see several species of butterflies such as Speckled Woods, Common Blues, and Brimstones. </p>
The rare Lesser Marsh Grasshopper can also be found here. Keep an eye out for Kestrels hovering in the sky and for singing Skylarks.</p></p>
