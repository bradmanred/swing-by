Apply for or renew a Blue Badge</h1>
<!--StartFragment --></p>
If you have a disability or a health condition, you may be able to apply for a Blue Badge to help you park closer to your destination.</p>
Who can get a Blue Badge</h2>
You automatically qualify for a Blue Badge if:</p>
you are severely sight impaired (blind)</li>
you receive 8 or more points in the ‘moving around’ part or 10 points (Descriptor E) in the ‘planning and following journeys’ part of a mobility assessment for Personal Independence Payment (PIP)*</li>
you receive the higher rate of the mobility component for Disability Living Allowance (HRMCDLA)*</li>
you receive the War Pensioners’ Mobility Supplement (WPMS)*</li>
you receive a qualifying award under the Armed Forces Compensation Scheme</li>
*Where entitlement for a Blue Badge is linked to these awards, the badge will only be issued to the current expiry date of that allowance.</p>
You may qualify for a badge if:</p>
you have a disability which means you cannot walk or have great difficulty walking from your car to where you want to go</li>
you are likely to come to harm while walking</li>
you are likely to harm someone else when you are walking</li>
you drive a vehicle and have a disability in both of your arms</li>
Children may be able to get a Blue Badge if they are over 2 years old and have a great difficulty in walking.</p>
You may be eligible for a badge if you have a disabled child under 3 years old who:</p>
needs to be accompanied by bulky medical equipment that is difficult to carry, or</li>
must always stay close to a vehicle in case of a medical emergency</li>
How to apply</h2>
You will need to provide:</p>
proof of identity*</li>
proof of address*</li>
a recent digital photo of your head and shoulders, taken against a plain, light background</li>
your national insurance number, if you have one</li>
contact details including phone number, email and postal address</li>
*You cannot use the same document to prove your identity and your address</p>

What happens next</h2>
You’ll be contacted within 14 days after submitting your application.</p>
The full process may take up to 12 weeks.</p>
A £10 administration fee applies to all successful applications, including new, renewal, or replacement requests.</p>
Help with your application</h2>
If you need help to apply for a Blue Badge, you can:</p>
have a family member or friend complete the application for you</li>
call us for help on 0345 8 506 506 </li>
Replace a lost, stolen or damaged Blue Badge</h2>
To replace a Blue Badge, you’ll need:</p>
your personal details</li>
your National Insurance number, if you have one</li>
the existing badge number, if you know it</li>
If the badge has been stolen, you will also need a crime reference number.</p>
