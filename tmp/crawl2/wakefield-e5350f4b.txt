 Bottle Art Creations: Painting Workshop
 </div>
 </div>
 23 Oct 2026
 </div>
 </div>
 Details: 
Join us at Anglers Country Park for a relaxed and friendly crafting session. Transform glass bottles into beautiful decorative pieces and unique candle holders using paints and embellishments. At just £5 per person, it's the perfect opportunity to try something new, catch up with friends, or enjoy a mindful few hours of creativity.</p>
Leave with your own handcrafted creations, new skills, and the satisfaction of making something truly unique.</p>
Spaces are limited and booking is essential at www.ticketsource.co.uk/aspire </p>
We will also be holding a Handmade Cards crafting session as part of the same series on Friday 9 October.</p></div>
 Start time: 11am</div>
 End time: 1pm</div>
 Age range: Adults</div>
 Free: No
 </div>
 Booking required: Yes
 </div>
 Location: Visitor Centre Anglers Country Park</div>

 Spellbound Creations: Halloween Crafting for Kids
 </div>
 </div>
 30 Oct 2026
 </div>

 Details: 
Step into a world of magic and imagination at Anglers Country Park with our enchanting Spellbound Creations workshop. Young witches, wizards and magical makers will craft their very own nature-inspired wands and design a spellbinding hat to complete their mystical look.</p>
Including natural materials and including plenty of creativity, children can unleash their imaginations, create magical accessories to take home, and enjoy a fun-filled Halloween crafting experience.</p>
The perfect activity to accompany our Spooktacular Scarecrow Trail, making for a wonderfully wicked day of seasonal adventure, creativity and family fun. </p>
