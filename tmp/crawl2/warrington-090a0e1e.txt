Address</h2>
5 Bankside, Crosfield Street Warrington, WA1 1UP</p>
The facility offers one medium sized flexible room displaying café style tables & modern bistro chairs but also has trestle tables to provide an alternative layout.  The room can accommodate 45 people cabaret style.  The Pavilion has a serving counter and table top appliances as well as separate dishwashing and food clearing space. Opening on to a beautifully maintained bowling green to the rear via patio doors and the park to the front, this light airy and air-conditioned building offers a picturesque and tranquil setting for conferences, meetings or small private functions.</p>
Facilities</h2>
Free Wi-Fi</li>
Kitchen facilities</li>
Outdoor space available</li>
This beautiful parkland pavilion is the only WBC community facility for hire within Warrington Town Centre. Located in the beautiful Bank Park, it is situated adjacent to the iconic Town Hall and famous Golden Gates.</p>
Birthday celebrations/Afternoon teas</h2>
If you are looking for the perfect venue to celebrate a special birthday, anniversary or family occasion, look no further than Bank Park Pavilion. If you would like to host a small party but don’t have room in your own home why not hire ours? With kitchen facilities and lovely modern furniture, you just need to bring the food, entertainment and guests. For a Golden Wedding, a family Christening or 80th Birthday celebration bring all your guests together. Mingling with your loved ones on the patio on a hot summer afternoon, what could be nicer?</p>
Private functions/Weddings</h2>
Within a five minute walk from Warrington Registry Office, why not book Bank Park Pavilion for your special wedding celebration.  The centres competitive hire rates offer a “country house” wedding for a budget price!  The centre’s wedding packages allow the Bride & Groom to plan their perfect reception with their personal choice of home/outside catering options.</p>
