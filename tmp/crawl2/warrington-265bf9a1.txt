 Open space, sport and recreation assessment 
</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
The Open Space, Sport, and Recreation Assessment 2023 (OSSRA)</p>
OSSRA is the term for a collection of documents that provide an up-to-date assessment of all the borough’s open space, sport, and recreation facilities.</p>
There are five documents in total:</p> 
 The Open Space Report (OSR) - Gives the latest position of the distinct types and levels of public open space provision</li>
 Playing Pitch & Outdoor Sport Assessment Report (PPOSA) - Gives the latest position of the type and number of playing pitches</li>
 Playing Pitch & Outdoor Sport Strategy (PPOSS) - Provides an action plan to guide future investment, development, and improvement of playing pitches</li>
 Indoor & Built Facilities Needs Assessment (IBFNA) - Gives an up-to-date position of the type and number of indoor sports facilities</li>
I ndoor Built & Facilities Strategy (IBFS) - Provides an action plan to guide future investment, development, and improvement of indoor sports facilities</li> </div>
