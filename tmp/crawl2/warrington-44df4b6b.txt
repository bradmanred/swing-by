Birchwood Forest park</p>
 </div>
</div>
Birchwood forest park has strong historical links as the area was occupied by the Royal Ordnance Factory, ROF Risley . A large World War 2 factory filling munitions, including the Grand Slam Bomb . While these areas are now covered by grass, you can still see the mounds where they used to be.</p>
