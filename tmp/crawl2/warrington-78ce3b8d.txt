</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
Apply for a resident parking permit</h2>
If you live in a resident parking zone, you can apply for a permit so that you can park near your home.</p>
There is also a resident parking scheme for local car parks if you live in Lymm but don’t have a driveway.</p>
Before you apply</h3>
When you apply you’ll need to tell us your name, address and contact details and which zone you’re applying for. </p>
We need details about your car, including the make, model, colour and registration number, and also proof that you own or lease the car, such as:</p>
a Vehicle Registration Document (V5C) showing your full name and address as shown on your application form</li>
a valid motor insurance certificate and schedule, showing your full name and address and your vehicle registration mark</li>
for a company vehicle, we’ll accept a typed letter (on official company letter-headed paper) confirming that you are the sole driver, and showing your full name and address and vehicle registration mark</li>
You’ll need to provide proof that you live at the address, such as a recent:</p>
utility bill (e.g. electricity, gas, water, landline, council tax)</li>
formal tenancy agreement</li>
housing association rent document</li>
property purchase document</li>
lease agreement document</li>
personal bank, building society or credit card statement</li>

Night-time parking in Lymm</div>
 </div>
If you live in the Lymm village area and you don’t have off-street parking, such as a driveway, you can apply for a permit so that you can park on either Pepper Street car park , Davies Way car park or Henry Street car park in the evening.  </p>
The permit is valid between 5pm and 10pm on weekdays and Saturdays – but you can leave your car overnight as the car parks are free after 10pm until 9am the following morning. They’re also free on Sundays. </p>
 You can pay £100 up front for a 12-month permit, or you can pay by direct debit at £10 per month. </p>
It costs £20 if you need to change your permit to another vehicle. We’ll contact you after you apply to arrange payment. You have to renew your permit every year.
 </p>

If you change your car or move house</h2>
Let us know as soon as possible if you change your car, or if you move house. Please note that you may need to fully reapply with up to date proof of your address.</p>
Visitor permits</h2>
If you’re visiting someone who lives in a resident parking zone, you’ll need a valid visitor permit </p>
However:</p> 
Bank Quay area - there are 90 minute waiting bays so there are no visitor permits available</li>
Green Street - there are two hour waiting bays so there are no visitor permits available</li>
Crosfield Street and East View - there are no visitor permits available at all due to capacity</li> 
Blue badges</h2>
If you have a Blue badge but not a resident parking permit, you aren’t allowed to park in a resident parking zone unless you’re parked in a designated disabled bay. Your Blue badge must also be correctly displayed.</p></div>
