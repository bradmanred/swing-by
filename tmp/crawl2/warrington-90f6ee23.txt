 Dogs in Warrington parks 
</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
Warrington parks welcome dogs accompanied by responsible owners.</p>
Dog owners are welcome to exercise their dogs in Warrington parks but should follow the good dog code.</p>
</div>
 </div>
</div>

 When walking your dog in a park or open space, they should be under close control at all times. </p>
 This can include exercising dogs off a lead if the dog is trained to return on demand and does not pose a threat or nuisance to other dog walkers or park users. </p>
 Owners of well behaved dogs are welcome to exercise dogs off lead in parks and open spaces, unless otherwise signposted (please see below for exceptions). </p>
Dogs are excluded from fenced play areas, are not permitted on bowling greens and owners are encouraged to keep their dogs off formal sports pitches.</p>
Owners should be considerate to sensitive conservation sites and are encouraged to keep dogs on leads at  Risley Moss .</p>
Dogs should be on a lead and not permitted to enter ponds or disturb grazing sheep at Rixton Clay Pits .</p>
Dog owners are requested to ensure their dogs do not disturb ground nesting birds and are encouraged to keep them on a lead at Paddington Meadows, Bewsey Meadows and Gatewarth Meadow areas between March and August.</p>
