 Apply for contract parking at a council car park 
</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
Contract parking permits are cheaper than the standard daily parking rates - you could save up to 30%. You can also spread the cost of your parking by paying monthly via direct debit, and you won’t have to queue at the payment machines!</p>
You can apply for parking for:</p> 
 Warrington Town Hall car park </li>
 Time Square multi-storey car park </li>
 Warrington West rail station </li> 
How to apply for contract parking</h2>
 You’ll need to tell us: </p> 
your name, address and contact details</li>
make, model and colour of your car</li>
the car's registration number</li> 
Applications are welcome for Time Square and Warrington West Station car parks as there are spaces, please be aware that there's a waiting list for the Town Hall.</p>
Once you've submitted the form, you'll be notified when a space becomes available.</p></div>
 </div>
</div>
Prices</h2>
Contracts run from 1 April to 31 March every year. If you want to sign up part way through the year we’ll charge you from the month you sign up until 31 March.</p>
</div>
 </div>
</div>
 </p>
 Time Square multi-story car park - annual contract is £640 (including VAT) or £53.33 per month </p>
 Warrington Town Hall car park - annual contract is £840 (including VAT ) or £70 per month. </p>
 Warrington West Station car park </p>
5 day permit (Monday - Friday): £140 a year  between April and March or pro rata depending upon remaining months of £11.66 per month.</p>
7 day permit: £170 a year  between April and March or pro rata depending upon remaining months of £14.17 per month.</p></div>
 </div>
</div>
After you apply, we’ll contact you and let you know if there’s a permit available.</p>
 If there is, you’ll need to pay within seven days by: </p>
Credit/debit card – over the phone (we’ll contact you)</li>
Cash – you can pay at 1 Time Square</li>
Cheque – payable to Warrington Borough Council</li>
Postal order – payable to Warrington Borough Council</li>
Direct debit – we’ll send you a direct debit form with your application, which you need to fill in and send back to us within seven days, along with a cheque for the first month of your contract. Direct debits will be taken on the first working day of the month.</li>
We can’t accept online payments at the moment.</h3>
Send cheques and postal orders to: Car Parking Services, East Annexe, Town Hall, Sankey Street, Warrington, WA1 1UH </p>
</div>
 </div>
</div>
If you change your car</h3>
Let us know if you change your car. Email [email protected] or ring 01925 442865 .</p>
Cancellations</h3>
If you want to cancel your parking pass, let us know in writing. We’ll refund you for every complete month remaining on your contract, minus a £10 administration fee.</p>
If you pay by direct debit we can’t give a refund for any money you’ve already paid, and it’s your responsibility to cancel the direct debit after you’ve written to us to cancel the contract.</p>
</div>
 </div>
</div>
Terms and conditions</div>
 </div>
If you don’t pay within seven days we’ll cancel the contract</li>
The contract is only valid for the car park you applied for</li>
You can’t reserve a parking space</li>
You can’t give the parking pass to anyone else or use it in a different car</li>
Only cars and small vans can use the car parks</li>
We may cancel your pass if you misuse it, or misuse the car park</li>
Vehicles are left at your own risk, we can’t accept responsibility for any loss or damage to your vehicle, its accessories or contents</li>
