Facts</h3>

The Twiggeries</div>
 </div>
 Opening Times:  24 hours a day</p>
 Facts: The Twiggeries was once a willow coppice for a thriving basket making industry in the 19th century. Willow baskets were supplied to the tannery industry at Howley for the transportation of hides.</p>
As the industry waned the willow coppice has become a dense woodland that supports a range of wildlife, from the shy Water Vole to Warblers all the way from Africa.</p>
