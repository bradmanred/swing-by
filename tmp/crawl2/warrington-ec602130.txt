 Report a problem with Parks, Recreation Grounds and Play Areas 
</h1>
 </div>
 </div>
All council owned play areas and outdoor gyms across the borough are inspected on a regular basis to ensure the health and safety of park users</p>
Play inspections are completed every 4 weeks</li>
All play areas have an annual independent inspection</li>
Where there is vandalism or arson attacks on play areas, trim trails or outdoor gyms and/or equipment is damaged, the play equipment will be removed/isolated. In some cases facility will be closed.</p>
Vandalised facilities are monitored for a minimum period of 6 months before any equipment is replaced or returned.</p>
 To report a problem on a play area, trim trail or outdoor gym on council land only please use the online form and tell us: </p>
The location</li>
What item is damaged, for example a swing or slide</li>
What damage has been caused</li>
Was the damage caused through vandalism or an arson attack</li>
