 About the venue
 </h2>
 Situated within The Deer Park on Allendale Road in Wingerworth. 
The courts are a short walk from a car park or the road and from the park's other facilities including a bowling green, play equipment, outdoor gym equipment, cricket pitches, a pavilion and an angling pond.
