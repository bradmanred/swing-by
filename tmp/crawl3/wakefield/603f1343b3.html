Wakefield Council Allotments

Renting an allotment is an inexpensive way of getting your hands on valuable gardening space.

With an allotment you get to produce cheap home-grown organic food, with the knowledge that it is fresher than anything you can buy from a shop. Nothing compares to the taste of your own freshly picked tomatoes: full of vitamins, free from harmful chemicals and not genetically modified.

Wakefield Council controls 60 sites across the District.

If you are interested in a site other than those listed on this website you must contact the site operator for further information.
