Anglers Country Park

Wakefield Countryside Events

Find out what events are going on at your local park.

What's on

Anglers Country Park is a Green Flag location with countryside, woodland, wetland, and trails to explore. Wildlife enthusiasts can enjoy bird hides, feeding stations, and a central lake.

Come to the Visitor Centre at Anglers Country Park to learn how we manage and improve the habitats for wildlife. You can view our full Management Plan there.

  • Location:

    Haw Park Lane
    Wakefield
    WF4 2EE

    Anglers Country Park is open 24hrs.

    The Visitor Centre is open from 10am - 4pm Tuesday to Sunday

    The Woodland Café is open from 9am - 4pm daily.

Facilities and services

  • Walking routes and nature trails
  • Cycle Routes
  • The Woodland Café
  • Visitor Centre
  • Room on the Broom Adventure Trail
  • Children’s Playground
  • On-site parking
  • Service dogs welcome
  • Bird hides and bird feeding stations

Visitors can bring their dogs in to order but unfortunately they are unable to stay while food is consumed.

Volunteering

The Friends of Haw Park Wood and Wintersett help to manage and maintain the park.

If you want to help maintain our green spaces as a volunteer, please contact the at countryside_admin@wakefield.go.uk

Car park prices

Time Price
Up to 2 hours £1
Up to 4 hours £2
Up to 24 hours £4

 

A valid parking ticket must be displayed in your vehicle upon arrival. Parking charges apply between 9am and 6pm, 7 days a week.
