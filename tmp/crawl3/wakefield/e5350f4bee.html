Spooktacular Scarecrow Trail
24 Oct 2026
Details:

Join us at Anglers Country Park this half term for a frightfully fun family adventure. Follow the Woodland Trail to find our spooky scarecrows, collect the secret code letters, and crack the case to claim your prize.

At just £2 per trail pack, it's a great value way to get outdoors, explore the park, and enjoy some Halloween fun together.

24 October to 1 November, 10am to 3pm

No booking required. Simply pick up your trail pack from the Visitor Centre and start your adventure!

Start time: 11am
End time: 3pm
Age range: Under 5s, Children, Families
Free: No
Booking required: No
Location: Visitor Centre Anglers Country Park

Spellbound Creations: Halloween Crafting for Kids
30 Oct 2026
Details:

Step into a world of magic and imagination at Anglers Country Park with our enchanting Spellbound Creations workshop. Young witches, wizards and magical makers will craft their very own nature-inspired wands and design a spellbinding hat to complete their mystical look.

Including natural materials and including plenty of creativity, children can unleash their imaginations, create magical accessories to take home, and enjoy a fun-filled Halloween crafting experience.

The perfect activity to accompany our Spooktacular Scarecrow Trail, making for a wonderfully wicked day of seasonal adventure, creativity and family fun.

Spaces are limited and booking is essential at www.ticketsource.co.uk/aspire

Start time: 11am
End time: 1pm
Age range: Children, Families
Free: No
Booking required: Yes
Location: Visitor Centre Anglers Country Park
