Springbank Pavilion

Springbank Pavilion is in Leazes Park. It is a flexible venue and can host a range of events and activities.

  • views over the park
  • recently refurbished
  • outdoor space can be used
  • one off hire or regular hire available

To make a booking, check availability or ask a question please contact the team at Parks.Bookings@newcastle.gov.uk.

Prices

The hire fees are:

  • Hourly rate: £30
  • Half day (4 hours): £85
  • Full day (8 hours): £145

Location

Springbank Pavilion is located in Leazes Park, near the children’s play area and family picnic area. 

Parking

Pay and display car park located at the entrance to Leazes Park.

On street parking is available on Richardson Road.

Food and drinks

Our cafe can provide a small selection of menu options if pre-ordered. Please let us know if you are interested in catering when contacting us.

There is a small kitchen in Springbank Pavilion which you can use. Facilities include:

  • crockery
  • cutlery
  • kettle
  • hot drink urn
  • fridge

Contact us

To make a booking, check availability or ask a question please contact the team at Parks.Bookings@newcastle.gov.uk.
