Newry, Mourne and Down District Council has over 128 play parks across the area. These parks give children safe, fun, and accessible places to play. They include a wide range of equipment for all ages, including inclusive play areas so every child can join in.

We have recently finished carrying out our Play Strategy, which included upgrading many parks to make play time even better. Whether children enjoy climbing, sliding, or swinging, our parks are designed to support physical activity, social skills, and creative play in a safe and welcoming space.
