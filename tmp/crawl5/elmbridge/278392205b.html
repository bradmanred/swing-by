Help shape the future of your local green space 
Residents living near Lower Green Recreation Ground, Littleheath Recreation Ground and George Froude Park are invited to share their views on potential improvements to these green spaces. 
The survey takes just five to 10 minutes to complete. Participants can also register their interest in a one-to-one interview (£25 voucher) or a local workshop (£40 voucher). 
Take part in the survey. 

The maintenance of our green spaces is managed by Glendale. 

Glendale are responsible for a range of maintenance activities, from grass cutting to horticulture and litter clearance. To find out when your local green space will be maintained, see Glendale Local - Elmbridge.
