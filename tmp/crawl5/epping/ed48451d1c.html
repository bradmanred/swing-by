Event organisers

North Weald Airfield is a busy and active airfield.

Every year it hosts a wide range of successful events including:

  • Air shows
  • Antique fairs
  • Bus rallies
  • Charity events
  • Classic and sports car shows
  • Cycle racing
  • Drag racing
  • Fly-ins
  • Garden shows
  • Model shows
  • Road safety and training
  • Trade events
  • And many more

If you are interested in hosting an event, activity, or filming project at North Weald Airfield please contact the Airfield Manager:
