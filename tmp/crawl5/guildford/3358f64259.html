Welcome to Ash Manor Sports Centre

Welcome to Ash Manor Sports Centre

At Ash Manor Sports Centre we’re passionate about helping you maintain a healthy, active and fun lifestyle, and we offer lots of ways to get active, entertained, and involved. Our friendly and engaging team is here to welcome you, no matter what your ambitions are or where you are on your fitness journey. We work closely in partnership with Guildford Borough Council to realise our vision of improving lives through leisure.
