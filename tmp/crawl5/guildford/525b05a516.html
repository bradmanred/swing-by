Welcome to Guildford

Set in the heart of Surrey, Guildford is a charming riverside town that perfectly blends history, culture, and modern living. Its iconic High Street, lined with elegant architecture and boutique shops, creates a picture-perfect setting for visitors to explore and enjoy.

Guildford is immensely proud of its heritage, which dates back to Saxon times when it was known as Golden (Guilden) Ford. Explore the ruins of Guildford Castle, a Norman landmark whose Great Tower offers panoramic views across the town and surrounding countryside, the perfect spot for photos and picnics.  Check opening days on our Guildford Heritage Attractions schedule to plan your visit.

Guildford offers something for everyone, from exciting events and live performances to fabulous shopping, welcoming pubs, and award-winning restaurants. Whether you’re here for a day trip or a longer stay, you’ll find plenty to see and do throughout the year.

Plan your visit around one of Guildford’s many events and festivals, which range from the quirky and creative to the dramatic and active. For a more relaxing escape, enjoy a vineyard tour and wine tasting and discover how the local geology helps Surrey produce its award-winning English wines. History lovers can step back in time at one of the area’s stately homes, such as the magnificent Horsley Towers, where you can even spend the night.

As you stroll up Guildford High Street, admire the blend of old and new that tells the town’s fascinating story. Join one of the official guided tours led by expert Guildford Town Guides to uncover hidden secrets and tales from the past.

Need help planning your visit? The friendly team at the Guildford Tourist Information Centre is on hand to share local insights, answer your questions, and help you make the most of your stay.

Come and discover Guildford’s vibrant town centre, beautiful countryside, and rich history for yourself  and see why this enchanting corner of Surrey captures the hearts of everyone who visits.
