How To Find Us & Parking

How To Find Us & Parking

Guildford Lido is located near the centre of Guildord, on Lido road off Packway (A25) and just behind Guildford College.

Very limited parking is available on site. On warm days this fills up very quickly and parking at the Lido can never be guaranteed. Once the car park is full please park nearby at Guildford Spectrum which is just a 10 minute walk away.

Guildford Lido thanks you for your co-operation, please be respectful to our neighbours.
