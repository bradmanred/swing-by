Playgrounds

We have a range of  play equipment in our playgrounds across the borough. These include:

  • zip lines
  • sand pits
  • multi units
  • roundabouts
  • play equipment for toddlers

Many of our playgrounds are disability friendly and many are accessible for wheelchairs.
Please contact parks@guildford.gov.uk if you require more information.

When using playgrounds remember:

  • most playgrounds are intended for use by those between 4 and 8 years old, check local signage
  • only assistance dogs are allowed in the playground
  • secure bicycles to the outside of the playground
  • no alcohol in playgrounds
  • no high heeled shoes on safety surface

We inspect playgrounds regularly to ensure they are clean and safe to use.

If you have any queries, please call 01483 505050 during in office hours.
For out of hours assistance between 7am and 8pm call our Ranger service on 07880 784947.
