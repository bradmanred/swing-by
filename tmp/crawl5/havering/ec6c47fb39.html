Parks

  1. Home
  2. Libraries, parks and leisure
  3. Parks

The London Borough of Havering is proud to manage a variety of parks and open spaces, from small village greens to vast country parks.

16 of our sites have been awarded Green Flag status which is the benchmark national standard for publicly accessible parks and green spaces.

With a team of dedicated staff and assistance from local community groups, we have been able to retain and even increase the number of Green Flag parks in the borough. 

Some parks have also been awarded various medals as part of the London in Bloom campaign, the largest horticultural campaign in London. 

The wide variety of parks and open spaces Havering has to offer means that there is something for everyone. Our teams works hard to ensure our parks and open spaces are welcoming, safe and inclusive for everyone.
