Parks, Recreation and Play Facilities

Oak Processionary Moth Caterpillars (OPMs) are active at this time of year. Nests are typically dome or teardrop-shaped and white when fresh, though it quickly changes to brown. The caterpillars have black heads and bodies covered in long white hairs.

Do not approach or touch either the caterpillars or their nests. The hairs of this insect can cause health implications to humans and animals upon close contact. This can range from minor skin complaints and eye and throat irritations to anaphylaxis.

 If you see one or more of this caterpillars or a nest in Mole Valley’s parks and open spaces, please report it to us on parks@molevalley.gov.uk, via our Tree Issue Report online form or by calling 01306 885001.

There are many recreation areas and play facilities across Mole Valley suitable for a range of ages and interests. Please report any damage in one of the MVDC parks or playgrounds by emailing parks@molevalley.gov.uk, reporting via an online form or by calling 01306 885001.

Ashtead Recreation Ground

Location: South of Barnett Wood Lane near the village pond.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Skate park
  • Hard court available (booked through Ashtead Football Club)
  • Football pitch (under license to Ashtead Football Club)
  • Tennis courts (under license to Ashtead Tennis Players Club)
  • Three car parks: one off Barnett Wood lane, one off Greville Park Road and one off Woodfield Lane

Merebank Recreation Ground

Location: Access from Willow Close, Merebank and Hawkesmoore Drive.

Facilities:

  • Toddlers playground
  • Adventure playground for older children
  • Full sized hard court area with basketball hoops

Chrystie Recreation Ground

Location: South of the village centre on Dorking Road.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Bowling and tennis clubs

Lower Road Recreation Ground

Location: Lower Road to the west of the village.

Facilities

  • Large playground
  • Skate park
  • Basketball half court
  • Small car park

Brockham Recreation Ground

Location: Middle Street.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Multi-use games area
  • Goal area
  • Youth shelter
  • The twin swing is currently out of action awaiting repair

The Big Field

Location: South of the A25 Reigate Road.

Facilities:

  • Three football pitches and changing pavilion  (under licence to Dorking Rugby Football Club)
  • The field is owned by the National Trust but managed by a local committee on which we sit

Meadowbank Recreation Ground

Location: Dorking town centre with access from Mill Lane, Ashcombe Road, London Road and Fairfield Drive.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Skate/BMX park
  • Multi use games area
  • Aerial swing
  • Facilities for football and cricket
  • Soft Play
  • Football ground

Pippbrook Grounds

Location: Between A24 Reigate Road and London Road

Facilities:

  • Tennis courts, managed by Dorking Sports Centre
  • Bowling green and pavilion of Pippbrook Bowling Club

Pixham Sports Ground

Location: South side of Pixham Lane.

Facilities:

  • Home to Dorking Cricket Club, Dorking Athletics Club and Dorkinian Football Club.
  • We own the ground and it is managed by the Pixham Sports Association

Cannon Court Recreation Ground

Location: A small open space with access from Cannon Grove and Cannonside.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Tennis courts which are leased and managed by the Leatherhead Tennis Club

Cock Lane Recreation Ground

Location: Adjoining Cock Lane

Facilities:

  • Large playground
  • A multi sports court
  • Tennis court

Kennel Lane Recreation Ground

Location: Accessed from Hazel Way, Gatesden Road and Ashwood Park, as well as the public footpath from Bell Lane.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Football pitch (licensed to Bookham Football Club)
  • Small pond

Fortyfoot Recreation Ground

Location: Between Fortyfoot Road and the main A24.

Facilities:

  • Small playground
  • Football pitch
  • Bowling green is the home of Leatherhead Bowling Club
  • Informal woodland area with a number of paths

Kingston Road Recreation Ground

Location: Leatherhead KT22 7PZ.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Skate park
  • Football pitch and pavilion
  • Sensory garden
  • Environmental area
  • Update – the basket swing is currently out of action. We are working with our contractors to resolve this as soon as possible

Leatherhead Leisure Centre

Location: Guildford Road, Leatherhead KT22 9BL

Facilities:

  • Skate park

King George V Playing Field

Location: A24 Deepdene Avenue.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Full sized and mini football pitch
  • Concrete skate park with a sunken bowl area, range of street features
  • Teen shelter
  • Changing pavilion

Cradhurst Recreation Ground

Location: North of Westcott Street with access available from Cradhurst Close, Springfield Road, as well as via the public footpath that runs northwards from the main A25 road.

Facilities:

  • Playground
  • Teen area with half pipe, football goals, half court and climbing boulders
  • Update – the basket swing is currently out of action. We are working with our contractors to resolve this as soon as possible

The facilities listed are managed by us.

Additional facilities are provided by local Parish Councils. Please contact the relevant parish for details.

Many of these recreation areas and open spaces are available for hire by community groups and individuals wishing to hold special events.

For more information, email parks@molevalley.gov.uk
