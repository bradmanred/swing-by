Playgrounds

We own and manage a number of children's playgrounds. Other local playgrounds are usually managed by the local town or parish council.
 

Hollybush playground, Sevenoaks

This playground was renovated in 2022. Most play equipment is suitable for children aged 3 plus. It has a climbing frame, monkey bars, a nest swing, an obstacle course, a see-saw, slide and swings.

It also has baby swings and a roundabout for younger children.

The playground is situated next to a café and has free parking nearby.

Address: Holmesdale Road, Sevenoaks, Kent TN13 3XL


Hilda May toddler play area, Swanley

This play area has a carousel, multiplay and toddler swings. Seating available.

Open during daylight hours.

Free on-street parking is available nearby.

Address: Hilda May Avenue, Swanley  BR8 7BT (close to White Oak Leisure Centre)


Cherry Avenue play area, Swanley

This play area has toddler swings, a  Multi-Use Games Area (MUGA), cableway runway, swing basket, junior swings, climber poles, multiplay and slide bars. It also has a large grassed area and seating.

Open during daylight hours.

Free on-street parking is available nearby.

Address: Cherry Avenue, Swanley  BR8 7DX.
