Public toilet facilities

Winter

  • 1 October - 31 March
  • 8.00am - 4.00pm
  • 25 December - all public toilets closed.

Summer

  • 1 April - 30 September
  • 8.00am - 10.00pm (apart from Sunny Sands, which closes by 6.00pm).
  • The public toilets at Oaklands, Stade Street and Le Quesne will close from 4pm to reduce the risk of further damage.
