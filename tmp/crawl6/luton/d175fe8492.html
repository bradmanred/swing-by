Lewsey Park

Lewsey Park is a large green space in north west Luton offering sports facilities, a swimming pool and gym, play areas, walking paths, natural features such as Lewsey Brook, and a range of outdoor recreation areas for the local community.

Lewsey Park is a large public green space in north west Luton (LU4 0PF). It serves as a key recreational area for nearby communities, including Lewsey Farm and Hockwell Ring, and offers a mix of sports facilities, play areas and natural features.

Lewsey Sports Park and Pool is located within the park and includes a 25 metre swimming pool, flume slide, gym, boxing studio and squash courts. 

The park has a large and popular children’s play area, as well as open green spaces and walking paths suitable for relaxation, exercise and family activities.

Lewsey Brook runs through the park, adding natural interest and supporting local wildlife. The spacious grassy areas and pathways provide plenty of room for walking and enjoying nature.
