St Mary's Park

St Mary's Park lies to the north of Walkden Town Centre and was first opened in 1894.

Among the park's main attractions include; a bowling green, children's play area, veterans' pavilion, five-a-side football pitch and multi-use games area.

Address: St Mary's Road, Walkden
Classification: Neighbourhood Park
Opening times: 24 hour pedestrian access

Facilities:

  • Bowling green

  • Children's play area

  • Five-a-side football pitch

  • Multi-use games area

  • Veterans' pavilion

Friends group: Yes - Friends of St Mary's Park

If you would like to organise an event or activity in this park please view our advice page.
