Graffiti

How to report graffiti, how we aim to remove it, and who is responsible for different types of property

Report graffiti

Use this online form to report graffiti or tagging. Please read the guidance on this page before you start.

Report online

We can remove graffiti and tagging on signs or buildings that we own. We also clean some private property if we have an agreement with the owner. Sometimes, we work with artists to create professional murals.

We handle reports differently based on the type of graffiti and where it is:

  • Offensive graffiti: We try to remove this within one working day.
  • Non-offensive graffiti: We try to remove this within one month, or sooner if we can.

It can take us longer to clean private property. We must first get permission from the landowner.

Permalink

When you report graffiti to us, please provide:

  • The location: We encourage you to use the what3words app. This app gives every 3-metre square in the world a unique three-word address. This helps us find the exact spot.
  • The type of graffiti: This helps us decide how to remove it as quickly as possible.
  • A picture: Please send a photo if you can.
Permalink

If the graffiti is on a private house, report it to us. We will try to talk to the landowner to arrange for them to remove it.

For graffiti on national infrastructure (such as utility boxes, bus stops, or railway bridges), please contact the companies directly:

  • Royal Mail: Call 0345 774 0740 or email postbox.appearance@royalmailpfs.com
  • BT phone boxes: Call 0800 661 610 or email customer.serv.payphones@bt.com
  • Openreach cabinets (Green): Call 0800 023 2023 or use the live chat on their website
