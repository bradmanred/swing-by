Welcome to My Council

If you're new here, here's a few tips to help get you started:

  1. Search for an address in the box above

  2. Once an address is set click on the Show side panel button to add and remove items to any page.

  3. See your property details, bin collection dates and councillors in My House. You can also view schools and planning applications near to you.

  4. Explore what is on your doorstep, including Council service and facilities, with My Maps
