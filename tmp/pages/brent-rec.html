Brent River Park

Brent River Park

Brent River Park is a large green space which is dissected by the River Brent. This natural landscape has recently been improved through the River Brent Project and further plans are proposed for future improvements.

Principally recreational with formal pitch provision to be included as part of the River Brent Project improvement plans.

This community space is suitable for informal ball games and is popular with local dog walkers and nature lovers. A walk along the open space leads to Wembley Stadium.

There is no car park at this park. Pedestrian access is good from all sides of the open space.

Facilities

  • Children's playground or play area
  • Sports hall
  • Wildlife area
