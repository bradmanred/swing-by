Lloyd Park

Location

Beside Coombe Road, Croydon. The tram service for the park stops at Lloyd Park - Route 3.

Area

114 acres 46.13 hectares.

Facilities

Opening times

  • Parkland with mature trees and woodland
  • Lloyd Park Cafe is on Facebook and Instagram
  • Changing rooms/pavilion
  • Car park (off Coombe Road) - 3hr free parking but you must get a ticket from ticket machine
  • Children's playground
  • Outdoor gym
  • Football
  • Cross country running
  • Bowling green - closed
  • Small pond
  • Disc golf
  • Tennis courts - managed by  CASSAC. You can book online on the CASSAC website
  • Recycling facilities for bottles, clothes and small electrical appliances

For use of the football pitches or to book the pavilion please telephone 020 8726 6900.
