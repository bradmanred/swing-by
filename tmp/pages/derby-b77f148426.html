Paddling pool and water play

No notices at this time.

Cool the kids down and enjoy family fun at one of our water play area.

Water play at Chaddesden Park

Make a splash in the water play area at Chaddesden Park. Open weekends and school holidays during the summer season (end of May to early September) weather permitting. Come along with your friends and family and have an amazing time!

Paddling pool at Markeaton Park

Part of the Mundy Play Centre. A free way for children to make a splash in the summer with 3 fountains for extra fun, bring a towel and a picnic and play all day. The paddling pool is open 10:30am – 4:30pm during summer months only and is supervised by trained Council staff.
