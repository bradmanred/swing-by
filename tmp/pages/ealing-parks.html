Parks in the borough

  1. Home
  2. Leisure, parks and libraries

  4. Parks in the borough

The borough of Ealing is home to many parks, including 10 large ones which are the most popular among visitors.

There are also 4 major nature conservation sites, which combine sites to create green corridors and large areas of green space.

Some parks are closed to the public overnight

There is plenty of opportunity to exercise with distance marker routes and outdoor gyms

Green Flag parks

Green Flags are national awards that are given to the best parks and green spaces in the UK. Ealing's parks have been awarded more new Green Flags than any other borough in the UK this year. Find out about the parks that will be flying a green flag
