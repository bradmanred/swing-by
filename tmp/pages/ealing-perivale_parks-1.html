Pear Tree Park

Pear Tree Park is a new park in Perivale. It lies North of Ruislip Road East, and to the West of the Greenford Branch Railway Line. The River Brent runs down its western edge.

Ealing Central Sports Ground

A large park that is used as sports pitches for football and cricket. There is a pavilion, children’s playground and car park and the site has an open stream on the north and west borders. The park also has a library support office and the Perivale Residents Association.

Access: Horsenden Lane South, Scorton Ave, Teighmouth Gardens
Transport: Tube: Perivale (Central Line), Bus: 297, 95
Facilities: Sports pitches and pavilions, playground, car park

Paradise Fields

A wetland area with ponds, reed beds, hedgerows and wildflower meadows adjacent to Horsenden Hill.  Managed for nature conservation, the site has a main lagoon that is good for bird watching from the viewing platform.  The site features Walk 9 of the Capital Ring Route

Access: Greenford Road and from Berkeley Fields across the canal bridge.
Transport: Tube Greenford (Central Line)
Facilities: Car park (area in Westway Cross car park), viewing platform, walks.

Perivale Park and Meadows/Ruislip Road East Conservation Area

Part of Brent River Park this site is devoted to sport with football and cricket pitches, Perivale Park Athletics Track and Gym, and Ealing and Southall Athletics Club. Costons Brook runs through the west side of the park skirting Perivale Meadows before joining the River Brent - walkers can enjoy the river by taking the Ruislip Road riverside walk. Access: Stockdove Way, Lever Gardens, Cowgate Lane and Costons Lane.

Transport: Train: South Greenford (Thames Trains), Bus: 92, 95, E2, E5, E6, E7, E9, E10, 105
Facilities: Bowling green and pavilion, football pitches, cricket pitches, sports changing rooms, playground, golf course, tennis courts, Perivale Athletics Track and Gym (020 8575 5776), Perivale golf course (020 8575 7116), car park
The car park is shared with the golf course and is locked at night. Please check the signage in the car park for locking times.
