Play Areas

We provide and maintain play areas, sports courts and skate parks to encourage children and young people to play outdoors.

Our play areas are inspected on a weekly basis by our trained staff to ensure all sites are safe and meet the required standards. Any problems identified are dealt with in a timely manner. We also have an annual inspection carried out by an independent company to comply with play and safety standards.
