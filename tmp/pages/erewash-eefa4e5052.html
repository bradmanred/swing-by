Directory of Parks

A directory of parks and open spaces in Erewash.

Facilities

  • (-) Parking
  • (-) Tennis Courts

Erewash has lots of parks and open spaces for people of all ages to enjoy. 

These pages include details of park facilities, parking and opening hours. 

Victoria Park, Ilkeston

Victoria Park opened in 1897 and hosts a variety of trees, shrubs, wildflower and lawn areas to walk around and relax in.

West Park, Long Eaton

West Park is the largest park in the Borough of Erewash and has achieved Green Flag status a number of times.
