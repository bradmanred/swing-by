Valentines Park

Valentines Park Cranbrook Road Ilford, IG1 4TG

Valentines Park is our premiere award winning park securing the prestigious Green Flag. The park has a diverse tree collection including a 300 year old field maple, ornamental gardens, flowering meadows and a network of accessible paths for you to enjoy. There are two children’s play areas as well as two different cafes with a great selection of food and beverages. The park also features one of the biggest outdoor gyms in London.
