Ashton Playing Fields

Promoted

Ashton Playing Fields Chigwell Road Woodford Green , IG8 8AA

They have an eight lane track, competition specification hammer, two long jump/triple jump pits and javelin and shot put areas. There are also high jump and pole vault facilities. They offer pay as you go access or an annual membership offering unlimited access to the track. For opening times please check the athletic track availability at the top of the page. They now have a brand new Gym and Track membership option available.

James Leal Centre

Promoted

James Leal Centre Woodford Woodford Green, IG8 7GL

The centre is a state of the art, eco building, which was opened in December 2009. Located next to one of Redbridge’s premier children’s play areas, outdoor gym, tennis and basketball courts, it is also a good place to start or end your walk or cycle along ‘Roding Valley Way.’ The centre also has café, serving a selection of hot and cold fresh foods and beverages. The café is open every day from 10am to 5pm during the Spring and Summer months.

Owls Play Centre

Promoted

Owls Play Centre Fairlop Waters Country Park Forest Road Barkingside , IG6 3HN

Owls Play Centre is a local indoor play and party venue packed to the rafters with fun and adventurous activity for children to the age of 12 years. Fullwell Cross Leisure Centre

Promoted

Fullwell Cross Leisure Centre High Street Barkingside , IG6 2EA

Located on Barkingside High Street, Fullwell Cross is a Leisure Centre for all the family, the centre is fully equipped for all your leisure needs. Mayfield Leisure Centre

Promoted

Kinfauns Road Goodmayes , IG3 9QN

Newly built Mayfield Leisure Centre is the newest facility within Redbridge and is fully equipped with state of the art equipment and is fully accessible.
