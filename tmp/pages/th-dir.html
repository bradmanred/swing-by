Opportunity for catering concessions in parks

Tower Hamlets has many beautiful parks and open spaces used by both residents and visitors to the borough.

We have a great opportunity for local businesses to trade in our parks for the summer season.

We are seeking to license:

- catering vans
- catering operators at fixed pitches
- healthy street food trucks

We have opportunities in parks across the borough for vendors who have Public Liability Insurance and Food Hygiene Certificate.

If you are interested in applying, please email [email protected].

Parks directory

Best in Class

Tower Hamlets, located in East London, is home to some of the city’s most vibrant and diverse green spaces. Among these, Victoria Park stands out as a centerpiece. An award-winning park often referred to as the “People’s Park.”

Spanning over 200 acres, Victoria Park features beautiful lakes, historic monuments, cafés, and open lawns. It regularly earns the prestigious Green Flag Award, celebrating its high environmental standards and community value.

Another highlight is Mile End Park, a modern linear park that stretches through the heart of the borough. It includes a rich mix of natural habitats, play areas and even an ecology pavilion, seamlessly connecting green space with urban life through its striking Green Bridge over Mile End Road.

Stepney Green Park is a more intimate space but no less important. With well-maintained gardens, sports facilities, and community events, it serves as a cherished neighbourhood retreat.

Together, these parks offer a vital green network in Tower Hamlets, balancing historic charm, ecological sustainability, and accessible recreation for residents and visitors alike.
