Playgrounds and Inclusive Play

We have a wide variety of playgrounds in parks and open spaces across the borough.

In our playgrounds you will find high-quality play equipment for children of all ages. Our playgrounds are safe spaces where all children can take part in creative and imaginative play.

Safety inspections are regularly carried out on all play equipment in line with current legislation.

Equipment in our playgrounds is suitable for children aged four and below (toddlers) and five and above (juniors). Equipment at adventure playgrounds is suitable for ages 8 – 15 (25 SEND).

Adults must be accompanied by children in designated play areas.

Adults must not use children’s play equipment for any purpose, including fitness training.

 Find a list below of all our playgrounds and play spaces.

For more information about any of our play spaces contact [email protected] or 020 7364 5227.

Adventure Playgrounds

There are three adventure playgrounds in Tower Hamlets.

An adventure playground is a special type of playground where children can have fun, play freely, and take risks within a safe and inclusive environment. Adventure playgrounds are staffed by trained playworkers.

The role of the playworker is to support children and young people in the creation of a space where they can play. Playworkers work within the play work principles.

All equipment in the Glamis, Weavers and Whitehorse Adventure Playgrounds is accessible to children and young people with additional needs (SEND) aged 8 – 25. Children under 8 must be accompanied by an adult. Children of any age with additional needs must be accompanied by an adult.

Please check the list below for addresses and facilities at our adventure playgrounds. For more information about adventure play contact [email protected] or call 020 7364 5227.

Whitehorse Adventure Playground

Adventure Play sessions take place 3 – 6pm Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday in term time. Booking is not required but spaces are limited. 

To take part in these sessions parents and carers will need to register their chidlren. This can be done using this form before you arrive.

All those in attendance need to follow our promises and the play work principles throughout their visit to the playground.

Inclusive Play

An inclusive playground is a space is where children of all abilities can play. The equipment gives them a rich range of physical, sensory, and social experiences.

Inclusive playgrounds allow children of all abilities to play together and create a nurturing environment for all.

Several of our playgrounds are fully inclusive, with all equipment accessible to children with additional needs. Other playgrounds have some inclusive equipment. This means the equipment is accessible to children and young people with additional needs up to the age of 21. Please see the list below for further information about our inclusive playgrounds.

Multi-use games areas

A Multi Use Games Area (MUGA) is an area with line markings for different sports including football, basketball and netball. Some MUGA's also have basketball rings or goal posts and colourful markings for games like snakes and ladders and hopscotch.
