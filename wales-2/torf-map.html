Play areas

We maintain 47 children’s play areas, multi-use games areas and skate parks across the borough.

There are a total of 69 play areas in Torfaen.

Find your nearest play area (opens in new tab)

Council-maintained parks are shown on the map in pink, Bron Afon-maintained parks are purple, green and orange indicate private or community council-managed.

All parks are wheelchair accessible, inclusive equipment is available at the following play areas:

  • Pontypool Park
  • Cwmbran Boating Lake
  • Brooklands Field Park
  • Pontnewynydd Park

Inspections are carried out regularly, and equipment and fences maintained to meet safety standards.

Please note dogs, including those on leads, are not allowed in any play areas.
