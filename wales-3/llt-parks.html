Parks and Playing Fields

The Town Council has responsibility for the playing fields and parks at Clos yr Ysgol Park, Crown Park, Havelock Park, Morfa Park, Nightingdale Court Park, Penyfan Park, Penygaer and Llanerch Playing fields and Peoples' Park.

The Town Council employs a dedicated Park Keeping Service. The Town Council Park Keepers, Gerwyn Jones and Jack Lynch can be contacted by emailing enquiries@llanellitowncouncil.gov.uk or 01554779999. The Park Keeping service will provide a visible presence across each Llanelli Town Council play area and green space.

 

Facilities available at the Llanelli Town Council parks include:

Clos yr Ysgol - Play Area

Nightingale Court - Play Area

Morfa Park - Play area and skate park

Penyfan Park

  • Play Area
  • Cruyff Court MUGA
  • Splash Pad (Summer period)
  • Exercise bikes

Peoples Park

  • Play Area
  • Paddling Pool (Summer Holiday period)
  • MUGA
  • Skate Park

Havelock Park

  • Play Area
  • Splash Pad (Summer period)

Crown Park

  • Play Area
  • Paddling Pool (Summer Holiday period)

Penygaer Playing Fields

  • Active play area
  • Sensory play area
