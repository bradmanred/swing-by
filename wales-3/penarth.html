Have Your Say on Penarth Play Areas
  • View Larger Image

Residents of Penarth have the opportunity to have their say on proposed improvements to local play areas.

The Vale of Glamorgan Council have launched a consultation into plans for Cliff Top and Cwrt-y-Vil play areas.

You can attend a drop-in session at The Paget Rooms on Wednesday 2nd November 4pm – 6pm or submit your comments online.

If you have any queries, please contact 01446 700 111 or email consultation@valeofglamorgan.gov.uk
