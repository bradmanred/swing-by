Our Play areas

Find out about Council play areas in Calderdale.

Updated: 25th June 2026

Calderdale has over 100 play areas from small local ones to those within some of our larger parks. These have a range of facilities to cater for children of all ages and abilities, such as:

  • Multi-use games areas.
  • Fitness equipment.
  • Water play.
  • Wheeled sports.

To find a play area near you, see: Find my nearest.
